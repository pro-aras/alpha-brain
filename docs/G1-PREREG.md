## 1 · The gene, its source and its proof
`GAZE  surprise fixations 4 grid 16 canvas 128 glance 32 ink contrast`
- **Source, carried verbatim (imported, not ported):** Alpha 2's `organs/retina.py` (sha256 aa95693dcd29f1f5d9432160ac7f5a520db9cbec5719f73112b7b6337fa81dc4) and `organs/gaze.py` (sha256 2ccea9b449efd4452cb3c4fc0a5d4d439e456b18bc365595f3c90c6fe230ae73), V0.1, commit d772bf7 (2026-09-05 11:03), pre-registered in `/data/dev/alpha2/docs/PRE-REGISTERED-a2-v0-vision-program.md`. The genome imports the two files from `/data/dev/alpha2/organs` and refuses to start if their sha256 is not the one written here. **Proof carried:** V0.1's dry run — the surprise gaze covered 12 salient regions per 12 fixations against its random twin's 3–4 (bar 1.5×); and the defect its twin exam caught before it shipped (a periphery prediction updated inside one frame → surprise 0 → a blind crawl from the top-left corner, 1 region vs the twin's 4), fixed as the organ now reads.
- **Mechanics, as the organ defines them:** the canvas (128×128 RGB) becomes the organ's frame dict (`w, h, pix` = the PPM P6 bytes the organ's own reader would produce); `retina.analyse` gives the 16×16 cell grid of mean RGB, the channels (luminance, red–green, blue–yellow, centre–surround contrast, four orientation energies) and the saliency map; `gaze.gaze_sequence(analysis, max_fixations=4, predicted=None)` gives the fixations: the first at the saliency peak, then the most surprising unvisited cell — with no earlier frame the surprise is the residual saliency (the organ's own rule for a first frame), inhibition of return over the grid. **4 fixations, not V0.1's 12:** 12 was per page frame (a web page of many regions); a picture of one thing needs the thing's cell and its neighbours. The number is declared here and not tuned after a number is seen.
- **A glance:** a 32×32 window of the canvas centred on the fixated cell's centre ((y+½)·8, (x+½)·8 px), clamped inside the canvas. **INK contrast:** the window's grey g = (0.299R+0.587G+0.114B)/255; ink = |g − mean(g over the window)| / max over the window (a flat window is all zero). One rule for drawings and photographs alike: ink is wherever the window departs from its own mean; the eye's own steps then follow (thresh0.25, centre, tower, sqrt, unit — the G0b RETINA unchanged). If gate G-a's bands do not separate under this rule on his pictures, the run still reads and the verdict says the sealed glyph tower cannot band his photographs under it — a finding about the estate's eye on photographs, not a reason to swap the front.
- **Glances are moments:** one showing of a picture = 4 moments in fixation order, each with the same word on the ear (HEBB window 1 wires each glance to the word). The STAGE genes stay in MOMENTS, the machinery's clock (STAGE 1 = 200 moments = 50 showings). BUDGET 400 per arrival stands unchanged (the estate's number); glances multiply novel inputs, so refusals are predicted possible on the toy and (iv) is reported there (§3).
- **Answering over glances (RECALL k=1 + ABSTAIN, extended):** a question picture is gazed the same way; each glance lights its resonating winners as in G0b §11 am. 6; the evidence for a word cell = the SUM over glances of the strongest edge from that glance's lit cells to it; best = the largest evidence; second = the strongest competitor by G0b's rule (a word cell whose own answer leads outside the union of the lit sets); abstain NOVEL when no glance lights a cell, NOEDGE when no positive edge exists, RATIO when second/best > 0.85 (the genome's ABSTAIN gene). Q3/Q4's edge = the same sum to the named word cell. **With one glance this is exactly G0b's recall** — pinned before the toy: over G0b's 380 held-out pictures the extended road returns the same cell, reason and edge as the rung-0 road, result for result, and the G0b run replays to hash `cfbc80b3231f8755` with GAZE off.

## 6 · Amendment 1 — the INK rule, stamped after G1-toy's first run and before G1b-toy (edit: Claude (Fable 5.1), 2026-09-19 17:35 +0400)

Gate-only measurements (no organism, no bar; `runs/g1-toy-run.log`'s session, this file's record): the bands on G0b's own road are within-median 0.968 / between-p95 0.902 / no conflated pair; through the canvas road as run they are 0.950 / 0.927 / three pairs. One factor at a time: u8 quantisation, the canvas's second noise, the clutter, a window with 100 % of the thing — none closes the bands; **G0b's road with the declared INK rule applied closes them (0.963 / 0.934 / the same three pairs)**, and the box window with raw ink restores them (0.960 / 0.903 / one pair at the median). **Mechanism:** INK = |g − mean(window)| / max makes a FILLED shape's background as bright as its ink (a filled circle's window mean is ≈ 0.5, so background pixels read 0.5 and the interior 0.5), leaving only the rim — circle, square and diamond become one shape; outline things (ring, cross, star, bar) are untouched, which is why every other thing read 19–20/20. A residual is the fixation window's cut-off (median 3 % of the thing's ink outside the window on fixation 1, 11 % on fixation 4): within-median 0.955 on the fixation window against 0.960 on the box with the same ink — accepted, stated.
**The amendment (a RULE, not a bar):** `GAZE … ink border` — INK = |g − median of the window's 2-pixel BORDER| / max: the background is read from the window's rim, so a filled shape keeps its interior, and a dark thing on a bright ground (his photographs) still becomes ink. Measured at the gate on the real road (fixation window) before any run: within-median 0.955, between-p95 0.906, conflated at the median: circle/square, circle/diamond (the residual above); the plain rule `ink raw` (g / max) reads identically on this world (0.955 / 0.906) but would make a dark thing on a bright ground a hole — `border` is chosen for his pictures' sake and declared here. Gate G-a is rerun on the amended road and writes the thetas afresh; the toy is rerun as **G1b-toy**; G1-toy's first verdict stands on the record as run. Predictions for G1b-toy: (i) Q1 ≥ .80 (the trio separates as in G0b — but the residual cut-off may keep circle/diamond close: uncertain); (vi) the null plant abstains ≥ .5 (a hexagon's interior is no longer a ring's); (v) uncertain — triangle→tree and diamond→house are a context fact (§1b's CONTEXT gene), not an ink fact; (x), (xi) PASS as before.
