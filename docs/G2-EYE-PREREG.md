## 1 · The eye's source and road
- **Trainer:** the estate's numpy conv trainer `experiments/E09c-grounding-tower/code/conv.py` (E09c, 2026-09-13; the tower E09d sealed and E46d grew), MIRRORED in `genome/train_eye.py` layer for layer: im2col convolution (the same index road), float32, ReLU, max-pool, mean-pool, softmax head on the pooled features, momentum 0.9, the same learning-rate schedule (×0.5 after 60 % of the epochs, ×0.5 again after 85 %), the head discarded at the seal. The only changes: three conv layers instead of two, a 64-pixel input instead of 32, and the layer list written into the seal so the genome's eye reads any tower of this family.
- **Seal:** `judge/eye-photo-g1/tower.bin` in E46's export format (`A3EYE1\0\0`, per array int32 ndim, dims, float32 C-order) + `SEAL.json` (sha256 of tower.bin, shapes, the layer list, the labels, the training journal, the gate numbers). `genome/eye.py` refuses a tower whose sha is not its seal's — the same law as the glyph eye.
- **Architecture, declared:** input 64×64 grey in [0, 1] → conv1 16 @ 5×5 pad 2, ReLU → 2×2 max (32×32) → conv2 32 @ 3×3 pad 1, ReLU → 2×2 max (16×16) → conv3 48 @ 3×3 pad 1, ReLU → 4×4 mean → 4×4×48 = **768 features**. Parameters 416 + 4,640 + 13,872 = 18,928 in the trunk; the head 768×92 + 92 is scaffolding. Seed 20260919. Batch 64. lr0 0.02.
- **What the eye sees, in training and in life — the SAME road:** the genome's own: a picture → canvas 128 (longest side, padded with its mean colour) → the gaze's fixations on Alpha 2's saliency (GAZE gene) → a 64×64 window on the fixated cell → INK border (G1 §6) → the tower. No `centre` step (a photograph has no ink centroid worth centring on; declared). RETINA for G2: `tower:eye-photo-g1 sqrt unit`. GAZE for G2: `surprise fixations 4 grid 16 canvas 128 glance 64 ink border`.

## 2 · The training world (held-out families, #158)
Caltech-101's **92 folders not used in rung 1** (8,510 pictures; `/data/datasets/caltech-101/`). The eleven rung-1 words (anchor, ant, barrel, bass, beaver, bonsai, brain, buddha, camera, cannon, ahmad) are NEVER shown to the trainer — they are the held-out families on which the eye is judged. Validation: every 5th picture per folder by filename (the trainer never steps on them). Training views per picture: its 4 gaze fixations' windows (the natural augmentation — the eye learns what the gaze will hand it) and their horizontal mirrors = 8 views; the head's label = the folder. Views are cut ONCE before training (deterministic), cached as uint8.

## 3 · Gates, in order, each before the next (a failed gate stops the rung; it is a finding)
- **G-1 the trainer's toy:** the drawn toy world's circle and bar canvases through the same road; the head must reach 100 % on 40 held-out canvases within 5 epochs, or the trainer is wrong. A gradient check on one batch (finite differences vs the backward on 20 random weights, relative error < 1e-2 in float32) before the toy.
- **G-2 the cost probe, then the epoch rule:** 20 training steps timed on the real views; **epochs = the largest of {8, 12, 16, 20} whose projected time fits 45 minutes** (OPENBLAS threads 8 for TRAINING only — declared; the organism's lives stay single-thread). The rule is written here; the number is filled by the probe, never by the result.
- **G-3 the sanity floor:** validation top-1 on the 92 classes ≥ 0.20 (chance 0.011). Not the mark — a floor under which the eye learned nothing worth sealing.
- **G-4 THE MARK — gate G-a on the eleven UNSEEN words:** first-fixation glances of the rung-1 development pictures through the new eye: the p95 rule MET (within-median > between-p95 + ear-max floor) — and per word, «ahmad» named: his within-median above his between-p95. **Twins on the same gate:** (a) the glyph tower (measured: −0.085); (b) an UNTRAINED tower of the same shape and seed (random weights, sealed the same way) — separation must come from the training, not from the shape's size: the trained eye's margin (within-median − between-p95) must exceed the untrained twin's by ≥ 0.05.
- **G-5 replay:** a second training run from the same seed reproduces the journal to 5 decimals and the tower bytes (E09d's discipline; single-thread BLAS gives bitwise, 8 threads may not — if the bytes differ under 8 threads, the seal is the FIRST run and the journal's per-epoch loss must agree to 3 decimals; stated on the seal).
- **G-6 rung 1 again, G2-his:** `genome/G2-his.genome` (G1-his with SENSE eye 64x64, the new RETINA and GAZE), gate G-a writes its thetas from the new eye's bands, `run_g1 --his --genome` on the same eleven words, bars as G1 §3 in counts, (iv) gating. Nothing sent.
