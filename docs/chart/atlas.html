4 · The theoretical solution

An organism is a genome (rules, not structure) run by fixed machinery, living in a world that answers back (a present where a word meets its picture; questions scored outside it), with a body that always perceives (no scheduler over senses), a mind that can say «I do not know» (a consumer for every dead end), growth that never eats (a budget; prune the wrong and the redundant), and a discipline that measures its own instrument first. Each of the ten failures is the absence of one clause. G0 tests the first seven on the smallest world where the truth is known; the body's threads, the open world and the lineage are rungs on the same substrate, in that order, each behind its own gate.

What would change this: a G0 organism that passes (i) and (ii) but fails (vi) would say the substrate learns and the metacognition gene is wrong, not the design; a G0 that fails (ii) — the shuffled twin reading above chance — would say the association is geometry, not co-occurrence, and the whole «present» clause is false for this world; a random genome in the top quartile (ix) would say E29 again, and the genome's numbers are decoration.
