## 1 · The law, exactly
The genome line: `HEAR     every word; thing last; arrival last`. The parser accepts these three values only. Any other word refuses by name.

With the line ABSENT, every row of every life is what it is today, byte for byte.

With the line present, at an eye row:
1. **W, the words the organism meets**, is the moment's ear rows in tape order, each distinct word once (its first place). Today the organism meets W's last word.
2. **One `Organism.moment` per glance, as today.** The clock `org.t` and every cadence counted in moments do not move.
3. **The words side of that moment** is T = the union, in W's order, of each word's own winners: `RESONATE(words, encode(w))` per word, its novelty birth included, paid from the ONE budget. *(co-signer: births run sight first, then each word in W's order, so the budget is spent in that order. KWTA applies per word. On every closed life a word holds exactly one cell (42 lives, 30,813 word cells, no word with two), so T holds one cell per word of W.)*
4. **HEBB, DELTA, PRUNE and MERGE read (S, T) as they do today.** MISFIRE's test is already a set test and stays: a cell misfires when its strongest edge is not among the other side's winners.
5. **An error birth in the words area** beside a cell holds the vector of the word that lit that cell. If two words ever lit one cell, the earlier word in W is taken and the event is counted (`hear_shared_cell`); §0's fact says the count is 0 on every genome on disk. *(co-signer: the misfiring cell holds that word's own vector, so cos = 1.0 > THETA_M 0.9969 and the birth is always refused as redundant. In the words area this clause births nothing, as today: 0 words-area error births in the three closed journals, beside 10,000 canvas rows carrying `births` and 7 novelty births each. What it prevents: unwidened, the code would birth a cell holding the LAST word's vector beside a colour cell (cos ≤ 0.875 < THETA_M). Every words-area misfire now adds to `births_refused_redundant`; G reads it.)*
6. **THRESH's own band** takes a push when the top sight winner's established answer is ANY cell of T. Today it compares with `T[0]`. *(co-signer: on a one-word moment T is that word's one cell, so «any cell of T» equals «T[0]».)*
7. **`thing last`:** the sight ledger's thing (`moment_word[t]`), the journal's `word`, and the episode's `word` and `x_w` stay the LAST word of W.
8. **`arrival last`:** an arrival opens on a never-heard LAST word, as today. A never-heard word that is not last births its cell from the running budget. `_heard`, `resume` and the blind-ear twin's clock are untouched. *(co-signer: the ARRIVAL line keeps its value «never-heard word»; under HEAR it reads «never-heard LAST word», and `_heard` holds last words only. A word first met not last and later said last opens an arrival then, though it has a cell. In the scene world the 7 colour and size words are never last, so both arms have the same 7 arrivals. A starved word that is not last: `_birth` refuses it (`births_refused_budget` + 1), T lacks it, and the organs' road answers «unheard» for its phrases until a hearing finds a unit left after that moment's sight birth. At stage 1 this cannot happen: the budget is at least 279 at every first hearing (§0).)*
9. **The words ledger is true.** Under the line, a word cell is named by the word its own vector holds. The ear's code is one-hot per letter slot (`genome/world.py:encode_word`), so the vector decodes to exactly one word. `build_ledgers` labels a words-area birth by decoding `org.words.cells[cid]`; merges still union the sets. The sight ledger stays `moment_thing` (`thing last`). Nothing new is stored, saved or copied; `org.births` and `org.c` are untouched. *(co-signer: the seat's 2026-09-29 proposal, replacing «the mind keeps the map cell → word, saved and copied». Checked: (1) `_birth` is the only writer of `area.cells`; `_merge` and `_scale` move W, mask, strikes and alive only; `load` restores the saved float64 array. **Why one moment and a union, and not one moment per word:** MISFIRE and DELTA read a moment's words as a set. Fed one word at a time, the same glance would name «small», then «green», then «circle»; each would strike the cell's answer to the other two, and the clock would run up to three times faster. The union is the smaller change and the one the laws already fit.
