## 1 · The law, exactly
The genome line: `HEAR     every word; thing last; arrival last`. The parser accepts these three values only. Any other word refuses by name.

With the line ABSENT, every row of every life is what it is today, byte for byte.

With the line present, at an eye row:
1. **W, the words the organism meets**, is the moment's ear rows in tape order, each distinct word once (its first place). Today the organism meets W's last word.
2. **One `Organism.moment` per glance, as today.** The clock `org.t` and every cadence counted in moments do not move.
3. **The words side of that moment** is T = the union, in W's order, of each word's own winners: `RESONATE(words, encode(w))` per word, its novelty birth included, paid from the ONE budget.
4. **HEBB, DELTA, PRUNE and MERGE read (S, T) as they do today.** MISFIRE's test is already a set test and stays: a cell misfires when its strongest edge is not among the other side's winners.
5. **An error birth in the words area** beside a cell holds the vector of the word that lit that cell. If two words ever lit one cell, the earlier word in W is taken and the event is counted (`hear_shared_cell`); §0's fact says the count is 0 on every genome on disk.
6. **THRESH's own band** takes a push when the top sight winner's established answer is ANY cell of T. Today it compares with `T[0]`.
7. **`thing last`:** the sight ledger's thing (`moment_word[t]`), the journal's `word`, and the episode's `word` and `x_w` stay the LAST word of W.
8. **`arrival last`:** an arrival opens on a never-heard LAST word, as today. A never-heard word that is not last births its cell from the running budget. `_heard`, `resume` and the blind-ear twin's clock are untouched.
9. **The words ledger is true.** A word cell is named by the word whose vector it holds. The mind keeps the map cell → word. It is saved in the ledger file and the snapshot and copied into the think thread's frozen inputs. It never enters `org.births` or `org.c` (both are hashed).
10. **The journal's canvas row gains `words`** (W) under the line. No other row changes shape.

**Why one moment and a union, and not one moment per word:** MISFIRE and DELTA read a moment's words as a set. Fed one word at a time, the same glance would name «small», then «green», then «circle»; each would strike the cell's answer to the other two, and the clock would run up to three times faster. The union is the smaller change and the one the laws already fit.

**The decision rule.** `HEAR` becomes the law of the next brain lives (the templates BRAIN-LEAN and BRAIN-LEAN-TWIN take the line together) only if I1, I2, A, P, K, K2, G and C all pass at every seed they are read on. If P fails with everything else passing, the cells cannot learn what they now hear: the line is NOT adopted, the cells stay on the last word, and the brain stays the one hearer of every word. If G or C fails, the law is not adopted as written and the failing series goes to the design. G's readings decide nothing here; they price F8 (the sight misfire) and F2 (`arrival last` against a starved word) for stage 2.

**What P does and does not say.** P asks whether the cells learn anything about a size or colour word. It is not a contest with the brain. The brain's marks stand beside it as the ceiling.

## 6 · Controls
- **The twin differs from ON by the word road alone:** same tape, same code, same genome text but one line; ARRIVAL, the budget law, the clock and the sight ledger are the same in both (§1.7, §1.8).
- **A test that fails on today's code** for each built clause: the union (a two-word tape: both words' cells win and wire); the true words ledger (the cell of «red» is named «red»); THRESH's push on a first-word answer; the error birth's vector; the refusal beside a stage-2 gene; the refusal of a bad word that is not last; the journal's `words`.
- **Resume at a cut** after a colour word was heard, then replay: equal journals.
- **The think thread's frozen copy** carries the map (a threaded think is refused at stage 1, so this is pinned by a unit test on `freeze_inputs` alone).
- Every zero in the gate's report stands beside the presence count of the field it was read from.

## 7 · What this does not claim
- It does not make the organism USE its brain. That wiring is its own build.
- It does not say the cells learn words as well as the brain. They bind every glance of a canvas to the words, the distractors' glances too; the brain attributes.
- It says nothing about the full organism until stage 2's gate.
- A pass at stage 1 changes no closed life and no record.
