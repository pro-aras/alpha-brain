## B · The seat's weak points — first the four primitives, the search cost, and whether arm D can be built in a day

### B1 · The four-primitive base (attend · compare · sequence · bind) is a plant in the primitive at the letters stage
**The opposition first (the seat's best case):** atoms may be given — the organs are hand-designed, and his page 3 allows organ features «by hand»; his 11:40 rule forbids hand-writing the MIXTURE, not the atoms; DreamCoder and Lake 2015 both start from primitives; four is small.
**My reply, checked against the code on this box:** at the letters stage the four primitives are not atoms below the tested skills — they ARE the tested skills, or the floor's machinery:
- **compare + attend** over the eye's 144-number traits is nearest-cell recall — `Organism.recall` / `recall_many` (`genome/machinery.py`) — i.e. arm A0, the cells;
- **bind a name to a thing** is MEET — the sight × words edge matrix (`_hebb`, `_delta`) — A0 again;
- **sequence two events** is the relation «after» itself; the teaching order is already STORED (the FAST ring in write order; `words_in_instant` in heard order — `genome/fast.py`), so «after X» with a `sequence` primitive is the RING-READ twin my research added (§5 of RESEARCH-OPUS: the four sequence families measure REACH, not composition).
- **So arm D, with these four, re-implements A0 + RING-READ and adds exactly one thing: SEARCH over compositions of them.** That one thing is worth measuring — but only on the families whose answer is NOT a single primitive: a new cue learned from a few pairs, a chain of two learned cues (the seat's own C7 (b)), cue transfer across families (C7 (a)), perceptual relations. On the sequence families D passes by construction (#175 / #222 (c): a plant whose signal sits in the primitive).
- **My position on «hand design»:** atoms are allowed where the organs deliver them — traits, the tape's order as DATA, the teacher's marks — and forbidden where they coincide with a relation the stage grades. «Sequence» as an operator is the second kind at the letters stage.

### B2 · The search cost — DreamCoder's own numbers (read in its PDF, arXiv 2006.08381)
- **With rich, hand-given primitive sets:** «The time needed to train DreamCoder to the points of convergence … typically takes around a day using moderate compute resources (20-100 CPUs).»
- **From minimal primitives** (1959-Lisp-like, plus the Y combinator): «With enough compute time (roughly five days on 64 CPUs), DreamCoder learns to solve all 20 problems».
- **The authors' own summary:** «Even if learning from scratch is possible in principle, such approaches suffer from a notorious thirst for data–as in neural networks–or, if not data, then massive compute: Just to construct ‘origami’ functional programming, DreamCoder took approximately a year of total CPU time. Instead, … we built in what we know constitutes useful ingredients».
- **Without a library at all:** a 32-call program «would take in excess of 10^72 years of brute-force search to discover» (the figure caption; `pdftotext` prints the exponent flat as «1072»).
- **Against this box:** 16 hardware threads, shared with lives and graders (load 18.35 at 11:32). The seat's §4 gives every arm «≤ 1 CPU-day» — 24 CPU-hours, i.e. **≈ 1–5 % of DreamCoder's typical per-domain budget** (a day × 20–100 CPUs = 480–2,400 CPU-hours) and ≈ 0.3 % of its minimal-primitive budget (5 days × 64 CPUs ≈ 7,700 CPU-hours). A four-primitive D at 24 CPU-hours is either given richer primitives (hand design, B1) or searches a space too small to show anything beyond B1's re-implementation. Stitch (arXiv 2211.16605 [opened]: «3-4 orders of magnitude faster» than DreamCoder's compression) cuts COMPRESSION, not SEARCH — the wake phase's enumeration is the cost that remains.

### B3 · Can arm D be built in a day on this box? — No, not faithfully
- **What a faithful D needs** (DreamCoder's own parts): a typed DSL whose primitives run on 144-number traits and the tape's order; an enumerator with a prior; a compressor (Stitch-shaped); a recognition network trained on «dreamed» tasks; our determinism law on all of it (seeded, ordered; a search with a wall-clock timeout breaks replay identity — the timeout must be a count of candidates, not seconds).
- **What exists:** none of it on this box — checked 2026-09-24 12:24 +0400: no `stitch_core`, `dreamcoder`, `torch`, `jax` or `mlx` module importable, and no DreamCoder or Stitch checkout under `/data` or `/home` (the only «ec» paths found are a git object folder and a shader cache). The estate's program road (INDUCE/DEDUCE) is hand-written rules, not a synthesizer.
- **My estimate [Guessing]:** a minimal faithful D is 3–5 builder-days (Opus), plus its toy (a planted relation it must induce from 3 examples) before any letters run. **A one-day D is a hand-enumerated program space over the relation vocabulary** — B1's plant again. So: D belongs in B1, but not in B1's first week, unless its primitives are restricted to organ-level atoms and its budget is declared (§DEBATE D1).

### B4 · Search is a SLEEP learner, not an online one
- DreamCoder's wake phase searches per task with a time budget (the text-editing run: «within 10 minutes»; competition conditions «1 hour & 8 CPUs per problem» [its PDF]). At the body's pace a per-moment search is impossible; D learns in sleep (batch), from the episodes. That is compatible with complementary learning systems — the library as a slow store trained in sleep — but D is then graded on what it knows AFTER a sleep, and U6 (one encounter, the same day) cannot be D's.

### B5 · Programs over continuous traits need a soft part
- Lake, Salakhutdinov, Tenenbaum (Science 2015) [opened in my research]: characters as programs over STROKES with a generative motor model — primitives richer than «attend» and a hand-built model of drawing. Our organs deliver 144-number traits, not strokes. A program that separates «b» from «d» over traits needs learned thresholds or a learned metric — a soft component. The seat's own design has one (the «small predictive front»); the letters experiment must say which part of each answer came from which (the provenance of U4 again).

## F · The seat's first experiment (§4), reviewed

B1 stays letters-only; «red circle» and «IF bell THEN look left» are the words stage's B3, with the game.
2. **No ceilings, nulls or power are stated.** «IF bell THEN look left» on unseen combinations has how many combinations, and what does the commonest answer score? B1's arithmetic (RESEARCH-OPUS §5) is the pattern.
3. **«Same budget ≤ 1 CPU-day»** is not the same budget for every arm: 24 CPU-hours is generous for a 10^5–10^6-parameter soft net and ≈ 1–5 % of DreamCoder's typical budget (§B2). Equal budgets must be declared, with D's budget sensitivity shown by a 10×-budget twin.
