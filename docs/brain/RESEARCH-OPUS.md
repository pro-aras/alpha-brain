## 0 · The answer in eleven lines
1. **His insight is right — and it is the root of the whole field.** McCulloch and Pitts (1943) described networks of threshold neurons as «a logical calculus»: with suitable weights a unit IS an AND or an OR. Every trained network since — the transformer included — is a LEARNED mixture of such gates.
2. **Modern networks are made of exactly his five words:** a thresholded sum = AND / OR; a multiplicative gate that lets the old state through or the new one = IF / ELSE (the LSTM's «multiplicative gate units learn to open and close access»); a state carried forward = THEN; an attention «induction head» is a learned «IF you saw A then B before, and you see A now, THEN say B».
3. **So «gates or the prediction engine?» is a false choice.** The gates are the SUBSTRATE; prediction error is the TEACHER that sets them. His page 5 says exactly this: understood, not predicted — «but the prediction plays important role in its learning curve».
4. **What is genuinely new in his project is the REGIME, not the gate:** grown by need; fed first by the body's own senses in his order; learning for life (awake online, asleep by replay); speaking from understanding — never trained once on text and frozen.
5. **Two kinds of learned gate, two different records.** SOFT gates (LSTM, GRU, attention) have carried language for a decade — and a model under 10 million parameters writes fluent simple stories when its corpus is simple. HARD, readable gates (learned logic-gate networks, Tsetlin clause machines) have NOT produced language: the best published recurrent logic-gate network reaches 5.00 BLEU where its GRU twin reaches 5.41 and a 2017 transformer 28.4.
6. **So: build the brain on soft learned gates, and run his hard-gate hypothesis as a first-class ARM beside it** — on the letters stage, where it is cheapest to try (§5). If it matches, his method becomes the substrate.
7. **Composition (his AND / OR / IF / THEN «over things it has seen») does not come free to ANY network.** Standard networks reach 96–99 % on familiar combinations and 16–35 % on new ones (COGS). It comes from training on a STREAM of compositional episodes (MLC, Nature 2023) or from built-in structure (a stack machine, rule synthesis). → The letters stage must be a GAME of compositional episodes, graded on relations held out by pair — and on relations whose rule is not simply the order they were taught in (§5).
8. **Continual learning has a biological answer the creature already half-has:** two stores (fast = the cells; slow = a network) and replay between them (complementary learning systems, 1995). The missing half is the slow store.
9. **Grounding-first is our BET, not a known fact.** A generic network learned word–thing links from 61 hours of one child's head-camera (Science 2024) — but it learned links, not speech; and in the 2024 BabyLM challenge no image + text model beat the text-only baselines. The design must measure the bet, not assume it.
10. **Scale is not the barrier:** letters-to-sentences fits in 10^5–10^7 parameters on this CPU; fluent simple text needs ≈ 10^7 parameters and ≈ 10^8 words. His «1b or 2b» is not needed to test the idea.
11. **His 12:01 bar — understanding «from the core», not «predicting next word as a token» — is made into six world-checked tests per word (§4.1).** No single substrate passes all six at the letters stage; the HYBRID (the cells + a soft-gate brain) covers all six by its two roles; a text-only model cannot take three of them (pointing, refusing, one-encounter learning) even in principle — it has no world. Understanding becomes a STATUS PER WORD (understood / only heard-of), and the mouth asserts only with understood words.

## 1 · His idea, exactly — and where it comes from

### 1.1 The historical root
- **McCulloch & Pitts, «A logical calculus of the ideas immanent in nervous activity», Bulletin of Mathematical Biophysics 5(4):115–133, December 1943** [metadata only: Crossref DOI 10.1007/BF02478259]. The paper's title is the claim: nets of all-or-none threshold neurons compute logic. [not fetched, from memory: the paper shows such nets realise propositional expressions; one threshold unit is an AND or an OR by its threshold.]
- [not fetched, from memory — the standard history:] Rosenblatt's perceptron (1958) learned one unit's weights; Minsky & Papert (1969) proved one unit cannot compute XOR; multi-layer networks trained by backpropagation (Rumelhart, Hinton & Williams 1986) learn the gates of hidden units, XOR included.
- **On this box:** E20's positive control — a learned clause machine solving plain XOR at 100.0000 % — is the same fact in the estate's own hands [from disk: `/data/dev/alpha3/experiments/E20-tsetlin-machine/VERDICT.md`].
- **Consequence:** «a learned mixture of logic gates» is not a new kind of machine. It is the DEFINITION of a neural network. What is open is (a) which gates (soft or hard), (b) learned how (a global gradient or a local rule), (c) grown or fixed, (d) fed what (a body's senses or a text corpus), and (e) for how long (once, or for life).

### 1.2 His five words, in the operations of networks that already exist
| his word | what it is inside a trained network | the source |
|---|---|---|
| **AND** | a unit that fires only when several earlier features fire together; the circuits reading of a vision network found a «car» neuron that fires when window detectors fire at the top of the image, wheel detectors at the bottom and a car-body detector in the middle | Olah, Cammarata, Schubert, Goh, Petrov, Carter, «Zoom In: An Introduction to Circuits», Distill 2020 [secondary: search — the page exceeds the fetch limit] |
| **OR** | a unit whose threshold is low enough that any one of its inputs suffices; OR-pooling in logic-gate convolutions | Petersen et al. NeurIPS 2024 (logical OR pooling) [fetched: abstract, arXiv 2411.04732] |
| **IF / ELSE** | a multiplicative gate that lets one thing through or another: «Multiplicative gate units learn to open and close access to the constant error flow» (the LSTM); a «trainable gating network» that decides which of thousands of expert sub-networks run on this input (mixture of experts) | Hochreiter & Schmidhuber, Neural Computation 9:1735–1780, 1997 [fetched: abstract, Europe PMC 9377276]; Shazeer et al., arXiv 1701.06538, 2017 [fetched: abstract] |
| **THEN** | a state carried to the next moment (recurrence); an attention «induction head» that completes «[A][B] … [A] → [B]» — a learned «IF you saw A followed by B, and you see A now, THEN predict B» | Olsson et al., «In-context Learning and Induction Heads», arXiv 2209.11895, 2022 [fetched: abstract] |
| **IF / THEN as a program** | readable programs (select, aggregate, compare) compile into transformer weights: RASP writes programs for histograms, sorting and Dyck languages as attention and feed-forward steps; Tracr compiles such programs into real decoder-only transformers | Weiss, Goldberg, Yahav, «Thinking Like Transformers», ICML 2021 [fetched: abstract, arXiv 2106.06981]; Lindner et al., «Tracr», NeurIPS 2023 [fetched: abstract, arXiv 2301.05062] |

**The reading that follows:** the transformer is already a learned mixture of soft gates, and reverse-engineering it finds his five words inside it. **The question that discriminates his project is therefore not «gates or prediction», but (1) the REGIME the gates are grown and taught in, and (2) SOFT gates (continuous, differentiable) versus HARD gates (discrete, readable AND / OR / XOR).**

## 2 · The survey — for each: what it demonstrably does · at what scale · what it has NOT shown · what it means for the letters-first stage

### 2.1 HARD learned gates — his idea taken literally (discrete logic whose wiring is learned)

**(a) Differentiable logic gate networks.** Petersen, Borgelt, Kuehne, Deussen, «Deep Differentiable Logic Gate Networks», NeurIPS 2022 [fetched: abstract, arXiv 2210.08277]: networks of logic gates («AND» and «XOR» among them) where each node LEARNS which gate to be, through «a continuously parameterized relaxation» trained by gradient descent, then discretized; «beyond a million images of MNIST per second on a single CPU core». Petersen, Kuehne, Borgelt, Welzel, Ermon, «Convolutional Differentiable Logic Gate Networks», NeurIPS 2024 oral [fetched: abstract, arXiv 2411.04732]: logic-gate tree convolutions, OR pooling, residual initialisations; **CIFAR-10 86.29 % with 61 million logic gates, «29x smaller» than the prior state of the art.**
- *Not shown:* language (neither paper). The gates are learned by the SAME engine as any network — backpropagation through a relaxation; only the inference is discrete.
- *For letters:* a logic-gate classifier over the eye's (binarised) glance traits is a cheap, fast, readable arm — a letter as a Boolean circuit over traits.

**(b) Recurrent logic-gate networks — the only hard-gate network that has touched language.** Bührer, Plesner, Aczel, Wattenhofer, «Recurrent Deep Differentiable Logic Gate Networks», arXiv 2508.06097, August 2025 [fetched: abstract] (a workshop paper at the ACM Edge and Mobile Foundation Models workshop [secondary: search]): the first application of differentiable logic gates to sequence modelling; on WMT'14 English–German translation, **«5.00 BLEU and 30.9% accuracy during training, approaching GRU performance (5.41 BLEU)» and 4.39 BLEU at discrete inference.**
- *Read against the task:* the Transformer reached **28.4 BLEU** on the same WMT 2014 English–German task in 2017 (Vaswani et al., «Attention Is All You Need», arXiv 1706.03762 [fetched: abstract]). **At 5 BLEU neither the logic-gate network nor its GRU twin translates.** «Approaching the GRU» is a like-for-like result at toy scale, not language.
- *Not shown:* fluent generation; online learning; grounding.
- *For letters:* this is the hard-gate THEN arm — the one published recurrent hard-gate design.

**(c) Three-valued recurrent logic gates.** Damera, Matheu, Puranic, Baras, Belta, arXiv 2605.24649, May 2026 [fetched: abstract]: gates over Kleene's three-valued logic {−1, 0, +1}, **0 = «unknown»**, trained through polynomial surrogates and discretised; predicts temporal-logic verdicts online, with «principled abstention»; a bound sizes the hidden state.
- *Not shown:* language.
- *For letters:* his «I do not know» built into the gate itself — worth carrying into the ANSWER law as a design idea (abstention as a third logic value), not as an arm.

**(d) The Tsetlin machine — clauses learned by a LOCAL rule, in integers.** Granmo, arXiv 1804.01508 (v1 2018 → v15 2021) [fetched: abstract]: propositional clauses (ANDs of input literals, voting like an OR) learned by teams of Tsetlin automata — a reward / penalty rule that is local and bitwise; «competitive accuracy compared with SVMs, Decision Trees, Random Forests, Naive Bayes Classifier, Logistic Regression, and Neural Networks» on the paper's tasks. **On language:** next-character prediction inside a partly given word (Rahimi Gorji, Granmo, Goodwin, «Natural Language Modeling with the Tsetlin Machine», IEA/AIE 2023, LNCS) [secondary: search — the chapter is behind a login; no number read]; a Tsetlin autoencoder that learns logical word embeddings self-supervised, «outperforming GLoVe on six classification tasks» (Bhattarai, Granmo, Jiao, Yadav, Sharma, arXiv 2301.00709 [fetched: abstract]; EACL Findings 2024 [secondary: search]); parallel FPGA training of such embeddings, word-similarity Spearman / Kendall 0.669–0.696 (Kadhim, Jiao, Shafik, Granmo, Shende, arXiv 2605.06982, May 2026 [fetched: abstract]).
- **On this box — E20 (2026-09-13)** [from disk: E20 VERDICT]: plain XOR 100.0000 %; noisy XOR 100 % up to 20 % label noise, 98.98 % at 30 %, 74.72 % at 40 %; on 62 glyph classes from 22 ordinal shape features, **48.21 % against a ridge's 53.42 % (5.21 pp behind; 2.01 pp of it the binary encoding)**; clause stability 0.738 against a random-clause twin's 0.051; every dumped clause readable after a lossless reduction to 11–22 terms. **Alive as an explainer, behind as a classifier.**
- *Not shown:* producing sentences; composition over sequences; how it forgets.
- *For letters:* the Tsetlin arm is the one whose learning is LOCAL and whose arithmetic is INTEGER — bit-exact on any machine (design Q2) and readable (his AND / OR on the page). Its known weakness is accuracy against a linear read-out on shape features.

**(e) Logic RULES learned from data.** Evans & Grefenstette, «Learning Explanatory Rules from Noisy Data» (∂ILP), arXiv 1711.04574 [fetched: abstract]: differentiable inductive logic programming with «robustness to noise and error in the training data» that classical ILP lacks. Dong, Mao, Lin, Wang, Li, Zhou, «Neural Logic Machines», ICLR 2019 [fetched: abstract, arXiv 1904.11694]: «perfect generalization» on family-tree and graph reasoning, sorting, shortest paths and blocks world. Riegel et al., «Logical Neural Networks» (IBM), arXiv 2006.13155, 2020 [fetched: abstract]: neurons as parts of weighted-logic formulas, «omnidirectional» inference, a loss that measures logical contradiction, the open-world assumption.
- *Not shown by any of the three:* learning language from a sensory stream. They reason over predicates someone has already named.
- *For letters:* IF / THEN rules over already-named things CAN be learned exactly and generalise perfectly — that is the letters stage's relation layer once the names exist. They do not name the letters.

### 2.2 SOFT learned gates — the ones that already carry language

**(a) Gated recurrent networks produced language before the transformer, and again now.** The LSTM (Hochreiter & Schmidhuber 1997) [fetched: abstract]: learned multiplicative gates; bridges «minimal time lags in excess of 1000 discrete-time steps»; «local in space and time; its computational complexity per time step and weight is O(1)». The GRU (Cho et al., arXiv 1406.1078, 2014) [fetched: abstract — the reset / update gate equations are in the body, not read]. **LSTM language models at scale:** on the One Billion Word benchmark, perplexity 51.3 → 30.0 with 20× fewer parameters, an ensemble 23.7 (Jozefowicz, Vinyals, Schuster, Shazeer, Wu, arXiv 1602.02410, 2016) [fetched: abstract]. **minGRU / minLSTM:** simplified gates, fewer parameters, «fully parallelizable during training», «rivalling recent models including Transformers» (Feng, Tung, Ahmed, Bengio, Hajimirsadeghi, arXiv 2410.01201, 2024) [fetched: abstract].
- *Shown:* a network of learned SOFT gates produces language.
- *Not shown:* that such a network learns language from a grounded stream, online, without forgetting — every result above was trained offline on text.
- *For letters:* the soft-gate arm is on firm ground; a recurrent state is also the WORKING MEMORY today's mind lacks (MIND-THINKING-LANGUAGE §0.4 item 4 [from disk]).

**(b) Attention, read as circuits.** Elhage et al., «A Mathematical Framework for Transformer Circuits», Anthropic, 22 December 2021 [fetched: page, through the tool's summary]: attention heads as independent read / write operations on a shared «residual stream», each split into a query–key and an output–value circuit; zero-layer models do bigrams, one-layer models add skip-trigrams, two-layer models compose heads into induction heads; «we've simply had much less success in understanding MLP layers so far». Olsson et al. 2022 [fetched: abstract]: induction heads appear exactly when in-context learning jumps during training — causal evidence in small attention-only models, correlational in larger ones. Ramsauer et al., «Hopfield Networks is All You Need», arXiv 2008.02217 [fetched: abstract]: «The new update rule is equivalent to the attention mechanism used in transformers»; the new Hopfield network stores «exponentially (with the dimension of the associative space) many patterns». **Attention is an associative memory — a learned, content-addressed lookup — which is what the creature's cells are in hand-written form.**
- *For letters:* two attention layers suffice for «THEN over things seen» (after A comes B; A for Apple). A small transformer is the MARKET's engine; it belongs beside the design as a check, not in it.

**(c) Kolmogorov–Arnold networks.** Liu et al., arXiv 2404.19756; ICLR 2025 [fetched: abstract]: learnable one-variable functions on the edges instead of fixed activations; smaller KANs match larger MLPs in data fitting and PDE solving; readable for science. *Not shown:* language. *For letters:* not a gate road — no arm.

**(d) Sparse mixture of experts — a learned IF at the size of whole sub-networks.** Shazeer, Mirhoseini, Maziarz, Davis, Le, Hinton, Dean, arXiv 1701.06538, 2017 [fetched: abstract]: «a trainable gating network» picks a few of thousands of experts per example; models «up to 137 billion parameters»; «greater than 1000x improvements in model capacity with only minor losses in computational efficiency». *For letters:* one expert per family (letters, digits, symbols) chosen by a learned gate is one way to meet the family-context law of #225 — a later rung, not the first experiment.

### 2.3 Structure that GROWS

**(a) The oldest precedent for «a unit is born where error persists, and old units are kept».** Fahlman & Lebiere, «The Cascade-Correlation Learning Architecture», NIPS 2 (the 1989 proceedings, published 1990) [secondary: search + the proceedings listing]: begins minimal, adds hidden units one by one, each trained to correlate with the remaining error, then FROZEN as a permanent feature detector. Fahlman, «The Recurrent Cascade-Correlation Architecture», NIPS 3, 1990 [fetched: abstract]: the recurrent version — «rapid learning», «automatic near-minimal network construction» and «incremental training». (The LSTM paper lists recurrent cascade-correlation among the methods LSTM outperformed on long-time-lag tasks [fetched: abstract].) These are E48's laws (#227) three decades earlier.

**(b) Growth for continual tasks.** Rusu et al., «Progressive Neural Networks», arXiv 1606.04671 [fetched: abstract]: a new column per task, lateral connections to frozen old columns — no forgetting by construction, transfer by reuse (Atari, 3-D mazes). Yoon, Yang, Lee, Hwang, «Lifelong Learning with Dynamically Expandable Networks», arXiv 1708.01547 [fetched: abstract]: selective retraining + expansion when a task arrives; «comparable to batch-trained models while using substantially fewer parameters». Chen, Goodfellow, Shlens, «Net2Net», ICLR 2016 [fetched: abstract, arXiv 1511.05641]: function-preserving widening and deepening — a grown network starts exactly where the smaller one left off.
- **On this box:** E48 — growth bought CAPACITY (a new script cleared its mark where a fixed twin did not) but not retention (+0.56 pp over a fixed twin of the same size on Latin; −0.70 pp against random growth); the sequential gradient head kept 0 of 120 letters where the local growing rule kept 58–66 %. E48b — a budget that refuses and no disuse decay kept the letters within ±1.7 pp [from disk: E48, E48b VERDICT].
- *For letters:* birth on persistent error under a budget + function-preserving insertion is buildable now; the check is a FIXED twin at the grown network's final size.

**(c) «Gates that grow» at the level of programs — library learning.** Ellis, Wong, Nye, Sablé-Meyer, Cary, Morales, Hewitt, Solar-Lezama, Tenenbaum, «DreamCoder», arXiv 2006.08381 (PLDI 2021 [not fetched]) [fetched: abstract]: a wake–sleep loop grows a library of abstractions — its own programming language — and trains a neural guide on «imagined problems»; it rediscovered basics of functional programming, vector algebra, Newton's and Coulomb's laws. **Letters, specifically:** Lake, Salakhutdinov, Tenenbaum, «Human-level concept learning through probabilistic program induction», Science 350:1332–1338, 2015 [fetched: abstract, Europe PMC 26659050]: handwritten characters from the world's alphabets represented «as simple programs»; **human-level one-shot classification**, beating the deep learning of the time.
- *Not shown:* learning from a raw stream WITHOUT hand-given primitives (strokes; a starting language).
- *For letters:* one-shot letter learning has a human-level precedent — through composition from parts. A sleep that grows reusable pieces (DreamCoder's shape) is his page 10 «actively dream … and predict results» in software.

### 2.4 Composition — his AND / OR / IF / THEN «over things it has seen»

**(a) Networks fail it by default.** Lake & Baroni, «Generalization without systematicity», ICML 2018 (SCAN) [fetched: abstract, arXiv 1711.00350]: sequence-to-sequence RNNs generalise when test commands differ little, but «when generalization requires systematic compositional skills … RNNs fail spectacularly». Kim & Linzen, COGS, EMNLP 2020 [fetched: abstract, arXiv 2010.05465]: **Transformers and LSTMs score 96–99 % in distribution and 16–35 % on the generalisation set, ±6–8 % across seeds.** This is the challenge Fodor & Pylyshyn (1988) raised against neural networks [named in Lake & Baroni 2023's abstract; the 1988 paper not fetched].

**(b) What passed it.** NeSS — a neural trace generator + a symbolic stack machine — **100 % on SCAN** and three other domains without trace supervision (Chen, Liang, Yu, Song, Zhou, NeurIPS 2020 [fetched: abstract, arXiv 2008.06662]). Rule synthesis — inducing the explicit rule system from a few examples — beats neural meta-learning on SCAN and on number words in many languages (Nye, Solar-Lezama, Tenenbaum, Lake, NeurIPS 2020 [fetched: abstract, arXiv 2003.05562]). LeAR — a homomorphism between a latent syntactic algebra and a semantic algebra (Liu et al., Findings of ACL 2021 [fetched: abstract, arXiv 2107.06516; no number in the abstract]). **MLC — a STANDARD network «optimized for its compositional skills» through «a dynamic stream of compositional tasks» reaches human-like systematicity, where probabilistic symbolic models are systematic but rigid and plain networks flexible but unsystematic** (Lake & Baroni, Nature 623:115–121, 2023 [fetched: abstract, Europe PMC 37880371]). Least-to-most prompting of a large pretrained model sets the CFQ state of the art with 1 % of the training data (Drozdov et al., arXiv 2209.15003 [fetched: abstract]) — a borrowed model, outside the pure road, cited only as evidence that DECOMPOSITION helps. Neural Module Networks assemble a network per question from reusable modules through a parser (Andreas, Rohrbach, Darrell, Klein, arXiv 1511.02799 [fetched: abstract]). The Neuro-Symbolic Concept Learner learns visual concepts, words and semantic parsing from images with question–answer pairs, executes programs on an object-based scene, and generalises to new compositions (Mao, Gan, Kohli, Tenenbaum, Wu, ICLR 2019 oral [fetched: abstract, arXiv 1904.12584]) — with a hand-given program language and a curriculum; no number in its abstract.

**(c) What it means for us.** Composition is either BUILT (a stack machine, a program language, modules) or TRAINED FOR (a stream of compositional episodes). It never simply appears. So:
1. the letters stage must be a **GAME of compositional episodes** — his own page 5–6: «It shall be placed in an initializing game. The game should have mechanisms for initial learning of Alphabets … The tasks, small experiments, goals, etc in the game» [photo; transcription];
2. «it thinks» must be graded on **RELATIONS HELD OUT BY PAIR** — a relation seen on some pairs, tested on pairs never asked;
3. a rule-inducing arm over named letters (∂ILP / NLM shape) is a legitimate check beside the gate arms.

### 2.5 The learning signal — prediction, local or global — and what language in the brain is

**(a) Predictive coding, and local rules.** Rao & Ballard, Nature Neuroscience 2:79–87, 1999 [fetched: abstract, Europe PMC 10195184]: «feedback connections from a higher- to a lower-order visual cortical area carry predictions of lower-level neural activities, whereas the feedforward connections carry the residual errors». **This is his page 6, «The brain and organs shall communicate on predictions of brain».** Predictive coding «converges asymptotically (and in practice rapidly) to exact backprop gradients on arbitrary computation graphs using only local learning rules» — CNNs, RNNs and LSTMs included (Millidge, Tschantz, Buckley, arXiv 2006.04182 [fetched: abstract]) — but predictive-coding networks «suffer significant performance degradation beyond five to seven layers» (Qi, Forasassi, Lukasiewicz, Salvatori, arXiv 2506.23800, 2025 [fetched: abstract]; their fixes reach backprop-level ResNets; the benchmark behind it: Pinchetti et al., arXiv 2407.01163 [fetched: abstract]). e-prop: local eligibility traces × top-down learning signals, strictly online, «approaches the performance of backpropagation through time» (Bellec et al., Nature Communications 2020 [fetched: page, PMC7367848]). Forward-Forward: two forward passes and a per-layer «goodness» — preliminary (Hinton, arXiv 2212.13345 [fetched: abstract]).
- *For letters:* a SHALLOW first brain is exactly where local rules are known to work. **A local-only arm is a real contender, not a courtesy.**

**(b) Is language in the human brain prediction? Both bodies of evidence, side by side.**
- *For prediction:* models' fit to brain responses to sentences is «strongly correlated with model accuracy on the next-word prediction task (but not other language tasks)» (Schrimpf et al., PNAS 2021 [fetched: abstract, Europe PMC]); while listening the brain shows «continuous next-word prediction before word onset» and post-onset surprise (Goldstein et al., Nature Neuroscience 2022 [fetched: abstract, Europe PMC]); the brain predicts a HIERARCHY of representations over several timescales, frontoparietal cortex the longer-range ones (Caucheteux, Gramfort, King, Nature Human Behaviour 2023, 304 participants [fetched: abstract, Europe PMC]).
- *Against language as thought:* «language is a tool for communication … language does not appear to be a prerequisite for complex thought, including symbolic thought» (Fedorenko, Piantadosi, Gibson, Nature 630:575–586, 2024 [fetched: abstract, Europe PMC]).
- **They do not contradict each other:** COMPREHENSION runs on prediction, and THOUGHT is not language. That is his page 5 in neuroscience's words — prediction in the learning and the listening; understanding beneath the words. **The open question — does a mouth driven by thought differ from a mouth driven by next-word prediction? — is an experiment (design Q1's four tests), not a citation.**

**(c) The language of thought.** Fodor, «The Language of Thought», 1975 [not fetched — a book]; learning as program-writing, «The Child as Hacker» (Rule, Tenenbaum, Piantadosi, Trends in Cognitive Sciences 24(11), 2020, DOI 10.1016/j.tics.2020.07.005 [secondary: search]); «The Computational Origin of Representation» (Piantadosi, Minds and Machines 31:1–58, 2021 [metadata only]). *Its bearing:* if thought is compositional symbol manipulation, the gates that matter are the ones that BIND, ROUTE and RECURSE — which both soft and hard gates can do, and which must be trained for (§2.4).

### 2.6 Continual — keeping the old while learning the new
- **Complementary learning systems:** «memories are first stored via synaptic changes in the hippocampal system … these changes support reinstatement of recent memories in the neocortex … neocortical synapses change a little on each reinstatement» (McClelland, McNaughton, O'Reilly, Psychological Review 102:419–457, 1995 [fetched: abstract, Europe PMC 7624455]); updated by Kumaran, Hassabis, McClelland, TICS 2016 [metadata only — the abstract is restricted].
- Slowing learning on the weights that matter to old tasks: EWC (Kirkpatrick et al., arXiv 1612.00796 [fetched: abstract]). Replaying the network's OWN hidden representations, generated by context-modulated feedback, reaches the state of the art on class-incremental CIFAR-100 without storing data (van de Ven, Siegelmann, Tolias, Nature Communications 2020 [fetched: abstract, Europe PMC]).
- **On this box, said plainly:** forgetting has been measured only in LABELLED heads with classes arriving in sequence — E48's sequential gradient head kept 0 of 120 letters; E46b's rehearsal curve read 0.00 → 0.08 → 0.34 → 0.51 → 0.57 over 0 → 1,550 → 3,100 → 12,400 → 31,000 rehearsed rows, confounded by a label conflict (#225) [from disk]. **A self-supervised predictive brain's forgetting has never been measured here.**
- *For letters:* the creature already IS a two-store system in shape (the cells = the fast store; sleep replay). The missing half is a slow store that learns from the replay. The letters → digits arrival is where its forgetting is first measured.

### 2.7 Grounding, and how much data language needs

**(a) The nearest existing thing to his design.** Vong, Wang, Orhan, Lake, «Grounded language acquisition through the eyes and ears of a single child», Science 383:504–511, 2024 [fetched: abstract, Europe PMC]: head-camera recordings of one child from 6 to 25 months, **61 hours of correlated visual–linguistic data**, «a relatively generic neural network»; it «acquires many word-referent mappings», «enables zero-shot generalization to new visual referents», and aligns its visual and linguistic concept systems. **It learned LINKS between words and things — it does not speak.**
- Agents in simulated 3-D rooms relate words to percepts and to action sequences, and «the speed with which this agent learns new words increases as its semantic knowledge grows» (Hermann et al., arXiv 1706.06551, 2017 [fetched: abstract]).
- Systematic generalisation in a situated agent grows with the number of object / word experiences, the agent's own (egocentric) frame of reference, and the variety of its visual input (Hill, Lampinen, Schneider, Clark, Botvinick, McClelland, Santoro, arXiv 1910.00571 [fetched: abstract]).
- BabyAI — the creature's own game — was built because «current deep learning methods are not yet sufficiently sample efficient when it comes to learning a language with compositional properties» (Chevalier-Boisvert et al., ICLR 2019 [fetched: abstract, arXiv 1810.08272]).
- The creature today: MEET names seen things (life 2's exam: Q1 .956 on held-out renderings) and abstains on the never-shown (Q5: 180 of 180 `novel`) — grounded LINKS, like Vong et al.'s; no speech [from disk: MIND-THINKING-LANGUAGE §1 (e) vi].

**(b) How much text language needs — and two counter-facts to our bet.**
- «Children can acquire language from less than 100 million words of input. Large language models … typically require 3 or 4 orders of magnitude more data» (Warstadt et al., Findings of the BabyLM Challenge, arXiv 2504.08165; CoNLL BabyLM 2023 [fetched: abstract]); tracks of 10 M and 100 M words; the winners used LTG-BERT; **«Curriculum learning attempts, which accounted for a large number of submissions, were largely unsuccessful, though some showed modest improvements.»**
- The second challenge: 31 submissions; a hybrid causal–masked model best on text; **«No submissions outperformed the baselines in the multimodal track»** (Hu et al., arXiv 2412.05149, 2024 [fetched: abstract]).
- TinyStories: models «below 10 million total parameters», or with «only one transformer block», write «fluent and consistent stories with several paragraphs … almost perfect grammar» — trained on stories that use only words 3- to 4-year-olds know, **GENERATED by GPT-3.5 and GPT-4** (Eldan & Li, arXiv 2305.07759, 2023 [fetched: abstract]).
- **What this means for our design:** (i) SIZE is not the barrier — under 10 M parameters speaks simple English given the right corpus (design Q7); (ii) **two facts against our central bet** — curriculum learning mostly did NOT help data efficiency, and no image + text model beat text-only baselines in 2024. **Grounding-first making language cheaper is our BET, to be measured, never assumed** — it belongs in the design's «what would show it wrong»; (iii) TinyStories' corpus was written by large language models — whether such a corpus may enter THE SCHOOL as a teacher (Bench D-9 allows outside models as teachers and judges) or is a borrowed mouth (forbidden by the pure road) is a question for the seat and for him (design Q13), not decided here.

**(c) Geometry (his pages 4 and 6).** Word vectors from co-occurrence: «less than a day to learn high quality word vectors from a 1.6 billion words data set» (Mikolov, Chen, Corrado, Dean, arXiv 1301.3781, 2013 [fetched: abstract]); the analogy regularities of his page 4 (king − man + woman ≈ queen) are the subject of «Linguistic Regularities in Continuous Space Word Representations» (Mikolov, Yih, Zweig, NAACL 2013, pp. 746–751 [metadata only — the example itself is from memory, unverified]). Contrastive cross-modal geometry is how Vong et al.'s child model grounds words [fetched]. **Hyperbolic geometry** (his page 6 «Hyperbolic tokenizing»): Poincaré embeddings «outperform Euclidean embeddings significantly on data with latent hierarchies» (Nickel & Kiela, arXiv 1705.08039 [fetched: abstract]; on WordNet, Poincaré at d = 5 MAP 0.823 against Euclidean at d = 200 MAP 0.168, the paper's translational baseline 0.565 [from disk: MIND-THINKING-LANGUAGE §1 (e) v, fetched there]); hyperbolic GRUs are on par with or better than Euclidean ones on textual entailment (Ganea, Bécigneul, Hofmann, arXiv 1805.09112 [fetched: abstract]). *For letters:* the estate decided hyperbolic geometry is not needed while no two words share a cell [from disk]; the letters have no hierarchy to encode. **Re-take the decision at the words stage**, when kinds and parts appear.

## 4 · MY CONCLUSION — and what would change my mind

**In six lines:**
1. **His insight is correct, and it is the field's own foundation** (McCulloch & Pitts 1943): networks of threshold units ARE logic, and every trained network — the transformer included — is a learned mixture of soft AND / OR / IF-ELSE / THEN gates (induction heads are learned IF-THEN; the car neuron is a learned AND). «Gates or the prediction engine» is a false choice: **the gates are the substrate, prediction error the teacher** — his own page 5.
2. **What is genuinely new is the REGIME:** gates GROWN by need under a budget; fed FIRST by the body's own senses in his order; learning FOR LIFE (awake online, asleep by replay); SPEAKING from understanding — never trained once on text and frozen.
3. **Hard, readable learned gates are unproven for language; soft ones are proven.** So the brain is built on soft learned gates, and **his hard-gate hypothesis runs as a first-class ARM beside it**, first on letters, where it is cheapest; if it matches, his method becomes the substrate of the next stage.
4. **Composition does not come free to any network** (COGS: 16–35 % on new combinations); it comes from training on a stream of compositional episodes or from structure. The letters stage is therefore a GAME of compositional episodes, graded on relations held out by pair whose rule is NOT the teaching order itself (§5).
5. **Scale is not the barrier** (10^5–10^7 parameters for letters-to-sentences, on this CPU); **grounding-first is our BET, not a known fact** (image + text did not beat text-only in BabyLM 2024), and the design must measure it.
6. **What makes it different from every text model is measured, not claimed:** the six tests of §4.1, word by word — and the design's candidate is the HYBRID, because it is the only form in the table that can pass all six at the letters stage.


**What would change my mind — each an outcome of §5:**
- **the HARD-gate arm matches the soft arm** on relations held out by pair, retention and cost at both seeds → his method becomes the substrate for the words stage (readable, integer, local);
- **the cells alone, or a hand-written read of their ordered store, match the brains on the DISCRIMINATING relation families** (§5) → either the test leaked (the canaries decide) or the stage is too easy for learned gates to matter (#228 (b)) — and the game is made harder before any brain is built on it. (On the four SEQUENCE families a hand-written ring read is EXPECTED to pass: they measure reach, not thought.)
- **the LOCAL-only arm matches the backprop arm** → sleep's exact gradients are not needed; the brain learns by local rules alone (the more human-like road);
- **the FIXED twin matches the grown arm** → growth bought nothing at letters; growth is kept only for capacity at later arrivals (E48's finding again).
- **a single arm passes all six tests alone** → the hybrid is unnecessary; the simpler learner is carried;
- **the hybrid fails a test its two roles predict it passes** → the roles are drawn wrong, and the design's spine is re-drawn before any body build.

### 4.1 · HIS BAR OF 12:01 — «understanding something from the core and meaning, not just predicting next word as a token»: the six tests, per substrate, at the letters stage
**His words (verbatim, 12:01 +04, relayed by the seat):** «What I'm most anxious about is that what will make it different from others. You or any other ai doesn't understand what it is speaking or doesn't understand the words or language it knows from the core. So that i want it to be different at. Speaking of understanding something from the core and meaning. Not just predicting next word as a token.»

**The seat's operational reading, adopted here in full:** the brain predicts the WORLD — what it will see, hear, feel if it looks, acts, or is told — and words are handles on those predictions. A word is UNDERSTOOD when **(1)** it points to or acts on the thing; **(2)** it refuses the word when the thing is absent; **(3)** it composes grounded parts on unseen combinations (AND, IF … THEN); **(4)** it explains from its own experience, not from text; **(5)** it rejects misuse («a red sound»); **(6)** it learns a new word from one grounded encounter and uses it elsewhere the same day. Each is checkable against its world.

**Two sharpenings — neither softens the reading:**
- **(a) Understanding is a STATUS PER WORD**, computed from the six tests on the world's own items: UNDERSTOOD, or only HEARD-OF (met as an element of a line read or a sentence heard, never anchored). The mouth ASSERTS only with understood words; a heard-of word may appear only as a quotation with its source («I read that …» — his page 11, «never trust any source»). The library phase then adds heard-of words and patterns, never meaning; a heard-of word becomes understood only when a grounded encounter anchors it (test (6)'s road). **This is the difference he asked for, made countable word by word at every stage: a text-only model has no world, so every word it knows is at best heard-of.**
- **(b) «Never the next word», made exact — and raised as the debate's first point, not decided.** The teacher's speech is part of the world. To learn «Apple is red» as a SENTENCE the order of heard words must be learned, so the brain may predict a heard word — but only FROM the world it is heard in (the thing in view, the act just done, the teacher's mark), never from the previous words alone; and the mouth never samples from a word distribution. If «never the next word» is meant to forbid even that, the sentence stage has no road to word order.

- **A finding against this design, written before any run (the advisor's catch, confirmed in `genome/fast.py`):** the four SEQUENCE families — successor, predecessor, two-after, between — reduce to index arithmetic (i ± 1, i + 2) on the teaching order, and that order is already STORED: the FAST episode ring keeps episodes in write order by id, and `words_in_instant` keeps the heard order. A hand-written read of the ring passes all four with no learned gate at all. **So they measure REACH — whether an arm can get at its own ordered memory — not whether its gates compose.** They stay in B1 (an arm with no road to its ordered memory fails them, and that is a real finding about the cells), but they are **never read as «it thinks»**, and a **RING-READ twin** (the hand-written i ± 1 / i + 2 read of the store, no learning) is their null.
- **The families that DISCRIMINATE substrates — U3 is graded on these:** (1) a **NEW CUE learned from a few pairs** and applied to held-out pairs — e.g. «two after» introduced as its own cue once «after» is known: the cue's MEANING is the learned thing, and no hand-written road can learn a cue; (2) the **other case** — the NAME as the bridge between two shapes; (3) **PERCEPTUAL relations and categories whose rule lives in the percept**, testable on held-out LETTERS — «looks the same in a mirror» (A H I M O T U V W X Y: 11 of 26 upper-case; the commonest answer scores 0.577, so the test set is balanced; italic fonts break symmetry, so the organ probe sets the ceiling), «has a closed loop»; (4) a category with NO rule in the percept (vowel) — across FONTS only.
- **Their power** (binomial, computed 2026-09-24): a new-cue family over upper- and lower-case gives 24–48 held-out pairs → 6 of 24 (0.250) or 8 of 48 (0.167) right needed for P < 0.001 at a 1/25 null — adequate; the other case, both directions, ≈ 26 held-out items at a 1/26 null — adequate; perceptual categories are graded per held-out LETTER (renderings of one letter are not independent), so their n is the number of held-out letters: a balanced 22 needs 19 right (0.864), a balanced 16 needs 15 (0.938) for P < 0.001 — **underpowered with 26 letters alone**; they gain power as digits and symbols arrive (stages 2–3). The alpha of each bar is declared in the PREREG.

**The six tests of understanding — his 12:01 bar, graded per arm, each against its own twin (§4.1's table is the pre-registered prediction for every cell):**
- **U1 · POINTS** = (i) + (iii) above, both directions.
- **U2 · REFUSES** — never-taught signs (invented glyphs, and another script's glyphs whose shapes no Latin letter shares — the organ probe of stage −1 picks them) get a bare, sure name ≤ 1 in 10 and «I do not know» (or «it resembles X») ≥ 8 in 10 (A4L-ANSWER A1's form); lures («is this A?» on a B) answered «no» ≥ the bar, the echo rate printed.

- **U4 · EXPLAINS** — every «why» answer cites episodes that EXIST on the arm's own tape and CARRY that letter; the cited-and-true share ≥ the bar against a SHUFFLED-CITATION twin (the same number of citations drawn at random). An arm with no provenance road reads «no road» — a capability, never a FAIL.
- **U5 · REJECTS MISUSE** — misuse items (after Z; before A; the other case of a digit once digits arrive; a relation asked of a never-taught sign): the refusal share ≥ the bar, against a FORCED-ANSWER twin (the same arm with its abstention removed — the misuse it accepts is the failure the twin shows).
- **U6 · ONE ENCOUNTER** — a planted NEW sign (an invented glyph with an invented name, «zog»-style) shown ONCE, in one font, at a declared point, placed where every arm's machinery can learn it (no budget refusal — #228 (a)); then within a declared count of events: named on 10 held-out renderings (≥ the bar), and refused on 10 unrelated never-taught signs (a name given ≤ 1 in 10).
- **The grade is per ARM and per TEST, never a blend (#170):** an arm «understands at the letters stage» only if it passes all six; the table's «CANNOT in principle» cells are read as «no road» and are themselves a finding — a PASS there would mean the instrument leaked, and the canaries of Bench §3.1 decide.

The single-substrate arms A1–A4 are the hybrids' ablations and the table's predictions: they are read, never carried.
