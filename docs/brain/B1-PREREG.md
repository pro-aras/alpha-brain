### 2.3 Names
- A letter's NAME is its lower-case letter as the ear's word (`"a"` for both A and a) — case is not in the name; the digits' names are `zero one two three four five six seven eight nine` (the ear takes letters a–z only; `genome/world.py:encode_word`).

### 2.4 The glyph → canvas road (the same for stage −1, every arm, every twin)
- The crop is scaled by **one factor per (font, size): s = 32 / h_line** (h_line = the crop's line-box height, asserted common to the font at that size), width by the same s, **centred horizontally in a 32 × 32 box** (vertical = the line box, so the baseline and the case sizes are kept); resampling by PIL's BOX filter, as `render` does. A line-box height that varies within a font, or a crop wider than tall, is RAISED (none today).
- The box (grey ÷ 255) takes the place of `render(thing, rng, THING_SIDE)` in **`genome/world.py:render_canvas`'s body, every other line kept in order**: the top-left `rng.integers(0, CANVAS − THING_SIDE + 1, size=2)`, the three faint strokes, noise σ 0.05, the clip, the round to uint8, grey → RGB by repetition, and the truth; its constants (`CANVAS` 128, `THING_SIDE` 32, `GRID` 16, `CLUTTER_STROKES` 3, `CLUTTER_INK` 0.30) IMPORTED, never re-typed; one `numpy.random.Generator` per rendering, seeded from `(seed, item_id)`.

### 3.3 Clause 3 — the organ ceilings (what the eye allows, before any learner)
- **The features an oracle sees — two, because the order of the located glances follows surprise and changes from canvas to canvas:** F_cat(canvas) = the gaze's feature vectors concatenated in their order, unit-normalised; **F_pool(canvas) = the creature's own BIND pooling — `creature/mind.py:_bind_pool` applied to the gaze's `feats` (their mean, unit-normalised), order-free.** Every θ, cosine filter and identity test below uses **F_pool**.
- **Two oracles, with the key** (the Bench's `docs/bench/DIALOGUE.md` (b) and R3-16: a ceiling is declared, then MEASURED at 3 and at 4 exemplars, and the measurement wins): (a) 1-NN — the label of the nearest TEACH exemplar by cosine of F, ties by label order; (b) a linear read-out — multinomial logistic regression with an L2 penalty of 1.0, fitted on the TEACH exemplars by `scipy.optimize.minimize(method="L-BFGS-B")` in float64 from a zero start, maxiter 500, under `/usr/bin/python3` (no sklearn on the box; deterministic by construction). **The organ ceiling of a family = the highest of the four readings (two oracles × two features)** — a ceiling read low makes every bar low, the false-positive direction — at 4 TEACH fonts and at 3.
- **The null:** the linear oracle refitted with labels permuted among the TEACH exemplars (20 seeded permutations; mean and p95 printed).
- **Naming:** per family (upper / lower / digits), graded under the family's own answer set; **label conflicts** — any pair (a, b) the oracle confuses a → b in ≥ 20 % of the test renderings — are named and kept (the bar sits under a ceiling that includes them).
- **Relations:** an item's ceiling = the oracle's naming accuracy on its operand(s) × (for picks) its accuracy in identifying the right candidate; the family's ceiling = the mean over items. **Other-case pairs identical through the organ** (their two forms' F cosine above the between-class p95 of the TEACH letters) are REMOVED before counting n (expected among c/C, o/O, s/S, v/V, w/W, x/X, z/Z) and k is recomputed.
- **Perceptual categories** (mirror, loop): the linear oracle fitted on the TAUGHT letter-forms' category labels, tested on the held-out letter-forms of §5.2's balanced construction (per form, majority over its 3 renderings); vowel: fitted on TEACH-font renderings of all 52 forms, tested on §5.2's TEST-font items.
- **Never-taught signs** (U2) and **the plant** (U6): candidates = base-32 glyphs of Hebrew, Arabic, Devanagari and Greek (the library's own renderings; Cyrillic is OUT — the tower trained on it). A candidate is kept only if its max F cosine to every TEACH exemplar of every taught letter and digit is below θ = the between-class p95 of the TEACH letters' own F cosines (Greek capitals that ARE Latin letters fall out here, by the organ, not by hand — #225). From the kept pool, seeded and disjoint: 40 for U2, 10 for U6's refusal check, 10 for U5's «after a never-taught sign», and 1 plant drawn only from signs with ≥ 11 fonts (base-32 fonts per script: Hebrew 20, Arabic 12, Greek ≈ 60, Devanagari 4 — Devanagari cannot give the plant 10 test fonts); the plant's TEACH rendering is in one of its fonts, its 10 test renderings in 10 others. A pool under 61 shrinks the four counts in proportion (k recomputed) and is RAISED if any falls under its NOT GRADABLE line. The plant's ceiling = the 1-NN oracle with ONE exemplar, on its 10 held-out renderings.
- **The probe's own controls:** a planted DISTINGUISHABLE family (two classes: a filled 32-px square vs a thin ring, through the same road) must read ≥ 0.99; a planted IDENTICAL family (one glyph given two labels) must read chance ± its binomial band. Either failing → the ceilings are void and nothing proceeds.
- **Output:** `docs/brain/b1/stage-1/ceilings.json` — per family: `n` (after removals), `organ_ceiling {nn, linear, max}` at 4 and 3 fonts, `null {mean, p95}`, `chance`, `k_min` (§5.2's rule), `excluded` (pairs or signs, each with its cosine), `controls {distinguishable, identical}`, the input shas.

### 4.1 · Memory Attention's twins (added 2026-09-24 16:05 +0400 on the seat's word, from Ahmad's 15:59 find; the paper read by the designer from its own HTML today)
- **The source:** Jiale Kang, «Memory Attention», arXiv 2609.28399 (submitted 2026-09-23). Its method in its own words: «Memory Attention replaces the value projection with **V = K + Norm(M)**, where M is retrieved by token ID» — M = Norm(E[s]) (its eq. 7: E ∈ ℝ^(N × d_v) per layer, N the vocabulary, s the input tokens' ids; «Norm applies RMSNorm independently to each token vector within each key/value head»), Q = XW_Q, K = XW_K, V = K + M (its eq. 8). **Its confound, in its own words:** «Because MA includes additional memory parameters, they do not isolate the contribution of its structure from the increase in parameter capacity» (its MHA-small model: Standard 373 M, Memory 1,135 M parameters). No matched-parameter control exists in the paper; B1 supplies it.
- **A1 has no attention to modify** — it is a gated recurrent network (§4). So «A1's attention with token-indexed values» is TWO changes from A1 (an attention read-out, and memory values in it), and comparing A1-MA to A1 alone could not say which one helped. Hence a PAIR:
  - **A1-ATT** — A1 + ONE causal attention read-out over its own last 256 steps: a single head, d = 64, Q = W_Q h_t, K = W_K h_τ, **V = W_V h_τ** (the paper's eq. 1, the standard value); the read-out concatenated into the ANSWER head's input.
  - **A1-MA** — A1-ATT with the value projection REPLACED as the paper does: **V = K + RMSNorm(E[s_τ])**, E one row of 64 per TOKEN id, RMSNorm with a learned gain (one head). **The token ids:** the 27 letter-steps, the 24 cue-mark slots, the event kinds, and — for a glance step — its glance POSITION 1–5: a glance is 144 continuous numbers and the organ gives it no token id, so the table cannot index WHAT was seen, only where in the canvas the glance sat. Said now: this is the gap the cells fill in §4.2's interface IF-1.
  - **Matched parameters:** both twins at FIXED width, no births; each one's H = the largest H whose TOTAL parameter count ≤ A1's FINAL count on the same seed (both counts printed; the difference is under one hidden unit's parameters). **A1-FIXED** (the same count, no attention) is their floor. At letters the table is tiny (≈ 64 rows × 64 = 4,096 numbers), so B1 tests the STRUCTURE nearly free of the paper's confound; at the words stage, where the vocabulary grows, the confound returns and the match is kept there too.
  - **Optimiser:** A1's (SGD, momentum 0.9) for everything, E included — the sparse-row question belongs to B2 (§ B2-PREREG 4.5).
- **The claim A1-MA is held to (the seat's words):** A1-MA ≥ A1 on U3 and ≤ A1 on cost, at both seeds — «else it is a parameter increase wearing a new name». **The isolation (the paper's missing control):** A1-MA − A1-ATT on each U3 family ≥ the declared margin at both seeds → the MEMORY VALUE carries the gain; inside the margin → any gain is attention's, not the memory's. Both are graded on all six tests like every arm; A1-FIXED printed beside them.
- **What it decides:** if the claim AND the isolation hold at both seeds, the soft network carries memory values into the words stage (B3), and B2 gains its conditional eighth arm (B2-PREREG §4.5). Otherwise the finding is recorded and nothing enters the body.

### 4.2 · The cells ↔ network INTERFACE — the first candidate and its alternatives (B1's SECOND WAVE)
- **What the co-signed design has (IF-0, the floor):** the parts exchange ANSWERS (the routers of A5 / F) and EXPERIENCE (the network's sleep replays the cells' episodes) — no representation crosses between them.
- **The first candidate (IF-1, MEMORY VALUES FROM THE CELLS — after Kang 2026, the seat's note `docs/brain/NOTES-MEMORY-ATTENTION.md`):** A5's network gains A1-MA's read-out, but its table IS THE CELLS: for a glance step, s = the id of the SIGHT cell the cells' recall lights for that glance (the cells TOKENIZE the percept — what A1-MA's own table cannot do); for a heard-name step, s = the name's WORD cell; the row = that cell's own vector, projected to 64 by the network's matrix P (learned): **V = K + RMSNorm(P · c_s)**. The keys come from the network's context (order, the moment), the values from the cells' content — «the cells answer content, the network answers order», made concrete. **The network's gradient never writes a cell** (one writer per store: the cells keep their own laws); a cell born after the fact is a new row at once — growth without touching the old.
- **The alternatives, each ONE difference from A5 (IF-0):**
  - **IF-2 CELL-ROW INPUT** — the lit cell's row (P · c_s) appended to the network's input at each glance step: the cells as the tokenizer, no attention.
  - **IF-3 ANSWER MIX** — the cells' recall (their score per name) mixed into the network's ANSWER logits by a learned gate per cue (the cache-model family — named as a mechanism only; not opened today).
  - **IF-4 THE NETWORK'S OWN TABLE** — A1-MA inside A5 (token ids from the steps, a glance = its position; no cell in the table): separates «memory values» from «the CELLS as memory values».
- **When:** after the first wave's VERDICT, on the SAME tapes, seeds, bars and graders — no new world and no new bar; arms **A5-IF1 … A5-IF4**, graded on all six tests.
- **The decision:** the interface with the highest U3 over the three discriminating families (TWO_AFTER · OTHER_CASE · CHAIN) at both seeds, ≥ IF-0 + the declared margin, becomes F's interface in the body (F is re-read with it if D1 kept the library); none clears the margin → F keeps IF-0. **Prediction (the designer):** IF-1 wins OTHER_CASE and CHAIN (the name's cell carries the bridge between two shapes), ties IF-0 on REACH, and IF-4 ≈ A1-MA.
- **The seam test (by the third hand, #235):** a planted change to ONE cell's row must change the network's value vector at exactly the steps whose s is that cell, and nowhere else; a planted change to a cell no step lights must change nothing.

## 5 · The tests
### 5.1 The scoring law
- **Training asks** (phases L4–L6) are answered with `answer(row, learn=True)` and never graded; `fit_event` receives every non-test row.
- **The FIRST answer is the grade;** every test item is asked once per rendering, on a FROZEN arm (`answer(..., learn=False)`; `state_sha` identical before and after each sitting — else the sitting is INSTRUMENT-VOID).
- **Answers:** NAME · PICK · YES · NO · UNKNOWN · RESEMBLES(name) · NOTHING · SILENT (the arm returned nothing). SILENT is never read as UNKNOWN.
- **The unit of a relation family is its NAME item** — a held-out name pair or triple; for OTHER_CASE a held-out letter — «known» iff answered right on a strict majority (≥ 4) of its 6 renderings: 3 in each case (for OTHER_CASE and CHAIN, 3 in each direction), each in a test font of the operand's case drawn by the seed. **The two cases (or directions) of one name item are repeated measures of ONE relation fact** — an arm that knows after(b) = c, and an arm that guesses by name, answer B and b alike — so they are never two items (#168's denominator, applied to cases as to renderings); the per-case split is printed as a breakdown, never a second bar. Families whose taught thing is a property of each FORM (mirror, loop) or whose test is perception across FONTS (vowel, naming, POINT, the lures) keep their own units, named in §5.2 and §5.3.
- **Per family, never blended (#170);** every mark prints n, the denominator, the null, the ceiling, the bar and k.

### 5.5 The positive controls — run on every arm, first; a failing control VOIDS the arm, never «fails» it

- **(0b) THE TOY** — each arm's learning machinery on its own toy, outside the tape, solved before the corpus (E20's rule): plain XOR (4 patterns through the arm's own input road) at 100 % for A0–A4; for D, a planted relation its atoms can express, induced from 3 examples and exact on 10 held-out.
- **(0c) KEY / SHIFTED-KEY** — the harness's KEY arm (answers from the truth) scores 1.0 on every grader; SHIFTED-KEY (item i answered with item i + 1's truth) must not — else the graders are INSTRUMENT-VOID.

- **(0e) FROZEN** — `state_sha` before = after every sitting.
