## For Ahmad — five lines

1. The network is not stupid and it is not blind: its memory keeps the letters apart (a plain linear reader on the network's own state names them at 96–97 % — F11, my probe (b)). It fails because of the last step: the piece that turns its state into an answer reads a state that is almost the same for every letter (the letter is 0.8 % of it), so it learns one answer for everything. That one piece is the whole failure, and it costs nothing to fix: the same rule on a centred state goes from 4 % to 84 % in one pass (F13).
2. B1.1 proves the collapse is not the schedule: with the CONST block moved, every network still gives ONE name to all 704 test asks (seed 1 «l», seed 2 «n») — the answer follows the state the ask starts from, not the letter (`read_b11.out`).
3. The thing that already learns like a child here — 3 pairs, then 20 of 21 right — is the rule library D. And the cheapest namer in the whole record is a running class-average over the eye's own numbers (87 % taught / 64 % on unseen fonts, without learning anything — F1). The brain should be built on those two: averages with a learned distance for WHAT a thing is, programs for HOW things relate, and the network kept only for what neither can do — order, context, prediction, speech.
4. Curiosity is a number the brain can compute: how fast its errors fall on each kind of thing (learning progress). It should pick the next lesson where that number is highest, and ask the teacher when its two best guesses are too close. That is your explore → teach → correct → test, made automatic.
5. One overnight experiment on B1.1's own tape settles the next step: the same network with a centred readout, then with a class-average reader, then the reader alone on the eye's numbers with no network — each one change from the last, with the old A1 as the control that must still fail.

## Q3 · Curiosity

- **Learning progress, the signal.** Oudeyer, Kaplan, Hafner 2007 (IAC): «an intrinsic motivation system which pushes a robot towards situations in which it maximizes its learning progress. This drive makes the robot focus on situations which are neither too predictable nor too unpredictable» (abstract); the interestingness of a situation is «the difference between the expected mean error rate of the predictions of M in the close future, and the mean error rate in the close past … the local derivative of the error rate curve of M» (§III.B); the space is split into regions, each with its own expert and its own error list, and the robot «chooses the one for which it evaluates, it will lead to a situation with maximal expected learning progress» (§IV.A) [fetched: PDF]. Schmidhuber 2008 states the same principle as compression: «This drive maximizes interestingness, the first derivative of subjective beauty or compressibility, that is, the steepness of the learning curve» [fetched: abstract]. The developmental fact behind it: 7–8-month-old infants look away most from sequences that are highly predictable or highly surprising and attend longest at intermediate complexity — «a U-shaped relationship», the Goldilocks effect (Kidd, Piantadosi, Aslin 2012; 42 and 30 infants) [fetched: article].
- **What it is here, concretely.** The prototype reader gives, for every family (a letter, a font, a cue, a relation), its own error series — right/wrong per showing, the margin between the two nearest means, the Mahalanobis distance of the new showing. Learning progress per family = the slope of that error over the last k showings (Oudeyer's «regions» are our families and cues; his experts are our per-family readers and D's per-cue programs). Information gain, in the same currency: the expected fall in the reader's ambiguity (the entropy of the softmax over prototypes, Snell's eq. 2) if one more showing of that family were given.
- **Asking the teacher.** When the margin between the two nearest prototypes is under the band, the brain asks («is this a or d?») instead of guessing — the same abstention road A0 has today, made productive. The recorded confidence BEFORE the check (CONCLUSION §9 addition 2) is exactly the reader's margin; the surprise |outcome − confidence| scales the update.
- **His flow and THE SCHOOL.** EXPLORE = the mind picks, among the things the world offers, the family with the highest expected learning progress (never the mastered, never the noise — IAC's two exclusions); TEACH = a TEACH row moves a mean (one showing is one update — no gradient, no lr); CORRECT = a «No» moves the mean of the wrongly-given name away and the right one toward, and updates the shared metric; TEST = held-out fonts, which the reader has never seen, graded by THE SCHOOL's key. «Harder each cycle» is then not a schedule but the frontier: the family whose progress is highest today is the lesson tomorrow, and a family whose progress has fallen to zero while its error is still high is the one to ASK about. Calibration (KNOWS HOW SURE IT IS) is graded on the margin's reliability curve — a reader whose margin is not a probability is a finding.
- **What the networks could not do.** A curiosity signal needs an error that MOVES with learning. A1's naming error stayed at chance for 7,932 rows on both seeds (`read_b11.out`: thirds 1–3 all 2–6 %): its learning-progress signal on naming would have read zero everywhere — «not curious enough» is the same defect as «cannot name», seen from the drive's side.

## Q4 · Growth from the genome

- **The organs' law, applied to the brain.** The cells are born on novelty under a regenerating budget; the network's units are born on persistent prediction error with Dohare's initialisation (incoming random, outgoing zero — «Initializing the outgoing weights as zero ensures that the newly added hidden units do not affect the already learned function», the record's fetch of PMC11338828, DEBATE D2); the library grows by compression. The BRAIN clause in the genome then declares THREE growths, each on its own error: (i) READER: a new prototype is born when a NEW NAME is heard (its mean = the showing; iCaRL's exemplar mean at n = 1), and a new prototype is born WITHIN a name when a showing's distance to its own name's mean exceeds the between-name p95 while the teacher confirms the name (a second font of the same letter is a second mean under one name — a mixture, so «c» in cmr10 and in DejaVu need not share one centre); prototypes MERGE at sleep when their means fall within the within-name band (CYCLE's law, on means); (ii) WORLD: A1's births, unchanged, on the world head's error — but with the buffers grown in chunks, because the 1,024-wide pre-allocation is 5.4 × of the arm's whole cost (`probe_cost_hmax.out`); (iii) LIBRARY: D's compression. All three under one regenerating budget per arrival (#233: the remaining and the refusals on the record as a series).
- **The precedent for «grow where error persists, keep what was built»:** Cascade-Correlation «begins with a minimal network, then automatically trains and adds new hidden units one by one … Once added, its input-side weights are frozen. This unit then becomes a permanent feature-detector in the network»; the paper names the trap the awake rule lives in — «the moving target problem: because all of the weights in the network are changing at once, each hidden unit sees a constantly changing environment» (Fahlman & Lebiere 1990) [fetched: abstract]. A prototype reader has no moving target: its means are independent (Hayes & Kanan, §3).
- **Sleep.** For the reader: recompute the shared covariance and its inverse (4–15 ms), merge the means that collapsed, re-derive the novelty band; for the world model: A1's replay, with the ANSWER heads' corrections replayed too (today only the world head is replayed — F5) — a change that A1-NO-SLEEP = A1 says is harmless to omit today and that becomes worth measuring only once the heads can read; for the library: D's search. A SLEEP before every sitting (R39's rule) so every learner's mechanism can fire.
- **Staying able to learn (B2).** The reader's plasticity does not decay: a new name is a new mean, always (no lr, no saturation) — B2's FIXED-HOT and NO-REPLAY controls will read NOT GRADABLE on it, by design, and that is the finding; the network under B2 keeps its ladder, now with a standardised readout so that a plasticity loss, when it shows, is the trunk's and not the head's.
- **Retirement (CONCLUSION §3 law 5).** A cell retires when the slow store predicts it — with the reader in place, «predicts it» is measurable: the cell's glance lands within its name's prototype band. Never for disuse (#227).

MLC (a standard network reaching human-like systematicity from a stream of compositional tasks) and COGS's 16–35 % for standard training (the record's fetches, RESEARCH-OPUS §2.4) stand as the two ends of what a network can do.
- **His AND / OR / IF / THEN, where they live.** Over NAMED things they are D's atoms and combinators (`eq`, `and`, `or`, `not`, `nthN`, `index_of`, `compose`) — readable, executable, checkable (U4b): «IF after(x) = y AND other_case(y) = z THEN …» is CHAIN, and D composes it without ever being shown it chained. Over UNNAMED percepts they are the prototype reader's geometry (AND = the intersection of two bands; OR = either band; a contrast is a distance) and, for binding two things into one («red» with «apple»), the cells' associative store or a hyperdimensional binding (Kleyko et al.). A network alone for composition is the unproven road here; MLC says it is possible with the right stream, at a cost in examples the DEBATE's own prediction put at ≥ 3 × D's.
- **Where the network belongs.** Where the rule library composes and the cells remember, the network keeps three jobs neither can do: (1) ORDER and CONTEXT — a working memory over the last moments (what came before this ask; the sequence of a sentence heard); (2) PREDICTION of the world's next moment for the learning-progress drive and for dreaming (his page 10); (3) the MOUTH's order road. It is the substrate for THEN. It must earn each job by a mark — U3 for order once its readout can read (Q7's P7), the game's act-outcome prediction for (2), the words stage's read-back for (3). Until it does, it is a measured passenger, exactly as D was before B1 — and D earned its place.
- **R-THINK (F8)** is the right kind of test for (1) — a recurrent thinker stepping a latent until it settles — and it read INCONCLUSIVE on two seeds; its chains and circuits are the D-and-network seam of the words stage, not of letters.

## Q6 · From letter names to words and sentences («Apple is Red»)

- **The road, held to the six marks.** A WORD is a prototype over an organ's traits: «apple» a mean over the eye's shape block, «red» a mean over the colour block (the 32 colour dimensions the gaze already delivers, zero on grey canvases — B1-PREREG §2.5), later «bell» a mean over the ear's traits. A SENTENCE is a program over prototypes: «Apple is Red» = `has(x, colour=red)` with x the thing in view — induced by D from a few shown examples, exactly as TWO_AFTER was induced from 3 pairs. The six marks then have their graders: (1) POINT — pick the red apple among four; (2) REFUSE — «is this red?» on a green apple → the colour prototype's distance says no; (3) COMPOSE — «blue apple», a combination never shown, is the AND of two bands (D's `and`), graded on unseen combinations; (4) EXPLAIN — 4a cites the showings behind the two means (the cells' episodes), 4b prints the program; (5) REJECT MISUSE — «a red sound» asks a colour prototype of an organ that has no colour block: no road, refused by construction; (6) ONE MEETING — a new colour word is a new mean from one showing, used on the next canvas. Word order is learned from the teacher's speech as D learns a cue's meaning: from the order of heard names in the world's own sentences, never from text alone.
- **The literature's frame.** Harnad 1990: «Symbolic representations must be grounded bottom-up in nonsymbolic representations of two kinds: (1) 'iconic representations,' which are analogs of the proximal sensory projections of distal objects and events, and (2) 'categorical representations,' which are learned and innate feature-detectors that pick out the invariant features of object and event categories from their sensory projections» [fetched: abstract] — the cells are (1), the prototypes are (2), the programs are the symbol strings that rest on them. Bisk et al. 2020: «Meaning does not arise from the statistical distribution of words, but from their use by people to communicate»; the five World Scopes — WS1 Corpus, WS2 Internet («most of current NLP»), WS3 Perception, WS4 Embodiment, WS5 Social [fetched: PDF] — place every text model at WS2 and this brain at WS3–WS4 from its first word. Vong et al. 2024 (61 hours of one child's head-camera; «acquires many word–referent mappings») is the nearest measured precedent, and it learned LINKS, not speech [the record's fetch].
- **Never «predicting the next token».** The mouth speaks a program: it chooses a grounded relation and renders it through learned forms, reads it back through its own comprehension road (CONCLUSION §9 addition 1), and speaks only with understood words. A heard word may be predicted from the world it is heard in (the thing in view, the act just done) — the DEBATE's law — but the prediction is a prototype's expectation, never a word-distribution sampled.
- **What B3 (words) needs from B1.2 first:** a namer that generalises across fonts and a refusal that works on unseen things — the reader gives both by construction; D gives relations; the network's state is there for word ORDER, the first mark it can earn.

## Q7 · ONE pre-registered experiment — B1.2 «THE READER» (the readout ladder and the organ floor)

**The question.** Is the network's naming failure the readout — fixable at no cost — and does the network's state add anything to naming over the organ's own features read by a prototype reader? Both on B1.1's tape and harness (`/data/dev/brain-runs/b1.1`, seeds 20261001 and 20261002, the frozen `brain.protocol.Arm`), with no new world and no new bar.

**The arms — a ladder, each ONE difference from its neighbour, built as config switches on `ArmA1` exactly as R46 built A1-TEACH, plus one small harness arm:**
1. **A1** — the negative control: B1.1's own record, re-graded in the same `results.json` (no re-run needed; the tape is one). It MUST reproduce F2's chance and the one-answer T_L, or the instrument is void.
2. **A1-STD** — A1 with every ANSWER head (NAME, YES/NO, PICK) reading the state standardised by its running mean and standard deviation (Welford, float64, updated on the states the heads see at `learn=True` asks and at TEACH rows; ε = 1e-6). Nothing else changes: the same trunk, the same awake and sleep rules, the same lr, the same zero-init rows. **The statistics are LEARNED STATE:** they enter `state_sha`, are persisted by `snapshot`/`load`, and are FROZEN under `answer(learn=False)` (control 0e; the harness asserts it at every sitting) — a running statistic that moved at a test ask would VOID the sitting, so the reader updates only on the `fit_event`/`learn=True` roads.
3. **A1-SLDA** — A1-STD with the NAME head's RULE replaced: a streaming LDA reader (one running mean per heard name; one shared covariance, updated by Hayes & Kanan's eq. (4)–(5); the precision Λ = [(1 − ε)Σ + εI]⁻¹ with ε = 1e-4, solved at every SLEEP and held between sleeps; the answer = argmax_k (Λμ_k)·h − ½ μ_k·Λμ_k; the confidence = the softmax margin). TEACH and FEEDBACK rows update the means (a «No» adds the showing to the RIGHT name's mean, as the shown answer says); YES/NO and PICK stay A1-STD's. Abstention: UNKNOWN when the nearest Mahalanobis distance exceeds the p95 of taught distances (A1's own band logic, in the reader's metric); NOTHING when the margin is under the p5 of training margins.
4. **A1-PROTO** — A1-SLDA with the shared covariance replaced by the identity (running means, cosine): the cells' rule inside the network — one difference from 3.
5. **IN-SLDA** — the organ floor: the SAME reader as 3 on the INPUT alone, F_pool (the unit-normalised mean of the 5 glances, the creature's own BIND pooling, from the cache — no network, no trunk). NAME by the reader; POINT = the candidate nearest the heard name's mean; IS = the margin's sign; every relation cue NOTHING («no road», as A0 — CONST/SAME read NOT APPLICABLE by R38's rule); U6: `zog` becomes a mean at its one showing; U2: the Mahalanobis band. A harness-level arm beside RING-READ (`brain/twins/`), reading the cache and never the truth (the AST sweep of §2.6 applies).
6. **A1-STD-IN** — A1 with the INPUT glance block standardised per dimension by running statistics before the trunk, the heads RAW (Q1 rank 4's lever): one difference from A1, and the control that separates «the head's input» from «the trunk's input».

**Twins and controls.** SHUFFLED-NAME on A1-STD, A1-SLDA, A1-PROTO, IN-SLDA (the grounding null: U1 at chance); FORCED on the same four (U2/U5's null: their abstentions carry `raw["forced"]`); SHUFFLED-RELATION on A1-STD and A1-SLDA (U3's null; the relation heads read the standardised state). ## Where I disagree with the co-signed CONCLUSION.md (09-24), and with one reading in EVIDENCE.md

1. **§3 part 2 — «a grown, gated, recurrent network (soft switches)» as the slow store that answers.** The record since 09-24 shows the network's ANSWER road broken by construction (a raw-state SGD head: Q1) and its state a clock (rank 2). The slow store's naming must be a normalised, metric readout — a prototype/LDA reader — and the network's claim to the body must be earned on ORDER, prediction and the mouth, not assumed. CONCLUSION's «the soft net stays in every hybrid» (D1's hold) should be reversed to «the reader + D is the candidate; the network is a measured passenger until U3 reads» — the position D itself was held to, and D earned.
2. **§3 law 2 — «it learns by predicting the next moment of every sense».** On this tape the next-step objective is dominated by the row's own clock and the state learned that (Q1 rank 2, Geirhos's shortcut). The law should read: it learns by predicting what the TEACHER will say and what an ACT will bring about the thing in view, and by pulling the showings of one thing together (his page 6's contrastive geometry) — prediction of the next glance is a part of the world model, never the whole learning signal.
3. **§3 law 3 and B2 — growth «under a budget that regenerates».** Kept — with the finding that A1's growth costs 5.4 × its learning through the pre-allocated buffers (`probe_cost_hmax.out`): B1's COST bar (307 ms) is failed by A1 in B1.1 (430–445 ms/event under contention, `summary.json`) for a reason that has nothing to do with the brain's design. Grow the buffers in chunks; price every arm in the launch's shape.
4. **§4.1's twins as decisions (GROWS, NO-SLEEP, LOCAL, BPTT-AWAKE, ATT, MA, MLC).** None of them has been measured: every comparison was between two raw-head readings at chance, while the states behind them all keep the letters (the standardised reader on A1, A1-BPTT-AWAKE, A1-TEACH and A1-FIXED: 92–97 % taught, 65–71 % unseen fonts, both seeds — `probe_state.out`). Their verdicts in B1.0/B1.1 must be read NOT GRADABLE (the instrument could not see the thing) until B1.2's readout fix, not «growth bought nothing at letters».
5. **§2, the six marks at letters — U6 and U2 for the network.** CONCLUSION's table (RESEARCH-OPUS §4.1) predicted the soft net «CANNOT» learn from one encounter «at a slow learning rate». With a prototype readout the network's own state CAN — a new mean from one showing (P5) — so the «cells for U6, network for order» split is not a law of substrates; it is a fact about the readout that was chosen.
6. **EVIDENCE.md F3 — «the one-name collapse is a SCHEDULE fact».** Refuted by B1.1 (`read_b11.out`, both seeds): with CONST interleaved, every network still gives one name to all 704 T_L asks. The collapse is the start-state fact of Q1 (e); the CONST block only chose WHICH name.
7. **§7 D-6 (the library ×4) and D1's decision rule.** With D at the floor budget already naming 75 % and composing 20/21, the open question is not D's budget but the hybrids' SAME failure (F7: the cells' name and D's name disagree). The reader fixes that at its root: one namer for the whole hybrid (IN-SLDA's means are the same object D's `taught`/`sim` atoms read). D1 (i)–(iii) should be re-read after B1.2 with the hybrids re-built on one namer.
8. **Kept, and said so:** the six marks and their laws (§2); the identity law (§3 law 8); THE SCHOOL's flow; D-1 (no chatbot text teaches); D-3 (this PC only); the perceptual families NOT GRADABLE by the organ (bars.json) — nothing here moves a bar.
