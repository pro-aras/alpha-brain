**Q1 · «Understood, not predicted» — do I read his page 5 right, and how is it MEASURED?**
The files: page 5 (verbatim) «The words AI speaks or the language it has shall be completely understood by it, not predict. But the prediction plays important role in its learning curve. It predicts what can be action to any reaction or any reaction to any action»; page 6 «The more predictions of brain about something get/goes wrong, the more it learns»; his 09-03 word «the prediction for speaking is a wrong direction and i never intended it for that» (quoted in `docs/organs/MIND-THINKING-LANGUAGE.md` §4.7); «never next-token» on `SENTENCE` and in `genome/chain.py`; his 11:10 «training a neural network on tokens again the same shit already there in the market».
*Provisional:* prediction is the LEARNING engine and never the MOUTH. The brain learns by predicting its own body's next moment in every sense at once (the next glance, sound, heard word, the outcome of its act — «action to reaction, reaction to action»); that does include predicting the next heard word, and in the library phase the next word read — said plainly, not hidden. It speaks by INTENT: what to say is chosen from its state for a declared goal (answer, name, ask, report a surprise), rendered through forms it learned, back-parsed and checked against its own perception before it leaves the mouth (his layer 6, «gate before speaking»); no free-running sampling from a word distribution ever reaches the mouth. «Understood» is measured, not asserted: (i) the content is fixed before the form (two contents → the same form — the A4L-LANG unit test's shape); (ii) every content word works in BOTH directions through the senses (word → the thing shown, thing → the word) on held-out items; (iii) a claim it says back-parses to what its own perception holds (L30: grounded and cited); (iv) a shuffled-world twin says different things (the words carry the world, not the habit).
*What turns on it:* §3 (the learning signal), §4 (the mouth), §6 (the library). **Also asked:** the seat's exact words to him between 11:03 and 11:24 (what his «Yes, we can grow a brain» answered), so the design never contradicts what he accepted.

**Q2 · Identity across machines — is replay byte-identical PER MACHINE CLASS, or everywhere?**
The files: the identity law (ALPHA4-PREREG §2: a closed tape replays byte-identically under its code); E48's port reached byte-exactness only by deciding the arithmetic first (float64 compute from float32 state, ranking on float32-rounded scores — #227 (5)); a multi-threaded BLAS can change a sum's order with the thread count; a Ryzen and an M1 do not give the same float bits for the same network; GPU reductions (MPS, MLX, CUDA) are not guaranteed deterministic.
*Provisional:* per MACHINE CLASS. A life replays byte-identically on the machine class it lived on, the thread count and the BLAS pinned on the record; a phase run elsewhere (the Mac, a rented GPU) enters the life as a SEALED EPOCH — what it read, in what order, on the tape; its product (the weights' sha) on the tape; its own replay proven on its own machine class by a sampled window (the replay-windows lane's road). Platform-independent arithmetic (integer or fixed-point weights and accumulators — a Tsetlin machine is integer by nature) is priced as a CHECK, not chosen.
*What turns on it:* the brain's arithmetic, which machines it may live on, whether the Mac can host any phase.

**The spine these questions assume — provisional, and the research may overturn it:** the body, its tape, its laws and its cells unchanged; a BRAIN clause grows a slow predictive network inside the mind (one writer); awake, one cheap step per moment (predict the next moment of every sense; error-driven updates); asleep, at machine speed while the world waits, replay of short windows from the cells' episodes and the tape; new units born where error persists, under a budget that refuses, never a cap that eats; the wrong and the redundant pruned, never the unused (#227); it speaks by intent through a judged mouth; THE SCHOOL grades every stage; the toy first. **His five words are read as LEARNED gates, never hand-written ones** (his 11:40 «These mixture of gates shouldn't be hand designed but should get adjusted as the learning goes on»): a unit's thresholded sum is an AND or an OR by its weights; a learned gate that chooses between keeping the old state and taking the new one is an IF / ELSE; a state carried to the next moment is a THEN. Whether soft learned gates (a gated recurrent network) or hard learned gates (learned logic gates; a Tsetlin clause machine) carry language further is the research's question and, after it, the letters toy's.
