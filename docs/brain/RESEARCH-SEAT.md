# RESEARCH-SEAT — «Can a grown, learned mixture of logic-like gates produce language and understanding?» — the seat's independent research (Claude Fable 5.1), 2026-09-24 11:51 +0400

**Method (his page 11):** every claim below is checked against a live source today (WebSearch/WebFetch, 2026-09-24 11:5x–12:2x +04) or marked *(from memory, unverified today)*; the seat's prior was written into the register BEFORE this research so it can be held to it: *a transformer is already a learned mixture of soft gates; the open question is what a logic-native, growing, discretely composed version buys.* This file is written without reading the Opus lane's research (protocol step 2).

## 0 · The question, made precise (three readings of «language is a mixture of AND/IF/ELSE/OR/THEN»)
1. **Substrate reading:** the units of the brain are gate-like (each computes a logical function of a few inputs) and learning chooses which gate and which wiring.
2. **Computation reading:** understanding a sentence = executing a composition of discrete operations over grounded things (the sentence IS a small program), and learning = acquiring the operations and how they compose.
3. **Semantics reading (his «understood, not predicted»):** a word's meaning is what it does to the body's predictions about its own sensory future; understanding is testable by acting on it.
The evidence below sorts by these readings; they have different answers.

## 1 · Evidence, line by line (what it has shown · at what scale · what it has NOT shown · what it means for the letters stage)
1. **Differentiable logic gate networks (DLGN)** — Petersen, Borgelt, Kuehne, Deussen, NeurIPS 2022 (arXiv 2210.08277): a network whose neurons are relaxed 2-input logic gates (a learned distribution over 16 gate types; wiring fixed at init); MNIST/CIFAR at >1M images/s on one CPU core. **Convolutional DLGN**, NeurIPS 2024 oral: CIFAR-10 86.29 % with 61 M gates. **Recurrent DLGN** (arXiv 2508.06097, Aug 2025): the first sequence version — WMT'14 En→De **4.39 BLEU at inference vs a GRU baseline's 5.41** — a proof of concept, explicitly not competitive. Also «Light DLGN» (2510.03250) and «Differentiable Logic Synthesis» (2601.13953). *Reading 1 has a working substrate for vision; for language it has ONE toy-scale result. The wiring is fixed at initialization in all of them — growth of wiring is not in this literature.*
2. **Tsetlin machines** — propositional clause learners (conjunctions of literals learned by automata); NLP classification and 2023–2025 word-embedding work (TM Embedding 2301.00709; Omni TM-AE 2505.16386; FastOmniTMAE 2605.06982); a dedicated ISTM community. Our own E20 (09-13): noisy XOR 74.7 % after the seat fixed a runtime-arithmetic overflow. **Not shown:** generative language, sequence-to-sequence, grounding. *Reading 1: interpretable, cheap, classification-grade; no evidence it composes sentences.*
3. **Logical neural networks (IBM, Riegel et al. 2020)** — one neuron per logic gate, weighted real-valued logic; KBQA demos; a 2024 comparative study (2508.03366) finds LNN and Logic Tensor Networks *limited to problems with known, well-defined rules*. *Reading 1 with the rules given by hand — exactly what he forbids.*
4. **Neuro-symbolic concept learner (Mao et al. 2019, CLEVR)** and its line (VCML 2020, DCL 2021): words become learnable concept embeddings, sentences become symbolic PROGRAMS executed over object-centric scene representations, near-perfect CLEVR and strong generalization. **Not shown:** learning the program grammar itself (a semantic parser and a DSL of operations are given); open-ended language. *Reading 2 works when the operations exist — the gates here are programs over grounded traits, which is precisely his page-3/4 (traits + geometry) and page-10 (identify → classify → do).*
5. **Program-library learning — DreamCoder (Ellis et al. 2021, PLDI), Stitch (2211.16605, compression 1,000–10,000× faster), LILO (ICLR 2024, 2310.19791)**: a wake–sleep loop that solves tasks by search, then COMPRESSES recurring sub-programs into a growing library — «gates that grow» as reusable abstractions; LILO improves DreamCoder's solve rates by +42 (REGEX), +63 (CLEVR), +43 (LOGO) points. **Caveat for us:** LILO's synthesizer is an LLM (borrowed weights — out on the pure road); DreamCoder's own neural recognition model is trained from scratch (in). *Reading 2's strongest form: the library IS a learned, growing set of composable operations. Its cost is SEARCH, and it needs a small primitive DSL to start from.*
6. **Compositional Program Generator (CPG, 2309.16467)**: perfect SCAN and COGS from 14 / 22 examples («1000× sample efficiency») — but *requires a context-free grammar of the input language*. And **transformers CAN be made to do COGS systematically** (RASP-constructed encoder–decoders, 2504.15349, near-perfect structural splits) while standard training gives 0 % on some COGS structural generalizations (the benchmark's own report). *Reading 2: composition is achievable by both roads; the gate/program road buys sample efficiency at the price of a given grammar; the transformer road buys nothing for free either — its success came from constructing the right circuit by hand.*
7. **Transformers ARE learned circuits** — Elhage et al. 2021 «A Mathematical Framework for Transformer Circuits» (QK routing, OV writes); Tracr compiles programs into transformers (2301.05062); saturated transformers are constant-depth threshold circuits (Merrill, Sabharwal, Smith, TACL 2022 — checked); 2024–25 work reads attention heads as *fuzzy Boolean operators whose compositions mimic multi-layer logic circuits*; «Circuit Transformer» preserves logical equivalence (2403.13838). *This settles reading 1 in HIS favour and against the dichotomy at once: the engine that produces language today is a learned mixture of soft gates. The difference between «his method» and «the market» is therefore not the substrate's nature but (a) discreteness/interpretability, (b) whether the wiring GROWS, (c) what it is trained on and graded by.*
8. **Kolmogorov–Arnold networks (KAN, ICLR 2025)**: wins only on symbolic regression; a critical assessment (2407.11075) and later comparisons find MLPs ahead on vision and NLP; slow training. *Not a road to language.*
9. **Predictive coding networks (local, Hebbian-plausible learning)** — Millidge, Salvatori et al. (IJCAI-22 review); 2025: μPC scales to 100+ layers (2505.13124), ePC (2505.20137), Pinchetti et al. ICLR 2025 benchmark: *matches backprop on small–medium tasks, lags for deep nets*; Sakana's augmented-Lagrangian PC trains 1,000-layer nets without backprop (2605.31022). *For a 10–50 M online brain at the body's pace, local rules are viable now; for a deep library-phase model, backprop still wins. His «prediction error is how it learns» is the mainstream of this literature.*
10. **Loss of plasticity (Dohare et al., Nature 632:768, 2024)**: standard backprop LOSES the ability to learn over long task sequences (ImageNet binary tasks: 89 % → 77 % by task 2,000, a linear network's level); the cure, *continual backpropagation*, continually re-initializes a small fraction of the LEAST-USEFUL units (utility = contribution, not mere usage). **A direct meeting with our E48 (#227):** our failing cap replaced the least-USED unit and that was forgetting; Dohare reinitializes low-UTILITY units and that preserves plasticity. The two are reconcilable only if utility ≠ usage — a point for the debate and a toy that can decide it.
11. **Complementary learning systems** — McClelland, McNaughton, O'Reilly 1995 (*from memory*); CLS-ER (Arani et al., ICLR 2022, 2201.12604): a fast plastic model + slow stable model (EMA weights) + episodic replay, state of the art across CL settings then. *Our cells (fast, episodic) + a slow growing network + sleep replay is this design; it has a measured record.*
12. **Event-driven substrates can do language at scale** — SpikeGPT (2302.13939; 45 M–260 M, competitive, 20× fewer operations on neuromorphic hardware); 2026 spike-driven LLM work reports 63 % MMLU; SpiNNaker2 language modelling (2312.09084); Loihi 2 demos. *His page 1–2 («no master clock, event-driven, low watts») is supported at the substrate level; the algorithms are still prediction-trained networks.*
13. **Hyperbolic geometry** — Poincaré embeddings (Nickel & Kiela, NeurIPS 2017): hierarchies at low distortion, SOTA lexical entailment on WordNet; Lorentz model (ICML 2018); 2024–25: hyperbolic pre-trained LMs (TASLP 2024), HELM hyperbolic LLMs via mixture-of-curvature experts (NeurIPS 2025), hyperbolic Mamba (2505.18973), a hyperbolicity atlas of LLM hidden states (2609.07053). *His «hyperbolic tokenizing» has a live literature: a geometric memory for IS-A hierarchies (letter ⊂ word ⊂ sentence; circle ⊂ shape).*
14. **Language is not thought** — Fedorenko, Piantadosi, Gibson, *Nature* 630:575 (June 2024): a double dissociation in humans; language is a tool for communication, not a prerequisite for complex thought; Mahowald et al. (TiCS 2024) apply the same to LLMs. *His ordering — a brain first, language as its output — is the neuroscience's reading, not against it.*
15. **The language of thought as program induction** — Rule, Tenenbaum, Piantadosi, «The child as hacker» (TiCS 2020); Piantadosi 2023 «Learning as Bayesian inference over programs»; Ellis et al. 2022 *Nature Comms* «Synthesizing theories of human language with Bayesian program induction»; «Program induction can learn language» (PNAS 2022 reply). *Reading 2 has a cognitive-science programme behind it: language learned as programs = compositions of primitives, by search with a simplicity prior. Its known cost: search, and a primitive set to start from.*
16. **World models** — DreamerV3 (2301.04104; *Nature* 2025): categorical latents, imagination-based learning, 150+ tasks with one configuration, Minecraft diamonds from scratch. *His «digital space it creates on his own» is a built method; the question for us is only its size on this box.*

## 2 · What the evidence says, in four sentences
- **(A)** His intuition that language is a mixture of learned gates is RIGHT at the substrate level, and the market's engine is one such mixture (item 7) — the dichotomy «this new method vs the token model» is false at reading 1.
- **(B)** A logic-NATIVE substrate (discrete gates, learned wiring) has produced classification-grade results and ONE toy-scale language result (4.4 BLEU); it has never produced fluent language, and none of its versions grows its wiring (items 1–3).
- **(C)** Every system that has SOLVED compositional language tests has done it at reading 2 — programs/operations composed over grounded representations, learned by SEARCH with a compression prior, from a small primitive set (items 4–6, 15). That is his page-9/10 loop (search → branch tests → found → memory saved) and page-3/4 (traits + geometry), not gradient alone.
- **(D)** Continual, grounded, event-driven learning has measured methods (items 9–13) that are indifferent to reading 1 vs 2; his continuous-active, prediction-driven, two-memory design is the mainstream of that literature, and the one hard fact in it (item 10) reconciles with our E48 only if «useful» is measured, not «used».

## 3 · The seat's conclusion (to be debated)
The design the evidence supports is a **hybrid, grown from the genome**: (i) a **growing library of learned operations** (reading 2 — DreamCoder-style: solve by search, compress what recurs into named reusable gates, from a tiny primitive set of body-level operations: attend, compare, sequence, bind) as the slow, compositional, READABLE store — this is where «understands, not predicts» lives, because a program over grounded traits can be executed and its failure named; (ii) a **small predictive network** (reading 1 — a learned soft-gate mixture, trained by prediction error online at the body's pace, local rules where they suffice) as the fast perceptual front and the mouth's fluency; (iii) the **cells as episodic memory and sleep as consolidation** (CLS), with growth under a budget and Dohare's utility-based renewal instead of disuse decay; (iv) grounding by the body's stream and the School's exams; (v) a small world model as his digital space. **Can «this new method» produce language? Yes for meaning and composition (demonstrated by item 4–6's class at toy-world scale); not yet for fluent surface generation, which no logic-native system has shown and which the predictive front supplies.** His «we can at least try» is right and cheap: the letters stage is small enough to run every arm.

## 4 · The first experiment (a pre-registration sketch for THE SCHOOL's letters stage)
- **World:** the School's stage 0–2 (letters in 30 training fonts → words → two-word and gated frames: «red circle», «circle AND square», «IF bell THEN look left»), grounded on the creature's eye/ear traits, with 30 held-out fonts.
- **Arms (same data, same budget ≤ 1 CPU-day each on this box or the M1 Max):** A a small transformer (2–10 M) online; B a differentiable logic gate net with a growth rule; C a Tsetlin machine; D a program-library learner (search + compression over the organs' traits, OUR searcher, no LLM); E the hybrid (D + a small predictive front).
- **Marks (bars written before the run; ceilings measured first, #228):** name a letter in an unseen font (generalization); compose «X AND Y», «IF X THEN act» on UNSEEN combinations (systematicity — the SCAN/COGS shape at our scale); retention across three arrivals (continual — E46/E48 marks); examples to bar (sample efficiency); ms per moment (cost); and a READ mark: can the seat state, from the model alone, why it answered (interpretability — his «understood»).
- **Controls:** a shuffled-label twin per arm (null), a frozen twin (growth matters), the identity law kept (deterministic seeds).
- **What refutes the seat's conclusion:** if B or C alone reaches the composition AND word-output marks at ≤ A's cost, the predictive front is unnecessary; if D cannot grow its library from the body's stream without a hand-written DSL beyond four primitives, the «gates by search» half is hand design in disguise and falls under his prohibition; if A wins every mark including READ, the logic-native premise is refuted at this stage.
