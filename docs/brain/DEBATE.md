### D2 · Renewal: is utility-based RENEWAL (Dohare 2024) a law of the brain?
- **Against my own position first:** loss of plasticity is real and was missing from my survey: standard backprop nets fall «from 89% accuracy on an early task down to 77%, about the level of a linear network, on the 2000th task» (Dohare et al., arXiv 2306.13812, its own abstract). A creature that learns for life with birth and freezing alone may stop learning; the seat is right that a law is needed.
- **Verdict: CONCEDE the law; HOLD its form.** A plasticity mechanism becomes a named, measured part of the brain. But, from the paper's own text (PMC11338828): continual backpropagation re-initialises «**less-used** units»; utility u ← η·u + (1−η)·|h|·Σ|w_out| is ACTIVITY-weighted; and «We continued training on the old classes (unlike in most work in class-incremental learning) to focus on plasticity rather than on forgetting». So:
  1. by that formula a unit silent on the current stream has near-zero utility and is renewed — on the awake stream alone, the same shape as E48's least-used cap, which forgot Latin on this box (#227). **Dohare offers no evidence either way** on what renewal does to old families once they stop flowing — retention was not measured («to focus on plasticity rather than on forgetting»). So the design measures renewal's utility over the awake stream **plus sleep replay** of the old families, and B2's RENEWAL-AWAKE vs RENEWAL-REPLAY pair is the FIRST measurement of the question;
  2. **the paper's criterion is «a random, non-gradient component»** («Plasticity is maintained indefinitely only by algorithms that continually inject diversity into the network»; «sustained deep learning requires a random, non-gradient component to maintain variability and plasticity» — its Nature abstract). Our births are not random as written: the cells' `_birth` COPIES the input (`area.cells[cid] = x`, `genome/machinery.py`), and A1's insertion was never given an initialisation. Dohare's own re-initialisation is random AND function-preserving: incoming weights resampled from the initial distribution, outgoing weights set to zero — «Initializing the outgoing weights as zero ensures that the newly added hidden units do not affect the already learned function» (PMC11338828). **So A1's births adopt that initialisation (random in, zero out)**, and B2 tests whether a COPY birth suffices: BIRTH-COPY vs BIRTH-RANDOM. Renewal is then required only where a size bound exists — if B2 says birth suffices;
  3. the seat's ten-arrival B2 is probably below the regime where the loss appears (Dohare needed thousands of tasks) — a bar above its own ceiling (#228 (b)) unless the stream is long;
  4. **B2 needs its own stage −1:** 200 arrivals × 5 invented signs = 1,000 glyphs through a tower trained on Latin, and many invented signs may be near-identical through the organ (E51's lesson; #228 (c)) — the organ oracle is run on the invented sign set, and near-identical signs are removed or merged, BEFORE any bar is declared.
- **The experiment — B2, amended:**
  - stream: ≥ 200 small arrivals (five invented signs each, with invented names, through the creature's organs), plus a permuted-trait stream in Dohare's style;
  - arms: FIXED (Dohare's failing control) · **BIRTH-COPY** (a regenerating budget; the new unit's incoming weights copy the input, as the cells do) · **BIRTH-RANDOM** (the same budget; Dohare's initialisation — random in, zero out) · BIRTH-RANDOM + RENEWAL-AWAKE (utility on the awake stream) · BIRTH-RANDOM + RENEWAL-REPLAY (utility over awake + replay) · BIRTH + DECAY (E48's failing form) · FIXED + CBP (Dohare's cure, awake only);
  - marks: plasticity = showings to the bar on arrival k, its slope over k; retention = arrival 1's (and every 20th arrival's) held-out score at arrival k; the fast store's size over k (the seat's C5); cost.
- **My prediction** [Guessing — no run yet]: FIXED loses plasticity by arrival 200 (if it does not, the stream is too short and the plasticity marks read NOT GRADABLE); **BIRTH-RANDOM** keeps plasticity (slope ≈ 0) and retention (drop ≤ 5 pp) on both streams; **BIRTH-COPY** keeps both on the arrival stream (every new sign births from its own input) but loses plasticity faster than BIRTH-RANDOM on the permuted stream (copies of permuted inputs carry no new diversity of weights); RENEWAL-AWAKE keeps plasticity and **forgets arrival 1 (drop > 5 pp)**; RENEWAL-REPLAY keeps both; DECAY forgets; FIXED + CBP keeps plasticity and forgets the old.
- **What flips D2:** if BIRTH-RANDOM loses plasticity like FIXED → birth is not diversity enough, and renewal-with-replay is a law; if RENEWAL-AWAKE keeps retention → Dohare's utility protects the old without replay, and the seat's reconciliation stands; if BIRTH-COPY matches BIRTH-RANDOM on the permuted stream → the random component is not needed where births come from new inputs.
