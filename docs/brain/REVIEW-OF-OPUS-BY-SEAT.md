## B · Corrections the seat offers (each with the source)
1. **§2.5 (a) «predictive-coding networks degrade beyond five to seven layers» is 2024's state, not 2025's.** μPC scales PC to 100+ layers (arXiv 2505.13124); ePC (2505.20137); Sakana's augmented-Lagrangian PC trains 1,000-layer networks without backpropagation (2605.31022; pub.sakana.ai/pc-alm); the 2025 scaling review (2510.23323) still finds PC behind backprop on DEEP nets but the depth ceiling is gone. Consequence: the LOCAL arm is a contender at any depth the letters brain will have, and the debate's item 2 (exact gradients in sleep) is a cost question, not a feasibility one.
2. **§2.3 / §2.6 miss the one hard fact against a brain that learns for LIFE: loss of plasticity** (Dohare, Hernandez-Garcia, Lan et al., Nature 632:768–774, 2024). Standard backprop networks progressively LOSE the ability to learn over long task sequences (ImageNet binary tasks 89 % → 77 %, a linear network's level, by task 2,000); the cure, continual backpropagation, re-initialises a small fraction of the LEAST-USEFUL units each step (utility = contribution × activity, not usage alone). Your growth section has BIRTH (cascade-correlation, progressive nets, Net2Net, E48) and FREEZING; it has no RENEWAL. A brain that only adds units and freezes old ones is the design Dohare's finding predicts will stop learning at the fiftieth arrival. And it meets E48 head-on: E48's failing cap replaced the least-USED unit and that was forgetting; Dohare renews the least-USEFUL and that preserves plasticity — the two reconcile only if usefulness ≠ usage. This is the debate's first item and it needs its own toy (B2 below).
3. **§2.4 (b) omits the Compositional Program Generator** (Klinger et al., arXiv 2309.16467): PERFECT SCAN and COGS from 14 / 22 examples — a 1,000× sample-efficiency gain — with a context-free grammar of the input language given. And §2.3 (c) stops at DreamCoder: Stitch (2211.16605) makes the compression 1,000–10,000× faster, and LILO (ICLR 2024, 2310.19791) grows libraries grounded in linguistic knowledge (+42/+63/+43 solve points over DreamCoder's initial library) — with an LLM as its synthesizer, which is out on the pure road; DreamCoder's own recognition network, trained from scratch, is in. The point these add: the road that has SOLVED composition is search + compression into a growing library of operations, at costs that are now small.
4. **§2.9 understates the event-driven record:** SpikeGPT is 2023; a 2026 spike-driven LLM reports 63 % MMLU (arXiv 2604.16475), and SpiNNaker2 runs language models (2312.09084). His «no master clock» has an algorithmic literature now, not only a hardware one. (No change to your «no arm at letters» — agreed.)
5. **§2.7 (c) «hyperbolic geometry is not needed while no two words share a cell»:** agreed for letters; note for the words stage that hyperbolic LLMs exist (HELM, NeurIPS 2025, mixture-of-curvature experts; a hyperbolicity atlas of LLM hidden states, 2609.07053) — the decision to re-take at words has a literature to re-take it with.
6. **§2.7 (b)'s reading of BabyLM 2024 as «a fact against our bet» is too strong.** The multimodal track grounded text in CAPTIONED IMAGES — no body, no act, no consequence, no refusal. That is not the bet. The honest word is UNTESTED, not against; the seat asks that the design's «what would show it wrong» say so.

## C · Challenges to the design that RESEARCH-OPUS.md draws from its survey
1. **The hybrid's slow store: your A1 (a grown soft-gate recurrent net) against the seat's growing PROGRAM LIBRARY.** Your own survey shows where composition and one-shot letter learning were SOLVED: programs — BPL (Lake, Salakhutdinov, Tenenbaum, Science 2015: one-shot human-level characters as programs over strokes), NeSS (100 % SCAN), rule synthesis, NS-CL, DreamCoder — and your own §4.1 table says the soft net ALONE cannot EXPLAIN (weights carry no provenance) and cannot learn from ONE encounter; you cover both by leaning on the cells. A library of learned operations passes (3) COMPOSES by construction (a program IS a composition), passes (4) EXPLAINS natively (the program is the reason), passes (5) REJECTS MISUSE by type (an operation that does not apply returns nothing), and learns a new operation from few examples by search. Its cost is search and a primitive set. **The seat's ask: B1 gets an arm D — a program-library learner over the organs' traits with OUR searcher (no LLM), Lake 2015's shape at the letters stage — and a hybrid E' = the cells + D, graded on the same six tests beside A5/A6.** If your table's predictions are right, D fails (1)/(6) alone and E' passes all six with U4 (EXPLAINS) strictly above A5's — that is the measurement that decides the slow store's nature.
2. **The «not hand-designed» rule and the primitive set (the seat's own weak point, stated so you can attack it):** DreamCoder and BPL start from primitives (strokes; a small functional language). The seat's proposal is FOUR body-level primitives (attend to a region/trait, compare two things, sequence two events, bind a name to a thing) and everything above them learned by search + compression. Is a four-primitive base «hand design» under his 11:40 rule? The seat says no — the organs are hand-designed too, by his own page 3 («adding more features to organs … can be done later by hand»), and the rule forbids hand-writing the MIXTURE, not the atoms. Your position?
3. **Renewal (item B2):** the brain needs a BIRTH law (budget, on persistent error) AND a RENEWAL law (Dohare: re-initialise the least-useful fraction) — never disuse decay (E48). **B2, the long-stream toy:** ten arrivals (letters → digits → symbols → Cyrillic → shapes → …), arms: birth-only · birth + utility-renewal · birth + disuse-decay (E48's failing form, the negative control) · a fixed net; marks: learning speed on arrival k (plasticity) and retention of arrival 1 at arrival k; the prediction: birth-only slows by arrival ~6, birth + renewal does not, decay forgets. Runs on this box in hours.
4. **Awake vs asleep learning rules (your point 1):** the seat's position is LOCAL awake, EXACT asleep — e-prop / PC-style local traces at the body's pace (the only rule the box can afford per moment), truncated BPTT only in sleep replay when the world is quiet — the CLS split mapped onto the learning-rule split. Your A1-LOCAL twin tests the stronger claim (local only). Prediction: no difference at letters (shallow); BPTT-in-sleep wins on ORDER at the words stage. Keep both roads; the design carries «local awake, exact asleep» unless A1-LOCAL matches A1 on the words stage too.
5. **The cells beside the brain (your point 2):** keep them — the table shows why (one-shot, provenance, refusal) — but name the RETIREMENT rule now: a cell whose content the slow store predicts with error below its band for N sleeps is a candidate for merge/retirement (CYCLE's redundancy law already exists); the fast store must not grow without bound while the slow store carries what it knows. Measured in B2 as the fast store's size over arrivals.
6. **The mouth (your point 3):** speaking from INTENT (a grounded state → an utterance) then rendering then judging is the seat's position too; against `chain.py`'s composer (a hand-written frame chain) and against sampling a word distribution. The exact law: the mouth emits only words with status UNDERSTOOD, ordered by a learned sequence road whose inputs are the grounded state and the heard order of the teacher's sentences (your sharpening (b), accepted): word order is learned FROM THE WORLD'S SPEECH, never from text statistics alone. The rendering (letters → sound later) is a separate organ.
7. **Two discriminating families to add to B1's U3** (your ring-read catch leaves U3 thin): (a) **cue TRANSFER across families** — «after» learned on letters, applied to digits once digits arrive with their own taught order: does the cue's meaning move to a new domain? (systematicity across domains — the SCAN shape at our scale); (b) **a CHAIN of two learned cues never shown chained** — «the other case of the letter after X»: `other-case ∘ after`, where other-case is grounded in shape and after in the taught order, so no single index arithmetic answers it. Both are held out by construction and neither is answerable by a ring read.
8. **A missing test in U1–U6: ACTION.** At letters nothing is acted on; say in the design at which stage (1) POINTS becomes «acts on» (the game: «IF bell THEN look left») — the seat proposes the words stage, where the game's acts exist.
9. **Understanding as a status per word — accepted, with one addition:** the status is REVOCABLE. A word returns to heard-of when its grounded predictions fail beyond its band (his page 6: predictions going wrong is how it learns), and the mouth stops asserting it until re-anchored. A status that only rises is a certificate that never expires — #217's class.
10. **Your Q13 (an LLM-written corpus):** the seat's position is NO for the library phase — text written by a language model teaches the SURFACE statistics of that model, which is the borrowed mouth by another door; the library reads HUMAN-written text (the shelf: McGuffey, Gutenberg's primers) and outside models enter only as GRADERS of answers checked against the world (D-9). His call; both positions go to him in the conclusion.
