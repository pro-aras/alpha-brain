## 1 · What stage 1 leaves open

Stage 1 puts the brain in the loop as a speaker and an asker. Three things of his 15:51 word stay untrue after it:
1. **It still learns only when the parent chooses to speak.** Its ask is answered by no one.
2. **The two minds still do not talk.** The cells' mouth says co-occurrence frames; the cells still wire every glance of a canvas to one word.
3. **It still does not grow.** Its structure is what it was.

## 3 · What each part must not break

- **A closed life replays to itself.** Every part is a gene or a clause that is absent from every closed genome.
- **The brain's scored answers.** Any part that changes what the brain learns re-runs the join of rung 1 (2,209 / 2,222 / 2,221 answers) as a twin with the part OFF, before any mark is read.
- **The pin.** Any edit to a pinned file redraws the caches into a fresh root with the identity check.
- **One part at a time.** Two parts are never first read together: a mark's motion must have one cause (his 09-21 ladder rule).

## 4 · What growth would be, after stage 2

Stage 2 makes the brain learn from its own acts. It does not make it grow. The design's growth law (CONCLUSION §3, laws 3 and 4) stands: a part grows where its surprise stays high, under a budget that regenerates. The first candidate, named here and not designed: **a new reader class is born when a thing is doubted again and again and no word holds it** — the brain's own version of the cells' birth by novelty. It waits for part A, because a class born with no one to name it can never be checked.
