### M1 — the chooser ranks acts by SURPRISE, and one reward locks it for good (CONFIRMED, root cause)
- **file:line.** `creature/mind.py:1037–1057` (`_record_reflex`: `expected` = the act's cumulative mean of past `got`, `residual = |got − expected|`, the surprise stored in the ring, the mean updated after filing), `:2042–2043` (`weights.append(sum(residuals)/len(residuals) if residuals else 0.0)` — the mean of the last 10 stored surprises per act), `:2054–2062` (10 % uniform, else argmax with rng ties).
