## 5 · ONE ORGANISM — the loop, and the order of the build

**The human loop, one turn of it:** the senses deliver → the brain predicts what it will sense → the difference is its surprise → the surprise chooses what it looks at, asks about and learns from → it acts (looks, points, speaks, moves) → the world answers → it learns → asleep, it replays and tidies. No one starts a turn from outside.

**The organism's loop today:** the senses deliver → the cells meet → the cell mind thinks, says, asks and runs → the world goes on. The brain stands beside the loop.

## 6 · What would show this map wrong

1. **The brain's own speech is no truer than the frame mouth's.** Then the brain in the mouth buys nothing, and B11's fix is refused.
2. **The brain asks about things it already knows as often as about things it does not.** Then its doubt is no guide, and B12's fix is refused.
3. **A twin that asks at random learns as fast.** Then asking is worth its answers, not its choices. Bazhydai 2026 predicts this.
4. **The brain's turn costs more than half again of a canvas.** Then it cannot keep up with a mind-paced world.
