## 0 · The question, and what is built

The architect's forgetting prediction (HEARS-2 AMENDMENT 2 A2.1): once `brain-long-3` hears the maker's world for days, the scene words decay, colour and size first. **The reader answers one thing and no more: what does the brain, as saved, answer on the SAME 3,011 sitting asks over the SAME canvases?** Items, canvases, grader and the reader's code are fixed; only the brain's state moves, so a difference between two states is exact for these items. **What it is read AGAINST is the brain at the sittings, rebuilt (§5) — never a daily copy.**

Built, in this order (the seat's): (1) `tools/brain/resit.py` + `tests/tools/test_resit.py` + `deploy/creature-brain-resit@.service`/`.timer` (written, **not enabled** — the seat enables it for `brain-long-3` after launch): R1 on the closed `brain-long-2` first, then R2–R9, R11, R12; (2) **after R1 passes**, `tools/brain/rebuild_origin.py` + its tests (§5, R10); (3) then EVOLVE-CAL's build, unless the seat reorders. **Builder: CLAIMS (Claude Sonnet 5.5, swarm claims) for all three.** The reader never writes under a life's root, never calls `Brain.learn`/`turn`, never touches a life's unit, and never imports `creature.mind`; the rebuild imports it (it replays the mind) and writes only under its own `--out`.

## 1 · One run

### 1.0 Which code is whose (A3 i–iii) — fixed before any code

- **Run BY PATH, never `-m`:** `python3 -B -I tools/brain/resit.py …`. The orchestrator process imports the standard library only, and refuses to run unless `sys.flags.dont_write_bytecode` is set (a cold import of `tools/brain/grade_life.py` — which the mind never imports — would otherwise write `<root>/code/tools/brain/__pycache__/*.pyc` INTO the live root) and unless `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS` and `OMP_NUM_THREADS` are `1` in its environment, as the life's own unit sets them (a brain state's float bits depend on the BLAS thread count, #246 (8)); both are recorded in the row. `-I` ignores `PYTHON*` variables, so hash randomisation is ON with a fresh seed per process (R2 reads that as a feature).
- **Two child processes, each run BY PATH with the same flags; no module of the life and of the grader ever meet:**
  - the **ANSWER child (the SUBJECT)** puts `<code>` first on `sys.path` BEFORE importing anything of `genome`, `brain`, `creature`, `tools`, then imports the life's own `genome.brain`, `genome.gaze`, `genome.machinery`, `creature.tape`, `creature.organs_road`, `brain.scene.*`. **After the imports it ASSERTS that every loaded module of those four packages has its realpath under `realpath(<code>)`** (a package imported earlier from elsewhere keeps master's `__path__` and silently serves master's submodules; `-m tools.brain.resit` does exactly this) and writes each file's sha256 into the row. `<code>` defaults to `<root>/code`, the checkout the life runs.
  - the **GRADE child (the GRADER)** is **the instrument's file, loaded by path from a PINNED commit that carries HEARS-2 AMENDMENT 4's grader** (`--grader-code <dir>`: a checkout of that commit; brain-long-3's learned types will hold maker words, and a grader without A4 crashes on exactly that, WorldError «arrow»). This is the second of the architect's two holds; the SUBJECT (brain, gaze, organs_road, tape) stays the life's. The pin (commit sha, the sha256 of `tools/brain/grade_life.py` and of every module the child loaded) is written once into `OUT/GRADER.json` at the first run; a later run under another pin is REFUSED. The answers cross from one child to the other as plain JSON.
- **The unit** (`deploy/creature-brain-resit@.service`) is its own cgroup with `CPUWeight=10` and `Nice=15` (`nice` divides a cgroup, never the box, #247 (4)), `-B -I` in its `ExecStart`, the two BLAS pins in its `Environment`. R12 reads the live life's load before and during the run.

### 1.1 The state — three sources, one mode each

- **S1 `--mode daily`:** `<root>/state/prev/` (the last COMPLETE snapshot generation, hard links). Read `prev/SNAPSHOT.json` `{files, last_seq, prints, t}`; `shutil.copy2` `brain.json` and `brain.npz` into `OUT/days/<stamp>/copy/`; each copy's `(size, mtime_ns)` must equal `prints`; a mismatch is retried 3 times, 20 s apart (a generation is being replaced), then the day is REFUSED by name. `Brain.load(copy/"brain", cfg)` adds its own check (the files must hash to the hash they carry).
- **S2 `--mode origin`, rebuilt (seat (i)):** `--state [SET=]DIR`, repeatable; DIR holds a `brain.json` + `brain.npz` pair **outside `state/prev`**, still hash-checked by `Brain.load`. `--state fresh=DIR1 --state s1b1=DIR2` answers the fresh items against the fresh-begin state and **the s1b1 items against the s1b1-begin state** (the brain learns maker canvases INSIDE a sitting: 126 in fresh and 114 in s1b1 on the bench); a bare `--state DIR` serves every set. A rebuilt DIR must also hold the rebuild's `ORIGIN.json` and `GENOME.in-force.genome` (§5): a bare pair of unknown provenance is REFUSED by name, its `state_hash` must equal the loaded copy's, the genome file's sha256 must equal the sidecar's. The copy is verified by sha256 against the source (no manifest of mtimes exists for an out-of-tree state).
- **S3 `--mode origin --origin-kind fallback_copy` (HEARS-2 A2.3 (6)(c)):** DIR is the seat's read-only copy of `state/prev` taken when `STATUS.json` showed the scene world ended (it carries its own `SNAPSHOT.json`, checked as S1). **Refused without `--reason "<the seat's words>"`**, which is recorded verbatim; every row paired against it prints `FALLBACK ORIGIN — late by at most one snapshot`. The reader cannot know whether (a)'s pilot failed: it records the seat's say.

### 1.2 The genome in force (A2) — the rule, not «the highest `GENOME.v<n>`» (v1's rule, withdrawn)

The eye (RETINA, GAZE, SENSE colour) and the BRAIN config are those of **the genome in force when the copied state was written**. The journal records an adoption as a row with an **`epoch` KEY** (not an `event` value): `{"seq": 211015, "epoch": {"epoch": 1, "genome_sha": "fd15f9e3…", "moment": 400200, "gene": "KWTA.k", "value": 4, "proposal_no": 5}}` — brain-long-2's journal holds exactly ONE (1 matching line over its five day files, in 2026-09-30.jsonl; 0 rows with `event: "epoch"`), and its `genome_sha` equals `sha256(GENOME.v1.genome)` (checked 12:37). The in-force epoch k is the highest whose row seq is **≤ the state's `last_seq`** (S1, S3) or **< the sitting's `begin` ask seq** (S2: the rebuild saves the in-force genome beside the state); the file is `GENOME.v<k>.genome` (k ≥ 1) or `GENOME.genome` (k = 0), and for k ≥ 1 its sha256 **must equal the epoch row's `genome_sha`**, else REFUSED. A root with `GENOME.v<n>` files and no readable epoch row is REFUSED (undetermined), never defaulted.
Every row carries the sha256 of the **RETINA, GAZE, SENSE colour and BRAIN lines** (canonical JSON of the parsed objects) and of the whole genome. **A row whose four line-hashes differ from the origin's is marked EYE-CHANGED**, and its pairing is printed under that label: it is not a brain-only difference (`BOUNDS` holds six keys that move the eye — GAZE.canvas, .fixations, .glance, .grid, RETINA, SENSE.colour — and none of BRAIN; brain-long-3 runs EVOLVE). **R1 on brain-long-2 stands by this rule and not by luck:** its `state/prev` (last_seq 331,518) is in epoch 1, whose file differs from `GENOME.genome` in ONE line (KWTA 5 → 4, which neither the gaze nor the brain's ask road reads); the four line-hashes of epochs 0 and 1 are printed equal as a reading the rule does not depend on.

### 1.3 The items (A4)

`--items-root` is explicit and **pinned** in `OUT/ITEMS.json` at the first run: `{root, n_asks, ask_digest (sha256 over the sitting asks' payload bytes in seq order), canvas_digest (sha256 over the sitting eye rows' `sha` in seq order), world_scene_line_sha, scored (path + sha256 of `scored/<ws>/fresh/marks.json`)}`; every later run re-checks it, and the SAME `--scored` reference is used every day. **For `brain-long-3` the items root is `brain-long-2`'s root** (the same seed's fixed sittings; brain-long-3 has no daily sitting — its own sittings happen once, at its scene stream's end, and are the origin's). In the origin run (only) the life's OWN sitting asks and canvases on its tape are read, read-only, and compared with the pinned items, item by item and sha by sha (**ITEMS-MATCH**): a difference REFUSES the origin (the series would not be about the life's sittings). The items root is a CLOSED life's root, read only.

### 1.4 The answers (the mind's own order, nothing learned)

Rows of the items root in tape order (`creature.tape.read_tape`): a canvas-less form (`begin`, `end`, `why`) is answered AT its ask row; every other form is held for its moment's eye row and answered there (`creature/mind.py`: `_consume_ask`, `_brain_canvas`); no other row is touched. At a held ask's eye row: `gaze.glances(rgb, retina, gaze, "surprise", row["sha"], colour=genome.colour, predicted=None)` — the call `_consume_eye` makes (a sitting canvas carries no ear word) — cached in `OUT/glances.pkl` under a content key (`sha` + the in-force RETINA/GAZE/colour line-hashes, #245). `brain.ask(payload, g)`; a `BrainError` is ANSWERED `refused`, as the mind does. The row is the mind's own `{"kind":"answer","by":"brain","item","mark","form","answer","moment","seq","eye_seq","at","fix","hyps","detail"}`, `fix`/`hyps` from `creature.organs_road.eye_view(g)`, `answer` through a ten-line copy of `_jsonable` pinned by a parity test. `begin` may calibrate the COPY (`Brain.ask` does so when `n_learned > 0 and calibrated_at != n_learned`) — the life's own sitting protocol; every row records `calibrated` and `calibrated_at` before and after. S2 switches to the set's own state at that set's `begin` row.

### 1.5 The grade and the row

`tools.brain.grade_life.grade(items_root, answers_journal, scored, label, genome=<items root>/GENOME.genome)` in the GRADE child (never `main()`, which writes `root/grade/`). `OUT/days/<stamp>/answers.jsonl` and `grade.json` are KEPT, their sha256 in the row (A4). ## 4 · The series, and the ORIGIN it is read against (rewritten; seat (ii), HEARS-2 A2.3 (6))

1. **Kinds.** `origin` — written ONCE by `--mode origin` from S2 (the rebuilt states, §5) or, labelled, S3; `daily` — S1, one per day; `gate` — R1/R1′/R10's runs, no series write. **The ORIGIN is the brain AT the sittings. A daily copy taken after the maker-only feed began has already absorbed the fast half of the drift and could refute a true prediction (A2.3 (6)); so the first daily run is the series' FIRST row, never its origin.**
2. **Pairing.** Every `daily` row is paired against the ORIGIN row **by item**, per set and pooled: g_c(t) = rate_c(t) − rate_c(origin) **on the items covered and gradable in both** (A2.2); printed with `n_paired`, `b` (right → wrong), `c` (wrong → right), the exact one-sided sign p, and 10 / n. No bar: the forgetting verdict is HEARS-2 A2.2's text applied by its reader of record; this series supplies its inputs.
3. **No origin yet:** the daily row is written with `vs_origin: null` and the line «NO ORIGIN». It prints no g_c.
4. **EYE-CHANGED** rows print their pairing under that label (§1.2).
5. **Restarts (HEARS-2 AMENDMENT 3 §A3.4 (3)):** every reading of a verdict copy and every g_c(t) prints the restarts that lie between the origin and the copy — the journal's `start` rows with `resumed_from_seq > 0` and the origin's fresh-begin seq < `resumed_from_seq` ≤ the copy's `last_seq` — because the maker world keeps its window/i/total in memory and, after any restart of the body, redraws its first pictures under the SAME moment names on NEW tape rows.

## 7 · Adversarial walk

1. **Can R1 pass without the reader being right?** Only if the reader copied the life's rows: it recomputes the glance from the TAPE's PNG and the journal row is what it is compared WITH; R3 nudges a COPY and must change answers computed from the tape.
2. **Can R1 fail for a reason that is not the reader?** Three: the brain moved after the sittings (P0, measured: it did not — R1 would read NOT GRADABLE, not a defect); a different code (hence `<code>`, asserted by realpath, R11); a different genome (the rule of §1.2, hash-checked). Not by luck: the premise rests on the journal's order and on SERIES.tsv, not on the HEALTH rows, which all post-date both events.
3. **Where can the mechanism fire?** On every class but two empty cells (§2). An ABLATED reader (`Brain.ask` replaced by the grader's uniform draw) reads M1 at chance — a unit test with a stub brain; the grader's M1 null (K = 20, p95 0.605) against the life's 0.980 says the mark can fire.
4. **Every «every / by N» counted:** 3,011 asks (the form census of §2, summing to 3,011); 1,464 sitting canvases; two sets; four dose values (R3: 0.001, 0.01, 0.1, 0.5, the last also the pass); ten refusals (R4 a–j); seven pairing tests (R8 a–g); the class counts of §2.
5. **The copy race:** `state/prev` is replaced every snapshot, its files never modified in place; the prints check, the state hash and three retries catch a generation torn between the two copies.
6. **A moving brain is not a frozen one.** Each day's copy is a different brain; `begin` calibrates it as the life's would. The series is the brain's reading AS SAVED, not what the life would have answered at a daily sitting (it has none).
7. **A daily copy is not an origin** (seat (ii)): enforced by mode (R8 a, b), not by habit; the fallback is labelled and needs the seat's reason.
8. **The origin's own fragility:** it rests on the replay being the life (R10c on every production run), on the begin rows' seqs, on the in-force genome saved with it, and on the pilot's price; if any fails the origin is not written, and nothing downstream is believed.
9. **What a pass does NOT show:** that the scene words are retained (the series, days later); that answers on other items are unchanged; that the organs' road answers the same (the reader answers by the brain alone); on an EYE-CHANGED row, which of eye and brain moved it.
