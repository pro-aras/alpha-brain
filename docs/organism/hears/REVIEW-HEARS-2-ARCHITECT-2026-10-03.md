## 2 · The defects, and AMENDMENT 1 as proposed

**A1.1 · C, COST — the launch, and the population of the bar.**
- *Launch:* OFF and ON start in the same minute through two slots; `cat /proc/loadavg; cat /proc/pressure/cpu` recorded at the start and the end of each arm. If the two arms' wall windows overlap < 90 %, C is VOID and the pair is re-run — a validity condition declared before the run, never a reading. Why: `--timings`' `cpu_ms` is `time.process_time()` — the whole process, every thread — and contention inflates a CPU-second as it inflates wall time (#242 addendum). The drive verdict's three seeds read absolute CPU per canvas 68.6 / 149.0 / 67.5 ms (a 2.2× swing on one box) while each ON/OFF ratio held 1.08–1.10 (`docs/organism/drive/VERDICT-BRAIN-DRIVES.md` row C); a ratio of two arms run under different loads prices the box.
- *Population:* the draft reader (`tools/brain/hears_read.py`, uncommitted at review) bars `pass_scene` only. On scene canvases both arms do the same brain work, so that ratio sits ≈ 1.0–1.15 and can barely fail — a bar placed where the mechanism adds nothing (#245 (2), #247 (2)). The law names «scene canvases and maker canvases»: the bar ≤ 1.5 is read on the MAKER canvases' ratio (median `cpu_ms` ON / OFF) AND on the whole replay's CPU total; the scene ratio is printed beside. Price: a maker canvas costs ≈ 93 ms CPU (median) with no brain work and the turn ≈ 12 ms (`/data/dev/brain-runs/day0929/drive/full/{off,on}/timings.jsonl`), so the maker ratio is expected near 1.2–1.4: the bar can bind there. Not a loosening — the population the law already names.

**A1.2 · T, TRANSFER — the maker-only arm is zero by construction (#247 (1)).** On its first three maker meetings a maker-only word has 0, 1 and 2 prior meetings at the turn (the turn precedes the learn of its canvas); `young 3` filters it out of `believed`, `held` and the types, so 15 of 15 maker-only turns can never agree, and the law's prediction («shared words are said truly … more often than maker-only words are») holds for any brain that agrees once in 18. Amended:
- the comparison is struck; the maker-only turns are kept as an OVER-EXTENSION reading — per turn, a scene shape word said (a child's «circle» for a dot), a colour or size word said, or silence; 13 of the 15 are calibrated (bar's and wave's first meetings precede the first calibration at 18:33:28);
- the SHARED arm (n = 18; all 18 calibrated with the word typed) is read as agreed over ALL 18 turns, against a DEALT-TARGETS null: the maker's words permuted among the same 18 canvases with the says fixed, ≥ 200 draws; transfer is shown iff the observed count exceeds the null's 95th percentile. With perfect says the null's p95 is 6 of 18 (pass ≥ 7); at k = 1 (n = 6) it is 3 (pass ≥ 4); both printed, and what was said on each miss.

**A1.3 · G, GROWTH — clause 1 cannot fail on this tape; clause 2's rate divides by the wrong count.**
- Clause 1 («every maker word with ≥ 3 meetings is mature by the last calibration»): `mature` IS meetings ≥ 3 (`RWLearner.mature_words`), and every maker word had ≥ 29 meetings at the last ON calibration (10,358 learned, 19:03:30; wave 29 the least) — it passes under either reading of «mature» whatever the brain does. Replaced by: every maker word typed at a calibration is SAID TRULY at least once (its first agreed say exists; its turn printed).
- Clause 2 («the true rate on the last 100 maker canvases is above 0.8»): the rate is agreed / ALL canvases on EXACTLY the last 100 maker turns. `worlds_read.py`'s windows are aligned at multiples of 100 (its last window holds 72 rows) and its `agree_rate` is agreed / SAID, which a nearly silent brain passes (#168). Ceiling 100 / 100 (all 100 come after the last calibration with their word typed); the floor of a mouth that always says the commonest maker word (≈ 0.12) printed beside.

**A1.4 · P, the scene words — the covered rates, both sittings, and the routes of a cost.** The draft computes rates over `graded`; the law names the COVERED rates. Both sittings are read, not only `fresh`. Discordant items are printed split (a) by phrase class — shape-only vs holding a colour or size word, «white» apart — and (b) by position in the sitting. Why: 240 of the 472 maker canvases arrive DURING the sittings (126 in `fresh`, 114 in `s1b1`), so ON's brain learns mid-exam while OFF's is frozen; and §5.2 names one route of a cost («one word, two depictions, one prototype») where the code has at least four:
1. the shared prototypes (§5.2's);
2. the delta rule updates EVERY word ever heard, with y = 0 when unsaid (`learners.RWLearner.train_scene`, step 2): 472 canvases of a WHITE thing named only by its shape push «white» (and «big», «small») down on those features — a PREDICTION, untested (#244 (8): the noisy-OR reads the parent's silence as evidence), its size set by 472 negatives beside the scene's own silences about colour (the parent says one word on 25 % of scenes, two on 50 %);
3. `stats.observe` runs on every learned canvas, so the maker shifts the running standardiser for every word;
4. the mid-sitting learning above.
A cost found only on colour/size phrases points at routes 2–3, not at prototypes, and the next design follows the route found, not the one assumed.
