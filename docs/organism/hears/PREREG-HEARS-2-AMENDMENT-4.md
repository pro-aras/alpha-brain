## A4.1 · The cause
The bench's ON arm (`hears scene maker`, replayed 11:22–12:07 under 55ef469's code) finished with exit 0 and its journal was written. `tools/brain/grade_life.py` then **crashed** on that journal (exit 1): `WorldError: not a word of this world: 'arrow'`, raised by `W.word_type` in `world_type_of_key` while the NAME road mapped the brain's learned types to the world's type names. OFF graded without error.

**The cause is the grader.** It was written for a brain that hears ONE world (the scene), so it assumed every word of a learned type — and of a phrase the brain SAYS — is a word the scene world can type. With the maker heard, a learned type holds the maker's words (`arrow`, `bar`, `diamond`, `dot`, `wave`, … none of them a word of `brain/scene/world.py`), and the brain may say one on a scene canvas. `word_type` raises on a word outside SHAPES, COLOURS, SIZES and the dax word. A second path of the same class was found by reading the scored marks: `in_parent_order` types every word of a SAID phrase of two or more words and would raise on a maker word there; `word_true_of`, by contrast, returns False for such a word and does not raise.

**What the real data exercised (an independent scan of both journals, not by the grader; presence counts beside every zero).** ON: 318 NAME answers, 954 learned-type keys (three per answer), and in ALL 318 the shape type's key holds the five maker-only words (`arrow`, `bar`, `diamond`, `dot`, `wave`) beside the scene's own shape words — that is the path that crashed; keys with no word of the scene world at all: 0 of 954. Said phrases: 348 `say` answers, 225 carrying a `spoken` list, 242 words in them, **0 foreign** (OFF: 234 lists, 260 words, 0 foreign). OFF's keys hold no maker word (0 of 954). So on this bench only the TYPE VOTE of the adaptation fires; the skip path and the said-phrase path read 0 and 0 and are pinned by the tests alone, until a longer life says a maker word on a scene canvas.

## A4.2 · The adaptation (in `tools/brain/grade_life.py` only; the scored modules `brain/scene/*` are untouched and pinned)
1. **The type vote** (`world_type_of_key`): the vote is over the words the scene world KNOWS; a learned type that holds none (a type of the maker's words alone) has no world type — `None` — and the NAME road SKIPS it and COUNTS it (`NAME:foreign_type_skipped`). Two learned types mapping to one world type is still a `GradeError`.
2. **A said phrase** (`world_words`): a foreign word in a phrase the brain SAID is read as the world's `DAX_WORD`, a shape word that is true of nothing a SPEAK, SPEAK2 or DECLINE scene shows (the moon, the dax shape, is drawn only in training scene 2,700 and in M6's scenes, and M6 takes no said phrase; a test enumerates the three splits of all six test seeds: 1,224 scenes, 3,096 things, 0 moons, with M6's 42 moons per seed as the plant): the phrase is then not TRUE, and its ORDER is read with a shape where the foreign word stood (the maker names shapes). The words so read are COUNTED (`<mark>:foreign_word→dax`, in `sets.<set>.forced`).
3. **Nothing else.** The pointing marks (M1, M2a, M3, M6), M5 and test 4a take no word the brain says; the adaptation does not touch their inputs.
The two counters (`NAME:foreign_type_skipped`, `<mark>:foreign_word→dax`) appear in `sets.<set>.forced` only when they are not zero; **an absent key is a zero, and the zero is read beside the independent scan above** (225 lists · 242 words · 0 foreign), never alone. They are a reading for P of how far the maker's words reach onto scene canvases (A1.5's over-extension): 0 on this bench.

- **Tests** (`tests/tools/test_grade_foreign_words.py`, 9, red first: 7 errors on the unfixed grader, `docs/organism/hears/records/tests/grade_foreign-red.log`): the type vote with the maker's words is still `shape`; a key of scene words only is what it was; a key of foreign words only has no world type and is skipped and counted; two types for one world type is still an error; a foreign word becomes the dax word and is counted; **the scored functions run on the adapted phrase** (`in_parent_order` reads «big arrow» as in order and «arrow big» as out of order, `unique_true` reads both as not TRUE) while they raise on the unadapted one; the dax stand-in is true of no shape.

## A4.5 · The reader's change: every bar of the grade whose pass flag differs
The same read stage showed a second instrument gap. «The six tests' verdicts» (`VERDICT_SOURCES` in `tools/brain/hears_read.py`) name seven of the fourteen bars the grade holds; the bench's first full reading flipped one that is not among them — **TRUTH-NEED** (SPEAK2; OFF 0.561, n 41, PASS → ON 0.389, n 36, FAIL; bar 0.5175) — and `hears.txt` said nothing: the flip was seen only because the two `report.txt` files were read side by side. The reader now prints, in P, EVERY bar of the grade (`grade.bars`, the scored sitting's) whose pass flag differs between the arms, with both values and both ns and the smallest passing gap over the SMALLER n; a pass of None is a reading, not a bar (None in both arms is no flip; None → a verdict is one); a bar present in one arm only, or a grade without bars, reads NOT COMPARABLE and says which, never a clean «none»; the count of bars read stands beside every «none». **It is a reading: no exit code moves, and C, X and I2 stay the only barred marks.**
Controls: 9 tests, 8 of them red first (8 errors on the reader as it was: `docs/organism/hears/records/tests/hears_read-flips-red.log`; the ninth, that an entry of `bars` with no pass flag is no bar, was added when the mutant that counted one survived); the reader's mutation sweep now has 35 mutants (the 25 of before and 10 new, one per way the new reading could go blind), **35 caught, no survivor** (`docs/organism/hears/records/tests/mutation_sweep-flips.log`); the rerun of the read stage on the same inputs differs from the earlier `hears.txt` by exactly ONE added line (the flips line), checked by `diff`.
