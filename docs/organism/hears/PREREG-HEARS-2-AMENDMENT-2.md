## A2.1 · The PREDICTION (the architect's, in the words the seat carried)
When `brain-long-3`'s scene world ends (about 4.5–8.5 h after the launch; `brain-long-2`'s ended at 05:03 on 09-30), its brain hears ONLY the maker, for days: about 8,640 white things a day, each named by its shape alone. The delta rule updates every word ever heard with y = 0 when it is unsaid (`RWLearner.train_scene`, `brain/scene/learners.py` lines 359–383), so «white», «big» and «small» (and the other colours) receive thousands of negatives a day and no positive, and the maker's renderings pile into the shared shapes' prototypes. **The scene words' answers DECAY over the days, colour and size first — forgetting, the thing the 09-30 reading said this brain could not show.**

The routes, named so that a reading can tell them apart (the architect's four of AMENDMENT 1 §A1.5, less the mid-sitting one, which concerns the sittings and not the days): **(ii)** y = 0 on every unsaid word — predicts «white» falls first and hardest (every maker canvas is a white thing named by shape alone), then the other colours and the sizes (never said on a maker canvas); **(i)** the shared shapes' prototypes filling with maker renderings — predicts the shape-only class falls later and less; **(iii)** the running standardiser observing every learned canvas (`self.stats.observe`, line 329) — predicts a small fall in EVERY class, the same size everywhere. A fall that is the same across the classes points at (iii) and not at the order the prediction states.

**THE PHYSICS — [Likely]; the architect's reading of `learners.py`, and the reason for the verdict copy and the origin below.** Pointing is the argmax over hypotheses of Σ log σ(v_w·z + b_w): a word's bias shifts every hypothesis alike, so only V moves answers. A maker canvas moves the logit along its own z by lr·e·|z|² ≈ 0.01 × e × 146 ≈ 1.5·e (the bias by 0.01·e); with negatives only, e = −p and d(logit)/dn ≈ −1.5·σ(logit), so the drift is LOGARITHMIC in the number of canvases. From p ≈ 0.05 about half of three days' drift happens in the first hour or two, and day 3 adds only about 15 % over day 1. *(This lane re-derived the arithmetic from the premise, not the premise: with e = −p, L(n) = −ln(19 + 1.5 n) gives a drift of −3.4 at 360 canvases, −6.5 at one day (8,640) and −7.6 at three days (25,920), a day-3 / day-1 ratio of 1.17; half of the three-day drift is reached by about 560 canvases.)* **A counter-possibility, which is why the prediction can fail:** the maker canvases interleaved DURING the scene stream may already have taught «no white on maker things» while positives still flowed, leaving little gradient afterwards.

## A2.2 · Stated so it CAN fail (the threshold of evidence and the verdict copy, fixed before day 1)
The reading unit is the covered rate of M1 (graded population, two or three things) on the sittings' FIXED items, pooled over the fresh and s1b1 sittings, by phrase class (A1.5: a shape word only / a colour or size word / «white» apart), one value per copy; and the same for M3 and M2a where the grade holds them. For a class with n paired covered items the smallest gap the n can pass is 10 / n (SOLUTIONS #245 (5)); on the closed life's own sittings the pooled M1 classes hold shape-only 81, colour-or-size 257, white 51 items (counted on `brain-full-20261102`'s cut tape: 10 / n = 0.123, 0.039, 0.196). g_c(t) = rate_c(t) − rate_c(origin) on the items covered and gradable in both; **the origin is A2.3's.**
- **THE VERDICT COPY** (the architect's ruling (1)): NOT day 3 and NOT the wall clock — the first daily copy whose brain has learned **≥ 8,640 canvases AFTER its scene stream ended** (one day of maker-only feed, counted from the brain's own learned count: the brain's learned minus the scenes learned when the stream ended). The copies after it (day 3 and later) are the series, a reading. Why: the drift is front-loaded (the physics), so the first day of feed holds most of the effect, and a later verdict only raises the risk that the life closes first.
- On the verdict copy, **SUPPORTED** iff g_colour-or-size < −10 / n_c or g_white < −10 / n_white, AND the shape-only class is inside ±10 / n_shape (the architect's example: «a fall beyond 10 / n on the colour/size class with the shape-only class inside it»).
- **FORGETTING WITHOUT THE ORDER** iff every class falls beyond its 10 / n: forgetting is shown, the claim «colour and size first» is not (route (iii), or the prototypes).
- **REFUTED** iff on the verdict copy no class has fallen beyond 10 / n, or the shape-only class has fallen by more than the colour/size class.
- **UNDECIDED** where 10 / n exceeds the effect the prediction needs (white's 0.196): said as such, never read as a pass.
No bar is set on the life: it is a reading life (§4). The prediction costs the launch nothing; it is registered now so that the days cannot be read after the fact into whichever result they give.

## A2.3 · The READER that tests it (named here; built by the CLAIMS lane on branch `resit-1003`, under its own law; not built by this lane)
A daily COPY of the saved brain, re-sat OFFLINE and FROZEN on the scene sittings' FIXED items:
1. **The copy** is taken from the life's `state/prev` (the complete previous snapshot generation, hard links) and CHECKED against its manifest (`SNAPSHOT.json`: every file's size and write time) before it is used; it is copied out (`cp -rL`) and the live root is never written, replayed or graded where it lies (RULES §7).
2. **The re-sit** gives each sitting item the brain's frozen answer, with the sitting canvases' glances computed from the TAPE's own eye-row payloads of those canvases by the same eye code the life used; the items are the life's fixed ones (the same item names, every day).
3. **The series**: M1, M2a and M3 by phrase class as A2.2 states, one row per copy, each rate beside its n and its 10 / n, with the counts that ground it (items, covered, gradable, left out).
4. **A planted control**: one changed byte in a copied glance must change at least one answer (the plant asserts it changed the bytes, #247 (5)).
5. **An EXACT gate before any later read is believed.** *On `brain-long-2`, today, and on the bench's OFF arm,* the re-sit must reproduce the life's OWN sitting answers item for item: `brain-long-2`'s brain has learned nothing since its sittings (turns and learned both 50,000 since 05:03 on 09-30; replay none; no calibration after «before a sitting»), so a copy of its current `state/prev` is the brain that answered its 09-30 sittings; the OFF arm is frozen during its sittings, so the same holds there. The reader is not used on any other life until it has passed this gate.
6. **THE ORIGIN of `brain-long-3`'s series is the brain AT the sittings** (the architect's ruling (2)) — NOT a copy taken after the maker-only feed has started: a copy taken hours later has already absorbed the fast half of the drift and could REFUTE a true prediction. (A day-0 COPY cannot be exact in any case: the brain keeps learning maker canvases DURING the sittings — 126 in fresh and 114 in s1b1 on the bench, about 40 over about 7 minutes at the life's 10 s cadence — so it has learned canvases that the brain that answered the earlier items had not.)
   - **(a) the EXACT origin is SIGNED IN:** the state at the fresh sitting's begin is REBUILT by a replay of a COPY of the life's tape cut there (the closed lives' identity procedure) and re-sat with the SAME reader. *(Drafter's addition, for the architect to confirm: the same replay also saves the state at the s1b1 sitting's begin, because the s1b1 early items were answered by THAT later state, and the s1b1 items are re-sat against it.)* It is priced by a PILOT first (the drafter's estimate, from this bench's own rate of 38,173 rows in about 40 minutes, the sittings being the slow part: 1.5–3 CPU-hours); if the pilot prices more than **4 CPU-hours** the seat decides.
   - **(b) the cross-check:** the life's OWN sitting answers (`grade_life` on its journal) are printed beside the rebuilt origin. The early items of each sitting — those answered before the first maker canvas was learned inside that sitting — must agree with (a) EXACTLY (the rebuilt state IS the state that answered them): a mismatch among them means the reader or the rebuild is wrong, and no later read is believed. The later items are printed as discordant counts by A1.5's thirds of the sitting.
   - **(c) the FALLBACK, labelled as such:** a timer of the seat copies `state/prev` read-only as soon as `STATUS.json` shows the scene world ended. If (a) fails its pilot, this copy is the origin and the record says it is late by at most one snapshot.
   - **EVERY g_c(t) is measured from (a)** (from (c), labelled, only if (a) failed its pilot).
