## A2.1 · The PREDICTION (the architect's, in the words the seat carried)
When `brain-long-3`'s scene world ends (about 4.5–8.5 h after the launch; `brain-long-2`'s ended at 05:03 on 09-30), its brain hears ONLY the maker, for days: about 8,640 white things a day, each named by its shape alone. The delta rule updates every word ever heard with y = 0 when it is unsaid (`RWLearner.train_scene`, `brain/scene/learners.py` lines 359–383), so «white», «big» and «small» (and the other colours) receive thousands of negatives a day and no positive, and the maker's renderings pile into the shared shapes' prototypes. **The scene words' answers DECAY over the days, colour and size first — forgetting, the thing the 09-30 reading said this brain could not show.**

The routes, named so that a reading can tell them apart (they are the architect's four of AMENDMENT 1 §A1.5, less the mid-sitting one, which concerns the sittings and not the days): **(ii)** y = 0 on every unsaid word — predicts «white» falls first and hardest (every maker canvas is a white thing named by shape alone), then the other colours and the sizes (never said on a maker canvas); **(i)** the shared shapes' prototypes filling with maker renderings — predicts the shape-only class falls later and less; **(iii)** the running standardiser observing every learned canvas (`self.stats.observe`, line 329) — predicts a small fall in EVERY class, the same size everywhere. A fall that is the same across the classes points at (iii) and not at the order the prediction states.

## A2.2 · Stated so it CAN fail (the threshold of evidence, fixed before day 1)
The reading unit is the covered rate of M1 (graded population, two or three things) on the sittings' FIXED items, pooled over the fresh and s1b1 sittings, by phrase class (A1.5: a shape word only / a colour or size word / «white» apart), one value per daily copy; and the same for M3 and M2a where the grade holds them. For a class with n paired covered items the smallest gap the n can pass is 10 / n (SOLUTIONS #245 (5)); on the closed life's own sittings the pooled M1 classes hold shape-only 81, colour-or-size 257, white 51 items (counted on `brain-full-20261102`'s cut tape: 10 / n = 0.123, 0.039, 0.196). The day-0 copy is the origin; g_c(t) = rate_c(t) − rate_c(0) on the items covered and gradable in both.
- **SUPPORTED on the day-t copy** iff g_colour-or-size(t) < −10 / n_c or g_white(t) < −10 / n_white, AND the shape-only class is inside ±10 / n_shape (the architect's example: «a fall beyond 10 / n on the colour/size class with the shape-only class inside it»).
- **FORGETTING WITHOUT THE ORDER** iff every class falls beyond its 10 / n: forgetting is shown, the claim «colour and size first» is not (route (iii), or the prototypes).
- **REFUTED at day 3** (the copy taken three days after the first maker-only day; ≈ 26,000 maker canvases; *the day is the drafter's proposal, for the architect to confirm or replace before signing*) iff no class has fallen beyond 10 / n, or the shape-only class has fallen by more than the colour/size class. The series continues to the end of the life and is read as a series either way; day 3 is where the prediction is judged.
- **UNDECIDED** where 10 / n exceeds the effect the prediction needs (white's 0.196): said as such, never read as a pass.
No bar is set on the life: it is a reading life (§4). The prediction costs the launch nothing; it is registered now so that the days cannot be read after the fact into whichever result they give.

## A2.3 · The READER that tests it (named here; built by the CLAIMS lane on branch `resit-1003`, under its own law; not built by this lane)
A daily COPY of the saved brain, re-sat OFFLINE and FROZEN on the scene sittings' FIXED items:
1. **The copy** is taken from the life's `state/prev` (the complete previous snapshot generation, hard links) and CHECKED against its manifest (`SNAPSHOT.json`: every file's size and write time) before it is used; it is copied out (`cp -rL`) and the live root is never written, replayed or graded where it lies (RULES §7).
2. **The re-sit** gives each sitting item the brain's frozen answer, with the sitting canvases' glances computed from the TAPE's own eye-row payloads of those canvases by the same eye code the life used; the items are the life's fixed ones (the same item names, every day).
3. **The series**: M1, M2a and M3 by phrase class as A2.2 states, one row per daily copy, each rate beside its n and its 10 / n, with the counts that ground it (items, covered, gradable, left out).
4. **A planted control**: one changed byte in a copied glance must change at least one answer (the plant asserts it changed the bytes, #247 (5)).
5. **A day-0 GATE before any later read is believed.** *On `brain-long-2`, today, and on the bench's OFF arm,* the gate is EXACT: `brain-long-2`'s brain has learned nothing since its sittings (turns and learned both 50,000 since 05:03 on 09-30; replay none; no calibration after «before a sitting»), so a copy of its current `state/prev` is the brain that answered its 09-30 sittings, and the re-sit must reproduce the life's OWN sitting answers item for item (the OFF arm is frozen during its sittings, so the same holds there). The reader is not used on any other life until it has passed this gate.
6. **On `brain-long-3` the day-0 copy CANNOT be exact, and no exactness gate is written for it.** Its brain keeps learning maker canvases DURING the sittings (126 in fresh and 114 in s1b1 on the bench; at the life's 10 s cadence about 40 over about 7 minutes), so no single frozen state gave all of its sitting answers, and the copy taken after the sittings has learned canvases that the brain that answered the earlier items had not. *(The architect's design note read «items answered before the first maker canvas inside each sitting must match EXACTLY». They cannot be promised to: those items were answered by the state before the first maker canvas, the copy is the state after all of them, and a borderline item can differ for that reason alone. The drafter has therefore written the day-0 comparison as a PRINTED reading and not a gate; the architect may replace it with a rebuilt state, below.)* The reading on `brain-long-3`: the day-0 re-sit is compared item by item with the life's own sitting answers and the discordant items are printed by A1.5's thirds of the sitting and by their position against the first maker canvas inside it; the series' origin is that day-0 re-sit; the reader's correctness on `brain-long-3` is carried by the gate passed on `brain-long-2` and the OFF arm (the same code) and by the planted control on every read. *Open choice for the architect:* an EXACT day-0 for `brain-long-3` is possible by REBUILDING the state at each sitting's `begin` from the life's own tape (a replay cut at that seq, as the closed lives' identity replays are), at the price of a replay of about 5 hours of tape (an ESTIMATE from this bench's own rate, 38,173 rows in about 40 minutes with the sittings the slow part: 1.5–3 CPU-hours, to be priced by a pilot before it is signed in); the amendment takes no such price unless it is signed in.
