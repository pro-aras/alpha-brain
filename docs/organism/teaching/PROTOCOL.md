## 0 · What teaching is, in this house

Teaching is a parent talking to a child: a few honest statements, spaced over days, into a door the organism
owns. It is NEVER flooding a channel, NEVER editing the life in place, and NEVER graded on what the organism
echoes back in the same breath (an echo is not learning; a retention read after sleep is). The chat door
cannot teach by force («a question never teaches» — the genome line's own words); teaching is OFFERED,
the organism's machinery does the learning. Every round lands in the life's journal and in this ledger.
**The anti-dependency clause (his 10-12 word):** the rounds are PROBES of the conversation channel and the
parent's naming acts — never the child's required diet. The organism feeds itself from every armed world
and door around the clock (its web grew from zero to 22,434 nodes in its first 8 h with zero teaching);
its advancement never waits on a teacher, and no round is ever a prerequisite for any developmental step.

The doors (all genome-declared, all journaled):
- `chat/in` / `chat/out` — the conversation channel (statements and questions; the organism may answer or
  abstain; an honest «novel» abstention is a HEALTHY sign, never a failure).
- `ask/graded` — the seat's grades, entering as tape rows through the grade sense (mentorship enters through
  the tape, so a replay grades the same).
- The lexicon door — the organism's own what-is asks answered by the gloss (it teaches itself by asking).

## 1 · The stages (content follows the child ladder, never ahead of it)

| Stage | When (the life's own development, not a clock) | Content of a round | The read (pre-registered below) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A · naming** | from birth | One declarative arc about its world or itself: «alpha is happy»; «the star is red»; «you are alpha». Words it SEES every day + its name. Probe words (chat-only, §3) to measure the channel itself. | Sleep-boundary web read + 24-h retention probe (§4) |
| **B · relations** | after stage A words ground (web w:-nodes with edges for ≥ 10 taught/world words) | Two-word relational statements in BOTH orders only when ORDER-BIND has PASSed; before that, single-position relations («big star» only, never shuffled by the teacher). Corrections: «no, that is small» after a wrong say. | Grounded-edge reads + the correction read (does the next say change?) |
| **C · conversation** | after MENTOR-STREAM and unasked two-word says exist | Back-and-forth: a question asked OF it («what is red»), its answer read, a follow-up. At most two exchanges per round (L28's form). | The conversation read: its answer ≠ abstention AND true ≥ the law's bar |
| **D · why** | after conversation is stable | «why» answered by showing causes; its own «why» questions answered honestly or declared unknown. | The why-read (L26/L27 forms) |

A stage is never skipped; a stage's read failing means the stage REPEATS with new content, never that the
bar moves (the standing rule: bars never loosen).

## 2 · The spacing rule (his word, declared)

- At most **6 rounds a day**, at least **45 minutes apart** («a few spaced rounds a day, never flood the
  door» — his 10-09/10-10 cadence, kept).
- A round is at most **6 short statements** (the chat line's own «at most 6 words» per statement), one arc
  per round. The same statement repeated within a round (the «5th rep» form of 10-11) is declared on the
  round's line.
- A round fires only when the life is healthy (crash null, backlog not rising, no HOLD on the box).
- Night rounds (00:00–08:00 +0400) only for the sleep-boundary read spacing, never to catch up a missed day.

## 3 · The contamination check (every probe word, no exceptions)

A PROBE word (a chat-only word invented to measure the grounding channel, the «mipplo» class) is grepped
ABSENT from ALL of: the video shelf, the read shelf's curriculum, the lexicon door's gloss reach, and the
life's own prior chat ledger — the three-command form printed in the round's ledger line (the 10-12
precedent: «sisu» was caught in the lexicon and the fallback «mipplo» declared, never silently swapped).
World words (red, star, …) are NOT probes — they are MEANT to live in the worlds; they are declared as
world words in the ledger and skip the check by name.

## 4 · The reads (pre-registered per round)

Every round's ledger line carries, BEFORE the round fires: the word(s), the arc, and the read form.
The reads:
- **R1 · the grounding read** — at the FIRST sleep boundary after the round: the taught probe word's
  `w:`-node in `state/web.json` (presence, edge count; both rules L+N). Absence after 24 h = the channel
  did not carry it — reported, never re-graded.
- **R2 · the retention read** — 24 h later, the organism's own answer form: a what-is through the chat door
  or the exam's form. An abstention is a reading (honest), never a failure to hide.
- **R3 · the correction read** (stage B+) — after a correction round: the organism's next say about the
  corrected thing changes or does not (L14's form).
- **R4 · the use read** (stage C+) — the taught word appears in an UNASKED say within 7 days.

Reads are printed with their numbers into the ledger. A read that cannot run (life closed, door changed)
is marked NOT RUN with its reason.

## 5 · The ledger

Every round: one appended line in `docs/organism/teaching/LEDGER-<life>.md` — id, date (from `date`),
stage, words, arc, the round's files (`chat/in/<id>.json`), the pre-registered reads and their outcomes,
the contamination print for probes. The ledger is committed with the life's records.

## 6 · The adversarial walk (the standing form)

1. «The teacher can leak the test» → probes are chat-only words absent from every world (§3); world words
   are declared, never counted as taught-by-chat.
2. «Echoes pass for learning» → the reads are post-sleep and 24-h, never same-breath (§4).
3. «The cadence floods the door» → the spacing rule (§2) with the 45-min floor and the 6-round cap.
4. «A failed round gets re-taught until it looks passed» → a FAIL is reported; the stage repeats with NEW
   content (§1); the bar never moves.
5. «The teacher improves the instrument mid-life» → the doors are genome lines; this protocol never edits
   a living life's code or root; content only, through the declared doors.
6. «Contamination drifts in later through new shelves» → a new shelf/curriculum re-runs the check for every
   ACTIVE probe word before promotion.
