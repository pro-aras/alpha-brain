## 1 · The rules of the ladder

1. **A rung is a thing a child does at an age**, in the child's own words where a source gives them. The ages are the CDC's «Learn the Signs. Act Early.» milestones (opened today) and the infant studies named on each line.
2. **A rung is graded only in a world that can produce it** (SOLUTIONS #228 a). A rung with no such world reads NO WORLD. It is never graded as a failure, and never skipped in silence.
3. **Every rung names its organ, its mark, its ceiling and its count.** A ceiling not yet measured reads «to measure first».
4. **An age here is an order, not a clock.** The organism is not bounded by time (his 09-24 word). A rung is «reached» when its mark passes at every seed. The order of the rungs is the child's.
5. **A rung once reached is re-read at every later life.** A rung that falls is reported as lost.
6. **Self-driven rungs are graded on what the organism does UNASKED.** An answer given to an exam is a different rung from the same thing done by itself.

## 3 · Where the organism stands as a child

- **Reached:** L0, L4, L7, L10, L15, L18 (two seeds), L20, L21 when asked, L22 half, L23 on the bench, L24, L27 half. That is a child who understands a few words when tested.
- **The gap is one kind of rung: the unasked ones.** L13 and L16 are what a one-year-old does by itself, and the brain does neither. That is his statement «It needs some one to do something with it», found on the ladder.
- **Three rungs have no world:** L3 (things that move), L8 (a stream to cut), L9 (things that hide). A world is staged, never an organ (his 09-21 word). Each needs its own world before it can be graded.
- **The order of the build follows the ladder:** L13 and L16 today; L11, L12 and L17 next; L19 after.

## 4 · What this ladder does not claim

- An age on a rung is the child's, not the organism's. Passing L20 does not make it «a two-year-old». A child of two also walks, plays and knows faces, and no world here affords those.
- A rung reached in the scene world is reached in a world of 14 words. The same rung is owed again in every wider world.
- The CDC's milestones are what 75 % of children do by that age [from memory of the 2022 revision, not opened today]. They are a screening list, not a theory of development.
