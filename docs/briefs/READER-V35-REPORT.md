### The identity mark's design — three traps the literal PREREG reading would hit, each found on the real pre-flight records before the mark was written
The prototype ran on copies of the CLOSED 20-min pre-flights (a4l2-pre-floor, a4l2-pre-cost, a4l-pre-cost, a4l2-pre-act, a4l2-pre-nursery):
1. **Meeting points are keyed on EVERY eye source (hands and lexicon included), not on the four world streams.** a4l2-pre-cost journals hands@1575 → folder@1580; a4l-pre-cost journals folder@1577 → hands@1578. Keyed on {maker, game, shelf, folder} alone the two folder rows are «the same» meeting point and read cells 725 vs 724 — a false disagreement made by the key.
2. **Only a canvas row that carries its OWN `cells` is a reading.** A hands canvas (and a life's first folder canvas) journals neither `births` nor `cells`; a reading carried forward over it lags that canvas's own births (the carry-forward gave the one false hit on each pre-flight pair).
3. **Counts, never ids.** With 1–2 fixed, a4l2-pre-cost vs a4l-pre-cost still disagreed at 7 meeting points — every one in the next-id counter only (784 vs 783) with births, cells and sleeps equal: a hands canvas births a cell with no journal row, and the two lives give the same births different ids by interleaving. The id counter is printed, never graded.
And the journal's births are a LOWER bound of STATUS's (a4l-cost: journaled sight 7,856 + sleep 448 = 8,304 vs STATUS sight_born 8,360 — the hands canvases' births) — the same instrument on both lives, said on the mark; STATUS's exact sight_born/births/cells/sleeps are printed as the wall-clock readings. On the pre-flights, the finished mark reads: **a4l2-pre-floor vs a4l2-pre-cost PASS** (598 meeting points, 0 disagree); **a4l2-pre-cost vs a4l-pre-cost PASS** (600, 0; the id order differs at 7, printed); **a4l2-pre-floor vs a4l2-pre-act FAIL** (the game stream diverges at canvas 8 — the act's value chose another act; 444 of 579 meeting points disagree after it) and **a4l2-pre-floor vs a4l2-pre-nursery FAIL** (the shelf stream diverges at canvas 0, the maker's at 148 — the world answers the minds' questions by showing) — the two negative controls the ladder itself provides.
