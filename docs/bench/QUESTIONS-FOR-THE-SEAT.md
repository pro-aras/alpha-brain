**Q1 · TOLD or SHOWN — what may the teacher do, given his 09-16 18:46 explorer rule («nobody answers his questions for him»), his 16:56 pure road, and tonight's «parents teach … explore then teach then correct then test»?**
*Provisional:* the teacher only SHOWS. TEACH = an instant that carries a picture (or a sound, or a line) together with its name — the maker's instant, scheduled by the curriculum instead of the rng. ASK = a practice question through the ask door (answered from a snapshot, the learner unchanged). CORRECT = the missed item SHOWN AGAIN with its right name soon after the miss — a targeted teaching instant, which `genome/machinery.py:_delta` already consumes as an error-correcting update (edges from the lit sight cells toward the heard word, away from every other word). A human grade stays a DATA POINT on the tape («graded by <who>»), never a weight. The learner's OWN questions are still answered by the world by showing (maker, lexicon, web) — never by a person. Words that explain («a lion is a big cat») enter only at the language stage and only as lines the learner reads or hears, never as a rule written into it. His two phrasings («explore → teach → correct → test» and «teaching, asking, correcting, then testing») are read as ONE cycle: EXPLORE → TEACH → ASK → CORRECT → TEST, where the TEST's items are never corrected (a corrected item is a taught item) and never reused as held-out.
*What turns on it:* the teacher module's whole action vocabulary (SHOW, NAME, ASK, CORRECT-by-showing, REWARD for the game) and whether «cannot be corrected» is graded by a measured effect — the missed items split at random into corrected / re-shown-without-the-name / left alone, then re-asked after a delay: correction effect = right(corrected) − right(re-shown), exposure effect = right(re-shown) − right(left alone).

**Q2 · What may the program ever say about the learner — and about «the true AI»?**
*Provisional:* the Bench never prints «true AI», «understands» or «AGI». It prints «cleared N of M declared stages of Bench vX (stage list dated D)», and for each stage which of its bars passed, each with its ceiling, its null twin and its control. «The true AI», in the plan's §1, becomes an operational statement: a learner that clears EVERY stage of the ladder — whose top stages are held-out (families never shown during any development), open-ended (items generated after the learner is frozen) and adversarial (lures, noise, occlusion, never-seen things) — while alive, replayable and keeping up with its world. The claim is always about THIS ladder at THIS version; a stronger claim needs a stronger ladder, never a louder sentence. About itself the program says only: its version, the learner's version, the tape's hash, its self-test status (the failure zoo, Q-note below) and, for any number it cannot back, «not measured» / «not gradable: <reason>».
*What turns on it:* whether §1 can be gradable at all, and the top stages' definition.

**Q3 · Does the Bench OWN the world (it writes every stimulus onto the learner's tape) or does it WATCH lives launched by today's tooling?**
*Provisional:* it owns the world. Teaching and correcting both need a road to SHOW, and a bar's ceiling can only be computed at declaration if the stage's showing schedule is the Bench's own. For the genome creature the road is a new sense — `SENSE bench …` — that publishes the Bench's scheduled instants through the EXISTING tape law (every delivery on the tape before the mind sees it; the mind the only writer of memory; replay identity), exactly as the folder and chat doors do today; the maker's rng schedule becomes the curriculum's schedule. The truth stays in the Bench's own directory, which the learner never opens. Today's lives, ladders and waiters keep running under today's tooling untouched; nothing running is migrated.
*What turns on it:* the creature's adapter (one new sense line = one genome difference, so the first Bench life is itself a ladder rung against a FLOOR), and whether ALPHA4's own STAGE/READINESS nursery is superseded by the Bench's curriculum.

**Q4 · The world's clock: does the world WAIT for a slow learner?**
*Provisional:* no. The real world does not wait; the Bench's world publishes at the stage's declared cadence, and «fell behind its world» (the backlog series) is a graded failure, never hidden. A stage may declare a slower cadence (declared before the run, with its reason). A backlog-throttled clock («publish the next row when the mind has consumed the last») exists only for the dry-run on toy learners and for replay twins — never for a graded life, because it would hide the cost failure that alpha4-1 has shown since 16:27.
*What turns on it:* whether cost and lag are failure modes the Bench grades, and how long a stage takes in wall-clock hours.

**Q5 · «Harder» — who authors the harder stages, and may the program generate them itself?**
*Provisional:* the ladder is written by a person (the seat, pre-registered, dated), built on the TWO ladders the estate already has — the worlds (drawn things → photographs → the shelf's text → the game → the hands → the world's voice → the web) and the exam bank's grade levels (K → G1-G3 → G4-G8 → G9-G12 → College → University, 33 families with pass marks written before any sitting). Inside a stage, difficulty KNOBS move by a pre-registered staircase once the current setting is cleared (more things, new render styles, scale, placement, rotation, noise, occlusion, composites, distractors, longer delay before the re-test), each setting's ceiling computed before it is used. The program generates ITEMS (seeded, procedural) but never invents a FAMILY or moves a BAR; a new family is a dated pre-registration by a person.
*What turns on it:* the curriculum engine's authority, and whether his «extreme and different» can be met without the Bench grading its own inventions (#158).

**Q6 · A bar found above its ceiling AT DECLARATION — refuse, or run and report?**
*Provisional:* refuse. The declaration validator computes each bar's ceiling under the stage's own showing schedule and item counts (#228 (b), #237 (b)/(c): e.g. a «settled by the 10th showing» bar on a thing shown five times; a p95 band under all()), its chance and null, and REFUSES to arm the stage when bar > ceiling or bar ≤ null; the fix is the test's design (the negative, the window, the item count, the showing schedule), never loosening the bar after a reading; the refusal and its arithmetic are on the record. The seat re-declares; the program never edits a bar.
*What turns on it:* whether stages can be armed unattended (e.g. the staircase at 03:00) — only settings pre-validated at declaration can be.

**Q7 · «Pre-training, like parents teach the kids» — in-life nursery, or offline training of organs?**
*Provisional:* in-life only. The organs a learner is BORN with (the sealed eye tower, the cochlear front) are part of the learner as delivered — declared, sha-pinned, never trained by the Bench. «Parents' pre-training» = the Bench's first stages: a small set of named things shown in spaced repetition, the nursery. A Bench that trains a learner's organs offline would grade its own training set (#158). Training a new organ is a separate sealed build outside the Bench, entered as a new learner version.
*What turns on it:* stage 0/1's content, and the line between «the learner» and «the Bench» in the adapter.

**Q8 · A learner that cannot be replayed or snapshotted (an external model): which marks stand?**
*Provisional:* the adapter DECLARES its capabilities — `{senses, acts, tape_in, snapshot, deterministic_replay, abstain_form, learns_online}` — and every mark names the capabilities it needs; a mark the adapter cannot serve reads «not gradable: the adapter lacks <X>», never PASS or FAIL. Whether non-determinism is itself a FAIL for a drop-in model (the estate's REPLAY law says a mind that cannot replay cannot be audited) goes to §9 as his decision, recommended: reported as its own line, not a FAIL of the stage.
*What turns on it:* what phase 3 can grade an outside model on besides its answers.

**Q9 · Outside models: which ones, over what road, and how are they stopped from cheating?**
*Provisional:* local open-weights models only, run on this box behind the adapter in a jail with no network and no read access to the Bench's truth directory. A remote API model cannot be sandboxed or replayed and would receive our held-out items. Anti-cheat laws: test items generated AFTER the learner is frozen, with seeds drawn at test time and recorded; learning measured on INVENTED material (never-seen symbols and nonce words like «zog», «vex») that no pretraining corpus contains; CANARY items whose answer exists only in the truth file (a score above chance on canaries = the key leaked); a PRE-TEST before any teaching measures what the learner already knows, and learning is the gain over its own pre-test. An outside model is a SUBJECT, never a mouth for the creature (the pure road).
*What turns on it:* phase 3's scope, the jail, and whether the Bench's grades can be compared across learner kinds at all.

**Q11 · A learner dies at 03:00 — what does he see, and is anyone woken?**
