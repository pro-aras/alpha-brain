## 2 · The body — the contracts (Builder A implements; the mind's law is Claude's)
**2.1 The tape.** `run/tape/<YYYY-MM-DD>.jsonl`, one row per sense delivery, seq strictly increasing across days:
`{"seq": int, "at": "<UTC ISO, ms>", "sense": "eye"|"ear"|"door", "sha": "<sha256 hex of the payload bytes>", "payload": "payload/<seq:09d>.<png|txt>", "meta": {"moment": "<id>", "source": "maker"|"folder"|"web", "word": "<str, ear rows only>"}}`
The payload bytes live at `run/tape/<payload>`. **Nothing that grades him is on the tape**: the world-maker writes the truth (the thing's cell, the thing's name for eye rows) to `run/truth/<seq:09d>.json`, a road the mind never opens.
**2.2 Order law.** For one moment the EAR row precedes the EYE row (same `meta.moment`). An eye row whose moment has no ear row is a sight-only moment (skeleton: counted `sight_only_skipped` and journaled; lived once `Organism.moment_sight` lands — §3 B2).
**2.3 The mind.** `creature.mind.Mind(genome_path, run_root, blind=frozenset())`; `consume(row) -> None`. Determinism law: the mind's state and journal are a pure function of (genome text, the tape rows in seq order). Wall clock enters ONLY through `row["at"]`; randomness only from `numpy.random.default_rng(genome.seed)` advanced by the rows; **no call to `time`, `datetime.now`, `os.urandom`, `random` in `creature/mind.py`** (a sweep test pins it). For an eye row: decode → `genome.gaze.glances(rgb, genome.retina, genome.gaze, mode, pic_id=row["sha"])` → for each glance `org.moment(x, encode_word(word))` — **the same feed as `genome/run_g1.py`'s `live()`**, so the organism's law is untouched by the body.
**2.4 The journal.** `run/journal/<YYYY-MM-DD>.jsonl`; the mind writes one row per consumed eye row: `{"seq", "moment", "word", "fix": [[y,x],…], "births": [[area, cell, reason],…], "cells": [n_sight, n_words], "edges": [born, pruned], "hash": "<state_hash>"}`; the body writes event rows `{"seq": null, "event": "sense_restart"|"start"|"stop"|"snapshot"|"page", …}`. The replay compares MIND rows only (event rows carry wall-clock facts).
**2.5 Liveness.** `run/STATUS.json` written every 10 s by the body on EVERY branch (#164): `{"heartbeat_at", "started_at", "moments", "tape_rows", "journal_rows", "births", "edges_born", "edges_pruned", "cells", "sight_only_skipped", "sense_restarts", "snapshots", "last_seq", "mind_ms_median_1min", "rss_mb", "crash": null|{…}}`. `tools/creature_watch.py` (a user timer, 5 min) reads two STATUS readings an hour apart and PAGES (a row in `run/pages.jsonl` + `STATUS.page`) when `moments` or `tape_rows` did not move — a unit reading `active` is not liveness (#179, #230).
**2.6 Crash law.** A sense thread that throws: journal `sense_restart`, restart after 1 s, the mind unaffected. A mind exception: STATUS.crash set, a page written, the process EXITS non-zero; `Restart=on-failure` restarts it, and the mind resumes from its last snapshot + the tape after that snapshot's seq — **a cursor advances only after the row it names is written** (#230). The plant: `CREATURE_PLANT_EXCEPTION=eye:100` throws in the eye thread at its 100th publish.
**2.7 Snapshot + the voice.** Every 500 moments the mind writes `run/state/organism.npz` (`Organism.save`) + `run/state/ledger.json` (moment→word map, last seq) atomically (write `.tmp`, rename). The voice (the ask door) is a thread that LOADS the latest snapshot and never writes memory: inbox `run/ask/in/<id>.json` `{"q": "what-is"|"show"|"is"|"which", "pic": "<path>", "word": …}` → `run/ask/out/<id>.json` with the answer as `genome/ask.py` computes it today; `python3 -m creature.ask what-is <pic>` writes the inbox row and prints the answer. A question never changes him.
**2.8 The world-maker** (`creature/world_maker.py`, a sense thread): a pure schedule of (SEED, window): windows of 6 h; window 0 shows `world.THINGS_11`, window 1 shows `THINGS_ARRIVAL2` + 3 of THINGS_11 chosen by the rng, alternating; every 5 s one thing (rng) is rendered by `world.render_canvas(thing, rng)` with rng = `default_rng([SEED, window, i])` — **never seed 2** (G1's held-out seed) — and published as ear then eye; the plant `THING_PLANT` is shown 5 times in the hour after moment 15,000 of the life; `THING_NULL` and `THING_NEVER` are never shown. The exam asserts disjointness by pixels (`world.assert_disjoint_canvas`) before scoring.
**2.9 Replay.** `python3 -m creature.replay --genome G --tape run/tape/<date>.jsonl [--against run/journal/<date>.jsonl] [--blind gaze] --out DIR` rebuilds the mind from the tape and writes DIR/journal.jsonl; with `--against`, exit 0 iff the mind rows are byte-identical, else exit 1 naming the first differing seq. `--blind gaze` runs the random-gaze twin (mode random, seeded by pic_id — G1's T-random-gaze); later blinds (`sleep`, `predict`, `curiosity`, `park`, `frozen`) are passed to the Mind as its genes land.
**2.10 The unit.** `deploy/creature.service` (user): `ExecStart=python3 -m creature.body --root /data/dev/genome/run --genome /data/dev/genome/genome/G4-skeleton.genome`, `Restart=on-failure`, `RestartSec=5`, `WorkingDirectory=/data/dev/genome`; `deploy/creature-watch.service` + `.timer` (5 min). `deploy/install.sh` copies and daemon-reloads; it never enables or starts — Claude starts, after the gates.

### 11.1 The mind's steps per canvas (Builder A, `creature/mind.py`; all deterministic over the tape; every step gated on `"GENE" in genome.declared`)
1. **PREDICT (before the glances):** `x_pred = predictor.expect()`; after the FIRST glance feature `f0`: `err = predictor.error(f0)`, `lr_scale = predictor.modulate(1.0)`; every glance of this canvas runs `org.moment(x, xw, lr_scale=lr_scale)`; the mind row gains `"err": round(err, 4)` and `"lr": round(lr_scale, 3)`. Blind `predict` → err 0.0, lr 1.0 (still journaled). The predictor's state (its last winner) lives in the mind and is saved in the snapshot.
2. **FAST (after the glances):** one episode per canvas: `episodes.write(moment=org.t at f0, x_s=f0, x_w=xw, word, surprise=err)`; the row gains `"ep": id`. Sight-only canvases write an episode with word `""` and are no longer skipped: they run `org.moment_sight(x)` per glance (B2) and journal `"word": null`. Episodes are saved with the snapshot (`state/episodes.npz`) and loaded on resume.
3. **THINK (every THINK.every canvases, a mind step between canvases; declared as `THINK every <n> canvases` — a new gene B parses):** the POOL = (a) the words of the last 200 episodes whose `recall` on their own episode abstains (a word he cannot yet answer), (b) episodes with word `""` (a sight with no word), (c) predictions filed and due. CURIOSITY chooses ONE item (`curiosity.choose(pool, rng)`; blind `curiosity` → the rng's uniform pick; no CURIOSITY declared → the first item). The item is WORKED: (a) → `why(...)` + `recall` again on its latest episode: answered → a belief row (`think.believe`), else dead end `no_cell_answers`; (b) → nearest word by FAST's `nearest` over episodes with a word: an edge suggestion filed as a PREDICTION via `FILE` (claim: the next showing of this sight resonates that word's cells; route: the journal's `err`/recall on the next canvas with that word; decider: recall == word; due: the next showing) — or dead end `no_route`; (c) → `predict.score`. Every dead end goes to `park.answer(want, reason)`; a parked want leaves the pool until `park.reopen` (a NEW word or a folder picture naming it).
