## 1 · §3 / §4 PLANT-FOUND: what gates a rule's gradability
**The fault (the architect's ruling of 2026-10-03 17:0x, on my RAISE).** The base text reads «each such set's G > 0» (§4) and «its G must be positive» (§3) on the rule's WHOLE set. That quantity is the GAIN reading plus the planted frame's saving, so the junk frames' model cost (2 log₂ V = 22.26 bits a frame on ALPHA4) sits in both: as a gate it is not blind to exactly one thing (#228, its fifth gate), and it turns a rule whose frames do not pay into NOT GRADABLE instead of a failed GAIN — it would hide a negative result.

**The rule (replaces those two sentences; GATE FIRST's consequence — a rule that fails the PLANT gate is NOT GRADABLE — stands, per stream):** on each PLANT stream (ALPHA4+PLANT, SCENE+PLANT) a rule passes the PLANT gate iff all three hold, F* = (zzq, qxk) being the planted frame (§2):
- **(i) it keeps F*.** WEB-S at ≥ 9 of its ten seeds, a seed counted only if (i) and (C) both hold at that seed (B does not depend on the seed).
- **(B) the compressor credits the frame alone:** G({F*}) > 0 on that stream — one number a stream, the same for every rule: it tests the compressor and the stream, not the rule.
- **(C) the frame's marginal credit inside the rule's own set is positive:** G(F) − G(F ∖ {F*}) > 0. The junk cancels in the difference.

Printed beside (B) and (C), per stream: the held-out plant count and the train plant count; V; the architect's first-order expected value **n_held_plants × log₂(N_train / n_train_plants) − 2 log₂ V** with its inputs counted from the stream (N_train = the train tokens under the empty set, END included); the measured G({F*}); and its decomposition (§1.3). **(A), the whole-set G on the plant stream, is printed as a READING beside them and gates nothing.** GATE FIRST is per stream: ALPHA4's GAIN, NULL-1 and NULL-2 readings are gated by ALPHA4+PLANT and ALPHA4+SHUFFLE; SCENE's readings by SCENE+PLANT.

**1.1 This cannot loosen GAIN.** GAIN's bar (G(F) > q95 of its dealt null AND G(F) > 0; WEB-S judged by the seeds that pass, ≥ 8 of 10) is untouched. The change moves a rule from the label «NOT GRADABLE» (which says the instrument was blind) to a graded GAIN result: a rule whose frames do not pay now FAILS GAIN where it would have been silenced.

NULL-1's k replacements are distinct (so |F′| = k, «same size»), a′ may equal a, a′ may equal b′; a bin without an admissible replacement falls back to the nearest non-empty bin by index distance (the two sides pooled at equal distance), and the fallbacks are counted and printed per rule (NULL-1 and NULL-2). q95 = `numpy.percentile(x, 95)` (linear), sd with ddof = 1, z = (G − mean)/sd (none when sd = 0).
- **(c) MDL-GREEDY.** Its candidates are counted once on the raw train (adjacent bigrams, count ≥ 2); a step's pick is the candidate within 1e-9 bits of the best delta with the smallest pair name; it stops when no candidate has delta > 0.

SENTENCE's stand-in store has cap = n_written (no ring eviction) and id = the moment's index; `find_frames` counts EVERY ordered pair a < b of a moment's distinct words, not only adjacent ones (frames never adjacent are kept as the cells would).
- **(e) Counting WEB-S.** SHUFFLE-QUIET holds for WEB-S when ≥ 8 of its ten seeds are quiet (the count GAIN uses); the law says nothing for the other bar.
- **(f) G1's χ².** `scipy.stats.chisquare` on the template by length (the one «dax» moment excluded and named), the shape, colour and size marginals and the uniformity of the 24 pairs, each p > 0.001 or G_max is printed and not gated.
