## 0 · What this is, and what was read BEFORE signing (disclosure)
Schmidhuber's «keep what compresses», his own §3.3 («the digits of π look random to any simple statistical learner»), and Ahmad's notes (frames with slots, grown and pruned) meet a plain fact: the organism has THREE ways of calling a pair of words a «frame», and no measurement of whether any of them is a frame in the compression sense.
- **The cells' SENTENCE rule** (`genome/sentence.py`, `find_frames`): an ORDERED pair that met in one instant (a moment's words) in ≥ `min_count` = 5 episodes AND whose count exceeds `factor` = 2 × its count under independence.
- **The knowledge web** (`genome/web.py`, KW-1, `docs/brain/memory/kweb/VERDICT-KW-1.md`): unordered pairs a rule CONSOLIDATES (c ≥ 3, three spaced occasions, a Poisson tail < 0.001; window d = 3 distinct previous words). KW-1 read it on the first 9,133 shelf words of alpha4-1: H (keeps everything) consolidated 560, S (budgeted, random growth, 10 seeds) 346.4, its enrichment of unheard speech 1.80 / 1.75, against a bar of 2 (K6 FAIL) and 0.95–0.96 on a shuffle.
- **An MDL rule** (new here): a pair is a frame iff it SHORTENS a two-part code (§1).

## 1 · The compressor (fixed before any run; every rule is graded by the SAME code)
- **Data:** moments (utterances) in seq order; a moment's words in the order heard. **Split:** the first 60 % of the moments are TRAIN, the last 40 % HELD-OUT (KW-1's fraction), by seq.
- **A frame** is an ORDERED pair `(a, b)` of words. **The parse:** inside a moment, left to right, `(a, b)` ∈ F at the next two words → one FRAME token, else one WORD token; the parse never crosses a moment (a frame lives inside an utterance, as SENTENCE's own domain). A single-word moment is a unigram token.
- **The token code:** the token's probability is its count in the TRAIN parse under F, add-one smoothed over the token inventory (words ∪ frames ∪ one UNK): `p(t) = (c_t + 1) / (N + K)`, `N` = train tokens, `K` = inventory size; a held-out word never seen in train is UNK.
- **The model cost** of F: `L(F) = |F| · 2 · log₂ V` bits (a frame is spelled by its two words), V the train vocabulary.
- **The gain:** `G(F) = bits(held-out | ∅) − bits(held-out | F) − L(F)` — how many bits F saves on text it never saw, net of its own description; `G(∅) = 0`; per held-out word `g = G / N_held`. A frame set that costs more than it saves has G < 0.

## 2 · The three rules (the candidates; each returns a set F of ordered pairs from the TRAIN part only)
- **(a) WEB** — `genome.web.Web`: rule **H** (once) and rule **S** with `B = 2V` (seeds 1–10, KW-1's), the train words fed in order; `consolidated()` returns unordered edges; each becomes the ONE ordered adjacent pair with the larger within-moment train count (ties: alphabetical); an edge with NO within-moment adjacent train occurrence is dropped and counted («no adjacent occurrence»).
- **(b) SENTENCE** — `genome.sentence.find_frames` over the train moments (a stand-in store exposing `get(i)` → `{"words_in_instant": …, "id": …}`, `n_written`, `cap`), `min_count` 5, `factor` 2.0, as the cells ran it.
- **(c) MDL-GREEDY** — candidates: every within-moment adjacent train bigram with count ≥ 2; greedily add the frame with the largest TRAIN gain `Δ = bits(train | F) − bits(train | F ∪ {f}) − 2 log₂ V` (the exact count-based change, the parse re-made after each addition, ties by the pair's name), until no candidate has Δ > 0.

## 3 · The streams, the nulls, the controls
- **ALPHA4** (the real stream): alpha4-1's ear rows, all five sources, seq order — 84,275 moments, 112,489 words. **SCENE** (the control with a known generator): brain-full's 10,000 scene moments.
- **THE NULL, a distribution of 100 draws (≥ 20, #244 (2)):** for a rule's set F of k frames, a DEALT set F′ of k pairs: each frame `(a, b)` is replaced by `(a′, b′)` with `a′` drawn uniformly from the words whose train count is in the same log₂ bin (width 0.5) as `a`'s, `b′` likewise, `(a′, b′) ∉ F`, `(a′, b′) ≠ (a, b)`. Same size, same frequency profile, same model cost; only the pairing is chance. The null statistic is `G(F′)`; q95 = its 95th percentile. Draws from `default_rng([20261103, rule_tag, seed])`.
- **PLANT (a positive control; every rule must FIND it):** a copy of the stream in which, after every 100th moment (ALPHA4) / every 10th (SCENE), a NEW moment `["zzq", "xxv"]` is inserted — two tokens in no vocabulary. Each rule must keep `("zzq", "xxv")`, and its `G` must be positive.
- **SHUFFLE (the instrument finds nothing in structure-free data, KW-1's K7):** train and held-out words each permuted among their own positions (the moments' sizes kept), seed 1.
- **THE CEILING (SCENE only):** `G_max` = the bits the unigram code spends on the held-out scene utterances minus the generator's own entropy (6.0774 bits × the held-out utterances); no rule's `G` can exceed it. The tighter ceiling for FRAMES: `G(F_oracle)`, F_oracle = the 24 allowed (colour, shape) pairs that appear adjacent in that order (the structure the 6 exclusions leave), read from the same compressor.

## 4 · Marks, bars, nulls
- **PLANT-FOUND (a bar, every rule, both streams):** WEB-H, SENTENCE, MDL-GREEDY keep the planted frame; WEB-S keeps it at ≥ 9 of 10 seeds; and each such set's `G` > 0.
- **SHUFFLE-QUIET (a bar, every rule, ALPHA4):** `G(F)` ≤ the null's q95, or F is empty (printed).
- **GAIN(rule, ALPHA4) (the bar the toy is for):** `G(F) > q95` of its dealt null AND `G(F) > 0`; WEB-S is judged by the number of its ten seeds that pass (printed; «passes» needs ≥ 8 of 10, exact sign p = 0.055).
- **GATE FIRST:** if the PLANT fails for a rule, that rule's GAIN is NOT GRADABLE (the instrument cannot find a frame it was handed); if the SHUFFLE is not quiet for a rule, its GAIN reads NOT GRADABLE (it finds frames in noise).
- **Readings (no bar):** per rule the size |F|, the share with a within-moment adjacent train occurrence, `G` in bits and per held-out word, the null's mean / sd / q95 and the rule's z; the rules' overlaps (Jaccard); on SCENE `G` beside `G_max` and `G(F_oracle)` and each rule's recall of F_oracle; **THE EYE'S READING (ALPHA4):** the cells' 40 distinct said frames, each with its says and its yes-count; for each rule, how many of the 40 it keeps, the pooled yes-rate of the says of the frames it KEEPS against those it does not (n printed), and the frames it keeps that the cells never said.
- **Predictions** (written before any run; the counts of §0 were read): **P-C1** [Likely] MDL-GREEDY's `G` is the largest (it optimises the TRAIN version of this very quantity). **P-C2** [Guessing] WEB and SENTENCE beat their dealt nulls on ALPHA4 (their pairs co-occur far above chance) but with `G` below MDL-GREEDY's, because they keep rarer pairs that cost more than they save. **P-C3** [Guessing] on SCENE no rule beats its null by much (the structure is the 6 exclusions): every `G` ≪ `G_max`. **P-C4** [Likely] the eye's reading splits the rules' frames: the game pairs («green box», «yellow key», «red box» …) are kept by MDL-GREEDY and are the ones the eye confirmed, while the function-word pairs it also keeps («of the», «to the») are not — compression and grounding point at different frames. **P-C5** [Guessing] SENTENCE keeps «the letters», «a big», «to the» (the most said, 0 confirmed) and a game pair or two.
- **Refutations:** «a rule's frames shorten unseen text because the pairing is real» is refuted for a rule if its `G(F)` is inside its dealt null; P-C4 is refuted if the rule's kept frames have the same yes-rate as its dropped ones (within the sampling of 40 frames); «compression finds what the eye confirms» is refuted if the kept frames' pooled yes-rate is below the 86 / 526 = 0.163 base rate.

## 5 · Where can the mechanism fire (the adversarial walk)
1. **Adjacent occurrences.** A frame compresses only where its two words stand adjacent INSIDE a moment of the held-out part; the ALPHA4 stream has 36,078 + 3,148 + … words in multi-word moments (shelf 799 × 2 + 592 × 3 + 8,176 × 4 = 36,078; game 3,148), and the 35,294 maker words and 37,546 single-word shelf moments can host none. Every kept frame's held-out adjacent count is printed; a frame with zero is a frame that cost bits and saved none.
2. **A bar above its ceiling?** GAIN's bar is a percentile of the dealt null, so it has no absolute ceiling; the SCENE ceiling shows that nothing there is worth more than `G(F_oracle)`; the PLANT is the arithmetic proof that the compressor CAN save bits on a frame it is given.
3. **Ablated systems on the marks' own items.** `∅` (G = 0) is the ablated compressor; the dealt null is the ablated PAIRING (same sizes, same frequencies, chance partners); the SHUFFLE is the ablated STREAM.
4. **A frame that spans a moment** (the last word of one chunk and the first of the next): not parsed, by §1, and the web may propose it (dropped, counted).
5. **MDL-GREEDY overfits TRAIN by construction**; the HELD-OUT part is where it is graded. The model cost L(F) is paid per frame once, against the held-out text only: a frame needs enough held-out occurrences to repay `2 log₂ V ≈ 23` bits.
6. **The eye's reading is 40 frames, and the cells' frames came from the cells' OWN rule on a stream of five sources**: a reading, never a bar; the n is printed.
7. **What a pass would NOT show.** That the organism should say frames by compression: the cells' mouth is judged by the EYE, and compression alone cannot know what the eye sees. The toy answers which selection rule gives pairs that compress unseen text beyond chance, and whether those are the pairs the eye later confirms.

## 8 · Open readings I chose, and where this differs from the sketch (for the architect to rule; each changeable BEFORE the first run, never after)
1. **The real stream is alpha4-1's, not brain-full's.** The sketch named brain-full's cut tape for «scene, maker, shelf, game ear rows» and alpha4-1's journal for the says and confirmations. brain-full's shelf is 884 words (562 moments) — too few for a held-out test — and its game 81 moments; alpha4-1 is the life that SAID the 526 frames, and its ear rows (112,489, five sources) are the stream those frames came from. brain-full's scene moments remain the SCENE control (the generator's entropy is known).
2. **Frames parse INSIDE a moment**, matching SENTENCE's own domain; the compressor never crosses a moment boundary.
3. **WEB-S's budget B = 2V** (KW-1's), the web read at its KW-1 defaults (d 3, k 3, α 0.001); the KW-2 options (`spend`, `beta`) are not used.
4. **The null's bin width 0.5 in log₂ count, 100 draws, q95**; the planted tokens `zzq` / `xxv`; the 60 / 40 split by seq.
5. **MDL-GREEDY's candidates** are bigrams with train count ≥ 2 (not SENTENCE's 5): the rule is allowed to be more permissive than the others, and the model cost is its only brake.
6. **No type-level frames** (slot patterns such as «size colour shape»): this toy asks about frames of specific words only. Frames WITH slots, the SCENE stream's real structure, are the next law if this one says word pairs are near their floor.
