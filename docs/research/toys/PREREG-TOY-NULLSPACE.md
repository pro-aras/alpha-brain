## 0 · What this is, and what was read BEFORE signing (disclosure)
Ahmad sent a «null space» explainer. The continual-learning papers behind it, **read here from their PDFs**:
- **OWM** (Zeng, Chen, Cui, Yu — arXiv 1810.01256; Nature Machine Intelligence 2019): «weights can only be modified in the direction orthogonal to the subspace spanned by all previously learned inputs»; `P = I − A (AᵀA + αI)⁻¹ Aᵀ`, A = all previously trained input vectors as columns; `ΔW = κ P ΔW_BP` (Methods eq. 1: the first task is trained UNPROJECTED, tasks j ≥ 2 by `P(j−1)`); P is built by the recursion `P(i,j) = P(i−1,j) − k x̄ᵀ P(i−1,j)`, `k = P x̄ / [α + x̄ᵀ P x̄]` over the batch-mean inputs of the finished task, `P(0) = I/β` (eq. 2); «the capacity of one layer can be measured by the rank of P»: it is consumed room by room, and «if the capacity limit … is approached: a larger α … or more layers». Its α and β values sit in the paper's Supplementary Table 7, which the text read does NOT contain: this law derives α from the data (§1) and does not cite theirs. Its experiments train a new class with positive samples only («no negative samples were used»).
- **Adam-NSCL** (Wang, Li, Sun, Xu — CVPR 2021): the update is projected into the approximate null space of the uncentred feature covariance of ALL previous tasks, `Δw = U₂U₂ᵀ g`, U₂ the singular vectors with the smallest singular values, selected as `λ ≤ a · λ_min` (a > 0, «controls the balance of stability and plasticity»; a = 50 in their ResNet example); task 1 unprojected.

**T-MEM-1 (the bench; `docs/brain/memory/VERDICT-TMEM1.md`, `TMEM1-REPORT.txt` in genome-wt-brain3; the schedule (a) SEQUENTIAL R1 × 50 blocks → R2 × 50 → R3 × 50 → N × 50, 10 scenes a block):** the plain A-RW learner answering from its weights forgets, on each room's own SHAPE words, BWT after R2 / R3 / N = **−0.158 / −0.241 / −0.734** (seed 20261301), **−0.137 / −0.267 / −0.774** (…02), **−0.144 / −0.324 / −0.710** (…03) — the brief's «24–32 %, 71–77 % after the noise room». Its GATE (a GEM/OWM-style projection against ≤ 8 anchors of each STRONG word, WITH uniform replay) read ≤ 0.4 % forgetting after R3 but its edge over random anchors was significant at 1 seed of 3 («any protection helps»). **This toy differs on three counts:** one GLOBAL projector over the whole input span of the earlier rooms (not anchors per word), NO replay (the BASE learner), and TWO nulls — a rotated projector of equal spectrum and a matched scalar step shrinkage — plus a ceiling for what weight protection could ever buy.
**The learner (read, `learners.py` `train_scene`):** every word ever heard has a logistic unit `σ(v·z + b)` on the standardised input `z = (x − μ)/σ` (running statistics, floored, clipped at ±8) and is updated at EVERY scene — a word NOT said gets `y = 0`, so every scene pushes every absent word's unit DOWN on the scene's hypotheses (dense negative pressure). The update `[ΔV ‖ Δb]` (words × 147) is a combination of the scene's inputs `[z ‖ 1]`; the hook receives it before it is applied.
**World counts read before signing** (`tools/toys/nullspace/spectrum.py` → `docs/research/toys/SPECTRUM-INPUTS.out`, all ten seeds; the rows a word unit receives, `[z ‖ 1]`, as the learner saw them): the inputs are HIGH-RANK. At relative eigenvalue thresholds τ = 0.1 / 0.01 / 0.001 / 0.0001 of the uncentred covariance's top eigenvalue, **R1 alone occupies 7 / 38 / 90 / 140 of 147 dimensions** (min–max over seeds 6–7 / 37–40 / 88–95 / 139–141), R1+R2 10 / 44 / 115 / **147**, R1+R2+R3 11 / 50 / 124 / **147** (147 = EMPTY null space, at every seed, at τ = 0.0001 after R2). The share of the NEXT room's input energy OUTSIDE the old span (what an update can still move without touching the old inputs), median over seeds: R2 **0.743 / 0.390 / 0.146 / 0.016**, R3 **0.637 / 0.221 / 0.034 / 0.000**, N **0.309 / 0.078 / 0.006 / 0.000**. So a projector at τ = 0.001 leaves R3 3 % and N 0.6 % of their energy to act with; at 0.0001 nothing — the stability–plasticity trade is real, and it is set by one resolution parameter, which is why this law registers THREE of them (§3) and reads the whole curve rather than a best cell. (τ = 0.0001 is not an arm: its hard null space is empty after R2, a count, not an experiment.)
**The two standing confounds I expect, named before any run (they are what the ceiling and the readings measure):** (i) the running standardiser moves as rooms change, so the OLD inputs' standardised values drift even when no weight moves — weight protection cannot reach that part; (ii) the bias is a weight on a constant input of huge energy, so protecting the span freezes the biases unless they are left out of the space (a reading arm).

## 1 · The question and the design
**H-null:** protecting the old inputs' span keeps old words BECAUSE it is the null space — an update confined to directions the old inputs do not excite — and not merely because the projector shrinks the step. **H-plasticity:** at some resolution it does so WITHOUT freezing the learning of the new room. The design makes each cell differ from PLAIN by ONE thing and gives every claim its null and its ceiling:
| arm | the one difference from PLAIN | what it answers |
|---|---|---|
| **PLAIN** | — (S1b-1's A-RW at lr 0.01, no replay, T-MEM-1's BASE) | the floor; identity-pinned to T-MEM-1's records |
| **OWM(τ)**, τ ∈ {0.1, 0.01, 0.001} | rooms ≥ 2: `[ΔV ‖ Δb] ← [ΔV ‖ Δb] · P_j`, `P_j = α_τ (α_τ I + G_j)⁻¹`, G_j = the Gram `MᵀM` of every `[z ‖ 1]` row of rooms 1..j as the learner saw them; `α_τ = τ · λ_max(G_1)` (derived once from room 1's own data, constant afterwards; room 1 unprojected) | the protection, soft: OWM's MAIN-TEXT definition (A = ALL previously trained input vectors, every hypothesis row), equal to its RLS recursion run over SINGLE ROWS; the spans counted in §0 are these all-rows spans |
| **NSCL(τ)**, same τ | rooms ≥ 2: `· U₂U₂ᵀ`, U₂ the eigenvectors of `C_j = G_j / n_j` with eigenvalue ≤ τ · λ_max(C_j) (a hard projector; Adam-NSCL's rule with its «a · λ_min» replaced by a RELATIVE threshold τ · λ_max: a rule on the SMALLEST eigenvalue of a 147-dimensional covariance hangs on one near-zero eigenvalue and cannot give three resolutions) | the same idea, hard |
| **ROT(τ, k)**, k = 1..10 | OWM(τ)'s projector built in coordinates rotated by a fixed random orthogonal Q (`P_rot = Q P Qᵀ`; Haar, from `substream(seed, "ns:rot:k")`): the SAME spectrum, a random eigenbasis | **the null of the brief**: equal rank, random directions — protection by chance alone |
| **RAND-RANK(τ, k)**, k = 1..10 | NSCL(τ)'s subspace replaced, at every boundary, by a random subspace of the SAME dimension (the first d_j columns of Q) | the same null for the hard form |
| **SHRINK(·)** (one per OWM(τ) and per NSCL(τ)) | the plain update multiplied by the scalar `ρ_n` = the arm's own realised ratio `‖[ΔV‖Δb]·P‖_F / ‖[ΔV‖Δb]‖_F` at the same scene n of the same seed | **the second null: the step shrunk as much, with no direction** |
| **STD-ONLY(j)**, j = 1, 2, 3 | no weight or bias update after room j (the standardiser, the ear and the stores keep running) | **the CEILING of any weight protection that leaves the old logits on the old inputs unchanged**: what forgetting is left when no weight moves. It is NOT «statistics drift only»: words first heard after room j sit at σ(0) = 0.5 on the old inputs and COMPETE in forced naming against the old words — and so they do for the IDEAL protector, whose new units' updates never touch the old span (a unit born at zero keeps outputting σ(0) there). Both effects are in the ceiling, by design |
| **FREEZE-OLD(j)**, j = 1, 2, 3 (reading) | only the units that EXISTED when room j ended stop updating; words first heard later learn plain | separates «old logits frozen» from «new words allowed to learn and to steal old inputs» |
| **LRSHRINK(s)**, s ∈ {½, ¼, ⅛} | lr × s throughout | the plasticity–stability FRONTIER of merely stepping smaller (a reading) |
| **OWM-NB(0.01)** (reading) | the projector built on the 146 inputs WITHOUT the constant; ΔV projected, Δb free | does freezing the biases matter? |
| **OWM-RESTD(0.01)** (reading) | P rebuilt at every block from the earlier rooms' stored RAW rows standardised by the CURRENT statistics (the protection in the coordinates the held items are read in) | how much does the standardiser leak? |
| **OWM-BM(0.01)** (reading) | OWM's METHODS recursion literally: P updated by eq. (2) over the BATCH-MEAN inputs `x̄` (a batch = one block of 10 scenes: the mean of its `[z ‖ 1]` rows), at room boundaries, with `α_BM = 0.01 · λ_max(G_1^BM)` (the Gram of room 1's block means); rank ≤ 50 a room — a LOOSER projector than the all-rows one | is the paper's batch-mean form as protective as the all-rows definition? |
| **OWM-OLDONLY(0.01)** (reading) | only the rows of units that existed when the room began are projected; words first heard in the room learn plain | separates «protection froze the old words» from «the new words, projected from birth with their bias nearly frozen, could not learn» |
| **ANTI-OWM(0.01)** (plant) | the update projected onto the span (`I − P`) instead of out of it | **an arm that must LOSE**: if it protects as well as OWM, the mark is blind to the projector |

**[arch] «The same τ» is the same resolution at room 1 ONLY.** OWM(τ) keeps `α_τ = τ · λ_max(G_1)` FIXED, as OWM's constant α; NSCL(τ) re-relativizes `τ · λ_max(C_j)` at every room. As rows accumulate OWM's effective resolution `α_τ / λ_max(G_j)` TIGHTENS by design (seed 20261301: α_0.1 = 3,543.7 against λ_max(G_3) = 36.93 × 2,391 ≈ 88,300, a ratio of 0.040 where room 1 gave 0.1). The two families are therefore matched only after room 1, and the report never reads them as matched afterwards: **it prints, per seed, per room boundary and per τ, SIDE BY SIDE, OWM's capacity `trace(I − P_j)` (the OWM paper's own measure: the soft rank removed) and NSCL's removed rank `147 − d_j` with its null dimension `d_j`** (`tools/toys/nullspace/spectrum.py` prints both; the lives' records carry them).

**Why every projector can be computed before the lives:** the running standardiser sees only the scenes, so the rows `[z ‖ 1]` — and with them every `G_j`, `P_j`, `α_τ`, U₂ and its dimension — are the SAME functions of (seed, τ) for every arm. They are computed once per seed in a pre-pass; a life is then a replay with a hook that multiplies by the right matrix. (SHRINK needs its parent arm's life first.)

## 3 · Bars, nulls, ceilings (decided at all 10 seeds)
Let `E` be OWM(τ) or NSCL(τ) (six arms). The barred BWTs are **j = 3 (after R3) and j = 4 (after N)**; j = 2 is printed.
- **GRADABILITY (the world's own counts, #228 (b)) — decided FIRST:** (c1) PLAIN forgets: `BWT_3(PLAIN) ≤ −0.15` and `BWT_4(PLAIN) ≤ −0.50` at ≥ 8 of 10 seeds (T-MEM-1: −0.24 … −0.32 and −0.71 … −0.77), else the toy reads NOT GRADABLE; (c2) there is something protectable: `Δ_3 = BWT_3(STD-ONLY(2)) − BWT_3(PLAIN)` and `Δ_4 = BWT_4(STD-ONLY(3)) − BWT_4(PLAIN)` must each exceed twice that seed's paired SE of the PLAIN-versus-STD difference, else B-PROTECT is NOT GRADABLE at that seed (the standardiser's drift would eat the whole bar); a verdict needs ≥ 8 gradable seeds. The `forgetting removed` share `(BWT(E) − BWT(PLAIN)) / (BWT(STD-ONLY) − BWT(PLAIN))` is printed for every arm (a reading; 1 = all that weights can give).
- **B-PROTECT(E):** `BWT_j(E) − BWT_j(PLAIN) ≥ 2 × the paired SE` for j = 3 AND j = 4, at ≥ 8 of 10 gradable seeds.
- **B-NULLSPACE(E):** at a seed, E's `BWT_3` and `BWT_4` each exceed at least 9 of the 10 draws of its null (ROT for OWM, RAND-RANK for NSCL; rank ≤ 2 of 11, H₀ probability ≤ 2/11 per bar); passes iff ≥ 8 of 10 seeds do (H₀ probability ≈ 4 × 10⁻⁵ per arm, ≈ 2 × 10⁻⁴ over the six arms — no further correction).
- **B-SHRINK(E):** `BWT_j(E) − BWT_j(SHRINK(E)) ≥ 2 × the paired SE` for j = 3 and 4 at ≥ 8 of 10 seeds. SHRINK is one deterministic arm, not a single-draw null: the ten seeds are its replicates.
- **B-PLASTIC(E):** `PLASTICITY_2(E) / PLASTICITY_2(PLAIN) ≥ 0.90` and `PLASTICITY_3(E) / PLASTICITY_3(PLAIN) ≥ 0.90` at ≥ 8 of 10 seeds (protection must not freeze learning; the 10 % is declared, not tuned).
- **Cost (a reading, not a bar, #243 (5)):** CPU of E's life ÷ PLAIN's, measured in one process back to back on one seed at low load; expected well under 1.5 (a 147 × 147 product per scene, one solve per room).
- **The frontier (reading):** per arm, mean over seeds of (PLASTICITY_2, PLASTICITY_3, BWT_3, BWT_4) — OWM, NSCL, ROT, RAND-RANK, SHRINK, LRSHRINK, STD-ONLY, ANTI-OWM — so that «protects by shrinking» and «protects by direction» are read off one table.
- **Planted controls / positive controls:** **PB1** ANTI-OWM must LOSE: `BWT_3(ANTI-OWM) < BWT_3(PLAIN)` at ≥ 8 of 10 seeds (else the mark is blind to the projector). **PB2** the gate toy and identities (§4 G3). **PB3** PLAIN reproduces T-MEM-1's BASE records (§4 G2). **PB4** every arm equals PLAIN through block 50 (room 1 is unprojected), byte for byte on `rates_rw[0..50]`.
- **Verdict classes (per arm; the headline is the best class any of the six reaches, the table prints all six):** (i) **NULL-SPACE PROTECTS** — passes B-PROTECT, B-NULLSPACE, B-SHRINK and B-PLASTIC; (ii) **PROTECTS BY SHRINKING** — passes B-PROTECT (and B-PLASTIC) and fails B-NULLSPACE or B-SHRINK; (iii) **PROTECTS BUT FREEZES** — passes B-PROTECT, B-NULLSPACE, B-SHRINK and fails B-PLASTIC; (iv) **NOTHING** — fails B-PROTECT; (v) **NOT GRADABLE** by (c1) or (c2).

P-B2 [Guessing] OWM(0.01) removes more than half of the forgetting that weights can give (share > 0.5) with PLASTICITY_2 ratio ≥ 0.9 and PLASTICITY_3 near 0.9 (R3 retains 22 % of its energy). ## 5 · Adversarial walk (where it can fire; ablated systems; every «first / by N / every»)
1. **Where it can fire.** The old span is not empty: at τ = 0.01 an update to R2, R3, N retains 39 %, 22 %, 8 % of its input energy (median over seeds); a protector therefore acts mostly on N (92 % of whose update is removed) — exactly the room that takes PLAIN from −0.267 after R3 to −0.774 after N (seed 20261302, T-MEM-1's report). At τ = 0.001 R3 retains 3 %: protection there may be indistinguishable from freezing; the report prints OWM's capacity `trace(I − P)` after each room (the OWM paper's own measure) beside it.
2. **Ablated systems on the marks' own items.** STD-ONLY(j) is the ablated protector (no weight moves): it scores the BWT mark with the weights taken out, and bounds what any weight protection can buy. ANTI-OWM is the sign-flipped protector. PLAIN is the unprotected system. The ROT / RAND-RANK / SHRINK arms are ablations of the DIRECTION and of the AMOUNT respectively.
3. **Every clause counted against the record.** «Room 1 unprojected» — blocks 1–50, 500 scenes, so every arm equals PLAIN there (PB4 checks it); the projector for room j + 1 uses rooms 1..j, so room 4 (N) is protected by three rooms' span; «at ≥ 8 of 10 seeds» is a sign count (exact one-sided p = 0.055); «rank ≤ 2 of 11» has H₀ probability 2/11 per seed; the pairs per task are 139 / 141 / 192.
4. **A bar above its ceiling?** B-PROTECT's gap is compared to what STD-ONLY allows (c2) before any bar is read; B-PLASTIC's ratio cannot exceed ≈ 1/PLASTICITY(PLAIN) — it asks for ≥ 0.9, below any ceiling; B-NULLSPACE asks for a rank, no absolute number.
5. **Confounds named.** (a) The as-seen projector acts in the coordinates the old rows had when learned, the held items are read in the CURRENT coordinates (the standardiser leak): OWM-RESTD and STD-ONLY measure it. (b) The OWM paper never trains negatives; this learner pushes every absent word down at every scene — a difference of the world, stated, not hidden. (c) OWM protects the old INPUTS; a held item protected only as far as it lies in the old span — the realised effect is what BWT measures. (d) The bias in the space freezes biases (OWM-NB reads it). (e) ROT has the same SPECTRUM, not the same removed ENERGY on real updates (a random subspace removes ≈ r/147 of a vector's energy, the true span far more): that is why SHRINK, whose scalar is OWM's own realised shrinkage, is the second null; the pair together separate direction from amount. (f) The ten seeds share the held items: the paired SE reflects held-item sampling only, the sign counts across seeds carry the training-draw variation. (g) The first learner's units were trained to a standardiser that kept moving for 2,000 scenes; no arm freezes it. (h) **New words under OWM:** a unit born in room j ≥ 2 starts at zero and has EVERY update, from its first, projected out of the old span; the constant-1 direction carries the largest energy of all, so its bias is nearly frozen from birth. That is OWM as the paper has it (all output units are columns of the projected matrix) and it is part of what B-PLASTIC measures — but a B-PLASTIC failure would read as «protection froze the old words» when it may be «the new words could not learn their base rate»: OWM-NB (bias outside the space) and OWM-OLDONLY (new units plain) are the readings that separate them, and the RESULT names which of the two a failure is before saying anything about plasticity. (i) **STD-ONLY's competitor** (see its row): the ceiling includes new words sitting at p = 0.5 on old inputs; FREEZE-OLD reads the other extreme (new words learn plain and may steal old inputs). (j) **All-rows versus batch-mean:** the spans counted in §0 and the arms OWM / NSCL are ALL-rows spans; a batch-mean span (OWM-BM) is smaller (rank ≤ 50 a room) and leaves more plasticity — a difference of the projector, not of the world.
6. **What a pass would NOT show.** That the organism should adopt null-space protection: its own `W` is a different learner, and T-MEM-1's answer for the organism's brain was an order-free STORE (BWT 0.000); this toy asks only whether the NULL SPACE is what protects a delta-rule map, and at what cost to learning.

## 7 · Open readings I chose (for the architect to rule; each changeable BEFORE the first life, never after)
