## The answer, in plain words
1. **The quantity, not the time scale.** Measure progress on **`C`** — the code length of the word the parent SAID, given its type and the scene — instead of **`L`**, the learner's own noisy-OR loss over the fixed 13 words, and the progress rule stops being a trap. **All four `C` cells pass BEAT and LEAVE** (words learned per scene A 0.595 / 0.646 / 0.671 / 0.684 against a uniform pick's 0.555 and a «never the noise room» pick's 0.713; share of policy picks in the noise room 0.106 / 0.051 / 0.001 / 0.000 against 0.248), over the ten seeds (at least 9 of 10 seeds positive for each) and, for the two ema cells, on the seven held-out seeds alone. **None of the four `L` cells and not Oudeyer's windowed learning progress passes BEAT.** Main effect of the quantity on A: **+0.2840** (95 % CI +0.2428 … +0.3190, positive at 10 of 10 seeds); of the time scale +0.0324 (+0.0119 … +0.0590, 9 of 10); of the scope −0.1485 (negative at 10 of 10).
2. **It is the credit's ADDRESS that matters, not merely leaving the noise room.** The law's stage-2 NULL (the same arm, its credit dealt to a different room) fails BEAT for all four arms (NULL A 0.390 / 0.367 / 0.475 / 0.486, D −0.165 / −0.188 / −0.080 / −0.069); every arm beats its own NULL at **10 of 10 seeds** (A +0.204 / +0.279 / +0.196 / +0.198). 3. **Scope is an INTERACTION, and the main effect hides a sign flip.** The paper's «the data both compressors are tested on = the history so far» is the TRAP on the learner's own loss (history − own on A **−0.3543** within `L`, SHARE_N +0.4447) and a small gain on the code length (**+0.0573** within `C`, SHARE_N −0.0778); interaction +0.4116 (CI +0.3288 … +0.4642). The `L-hist` cells fall into the noise room (SHARE_N 0.626 / 0.649), as predicted (P1 holds).
4. **The law's own explanation of the trap is refuted as worded.** §0 item 4 said the bias part (calibration of how often each word is said) is the majority of the drop a block in the noise room pays. **It is not: +0.0467 of +0.4658 (0.100)** in the noise room's own-window drop; the weight part is +0.4419. On the history-wide drop the bias part IS the majority (0.776) — but equally in the learnable rooms (0.806); what makes the noise room look better to the history-wide `L` is the TOTAL: +0.0590 a visit against +0.0492 (details below).
5. **The `C` cells use an ORACLE element (the world's word-type map).** The law asks for the precondition to be measured: the ear's own cover equalled the map at block 200 in **all 1,810 lives that carry ear rows**, first at block 3 – 69; so the types are RECOVERABLE by the ear. The arms were NOT re-run with the ear's cover instead of the map: transfer is a measured precondition, not a demonstrated result.

## Why the quantity matters: readings (no bar; the policy is taken out of the loop where it says so)
- **The drop's anatomy on RANDOM's lives (196 policy blocks; single-factor drops from the state before a block toward the state after it; the law's decision rule is the own-window share in the noise room).** Noise room, own window (n 9,706): total +0.4658 = bias +0.0467 + weight +0.4419 + statistics +0.0053 + interaction −0.0280; **share_bias 0.100 → §0 item 4's mechanism REFUTED in the law's words.** Learnable rooms, own window (n 29,494): +0.9236 = bias +0.0512 + weight +0.9328 + statistics +0.0216 + interaction −0.0820 (share_bias 0.055). **History-wide** (the paper's scope): noise room +0.0590 = bias +0.0458 + weight +0.0344 + statistics +0.0044 + interaction −0.0255 (share_bias 0.776); learnable rooms +0.0492 = bias +0.0397 + weight +0.0647 + statistics +0.0060 + interaction −0.0612 (0.806). So the history-wide `L` pays the noise room MORE per visit (+0.0590 against +0.0492) and the bias part is the same large share in both: **the paper's quantity read on the learner's own loss is dominated by calibration that every block supplies, and the noise room supplies slightly more of it** — which is the trap. On all blocks (1–200) and blocks ≥ 21 the same split reads share_bias 0.110 / 0.072 (own, noise room).
- **The per-visit signal** (RANDOM's 200 lives, own-window drop of the visited room, blocks ≥ 5): `L` — noise room mean +0.4658, sd 0.5049, cv 1.08; learnable rooms mean +0.9236, sd 1.9016, **cv 2.06**; mean ratio noise / learnable 0.504. `C` — noise room +0.0423, sd 0.0461, cv 1.09; learnable +0.0829, sd 0.0668, **cv 0.81**; ratio 0.510. - **The open-loop ranking probe** (on each of RANDOM's visit streams at every block ≥ 40 — 32,200 scored (life, block) pairs — how often «argmax of the room's own-window drop» would name the noise room; chance 0.25; the dealt null permutes the room labels, 0.252 – 0.262): `L` last-2 **0.272** (no separation from its null 0.252), `L` ema 0.075, `C` last-2 0.156, `C` ema **0.040**. ## Prediction P4 failed at ONE seed, and the report now says why
**P4 [Likely]: «no arm reaches R-ONLY's A at any seed».** `L-hist-ema` reaches it at **seed 20261305**: A **0.702** against R-ONLY's 0.700 (the ten records `runs/L-hist-ema/20261305/00.json … 09.json` read 0.648 – 0.744 each; a figure from the records, not printed by the report), SHARE_N **0.000** — while at the other nine seeds it reads 0.031 – 0.257 with SHARE_N 0.448 – 0.895. **The cause, from the report's first-policy-pick table:** the four round-robin blocks are the same in every replicate of a cell, so a cell's estimates at the first policy pick (block 5) are one value per seed. For `L-hist-*` the estimate for the noise room minus the best learnable room's is **+0.10 … +1.88 at eight seeds** (the arm enters the noise room at its first pick and stays: it is the highest history-wide reward) and **negative at two, −0.93 (seed 20261303) and −2.03 (seed 20261305)**. A greedy rule with one reading per room, whose estimate for a room changes only when the room is visited, falls the way that first reading points; at seed 05 the arm's `ema` never visits the noise room by policy (the 10 % ε-exploration visits are excluded from the share) and reads as a perfect escape. The `last2` cell at the same seed enters the noise room later (SHARE_N 0.429). **So the L-hist trap is bistable, decided by one early number — not a quirk of the instrument.** For the `C` cells the first reading puts the noise room BELOW the best learnable room at all ten seeds (−0.09 … −0.34), and the own-window `L` cells too (−1.64 … −7.03): the first reading alone does not decide the life there.
