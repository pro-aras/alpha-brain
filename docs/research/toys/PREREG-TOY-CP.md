## 1 · The question, the hypotheses, the design
**The brief's hypothesis:** the progress estimator measured the wrong quantity or at the wrong time scale. This law TESTS it as a 2 × 2 × 2 factorial in which every cell differs from its neighbours by ONE thing, with S1c's PROGRESS as one corner and its identity pinned (§6 G2):

| factor | low (S1c) | high | what it answers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **loss** (the compressor's code length) | `L`: S1c's noisy-OR cross-entropy over the fixed 13 words | `C`: the code length of the said word GIVEN its type and the scene (§3) | is the quantity dominated by calibration? |
| **scope** (the data both compressors are tested on) | **own**: the visited room's held window | **hist**: the history so far — every room's window weighted by the scenes experienced in it (A.5) | the paper's «same data, the history so far» |
| **time scale** (how the estimate of a room is formed) | **last2**: the mean of the room's last 2 rewards (S1c) | **ema**: an exponentially weighted mean over ALL the room's visits, a = 0.1 | A.7: detect and predict the decay; noise-robust |

plus **LP-OUDEYER** (the literature's windowed learning progress, a separate arm: §3), the controls (§3) and, conditionally, each passing arm's NULL (§6 stage 2). The factorial's PRIMARY reading is the three MAIN EFFECTS (loss, scope, time) on A and on SHARE_N, with the cells' BARS read one by one (§5).

## 3 · The arms (every one a picker over the same life)
Notation: `ℓ_k(b)` the loss of room k's window after block b (`ℓ_k(0)` before block 1; `ℓ` is `L` or `C`); `r_b` the room of block b; `n_j(b)` the scenes experienced in room j through block b (10 per visit, the round-robin included).
- **own reward** of block b: `ℓ_{r_b}(b−1) − ℓ_{r_b}(b)` — S1c's drop.
- **hist reward** of block b: `Σ_j n_j(b) [ℓ_j(b−1) − ℓ_j(b)] / Σ_j n_j(b)` — the improvement of the compressor on the history so far (each room's window standing for its share of it; weights normalised to 1 so the scale matches `own`; a room never visited has weight 0).
- **last2**: estimate(k) = the mean of the last 2 rewards credited to k (one until it has two). **ema**: estimate(k) ← (1 − a) estimate(k) + a · reward at each visit of k, the first reward initialising, a = **0.1** (half-life 6.6 visits; declared once, informed by phase 0's a = 0.1 vs 0.3, then NEVER tuned; a = 0.3 is a printed reading from phase 0 only).
- **The pick:** argmax of the estimate with probability 0.9, uniform over the four rooms with probability 0.1 (recorded «explore»), ties → least recently visited. After the round-robin every room has a reward, so no estimate is undefined (LP-OUDEYER excepted).
- **`C`, the second loss** (declared reading, §9 item 1): per window scene i, per type T ∈ {shape, colour, size} for which the parent said a word w* of that type, `C(i,T) = −log q_i(w*)`, `q_i(w) = P_i(w) / Σ_{w' ∈ T} P_i(w')`, `P_i(w) = 1 − Π_t (1 − p_w(t))` over the scene's hypotheses, `p_w(t) = σ(v_w·z_t + b_w)` for EVERY word of the fixed 13-word vocabulary (an unheard word at σ(0) = 0.5, S1c's rule), clipped to [1e-6, 1 − 1e-6]; `C_k` = the mean of `C(i,T)` over the window's (scene, type) pairs. It reads only what the parent SAID (never the truth); **[arch] `C` with the WORLD's type map (shape / colour / size, the vocabulary's) is an ORACLE element: the learner holds no types (R-TT).** Whether the ear could supply them is MEASURED, not assumed (T-MEM-1 found the ear's cover equals the map once the words are mature): at every block of every life the instrument records whether `Ear.cover` over the HEARD words equals the vocabulary map restricted to them, and the report prints per seed the first block at which all 13 words are heard AND the cover equals the map («never» if it never does), its last wrong block, and the share of lives where it is wrong at block 200. N's noise in the parent's own channel can corrupt the ear's exclusion graph; if the ear never recovers the types, a passing `C` cell CANNOT transfer to the organism and the RESULT says so. A window scene with no hypothesis contributes the uniform `log |T|` (S1c's empty-scene rule).
- **The eight cells:** `L-own-last2` (= S1c's PROGRESS), `L-own-ema`, `L-hist-last2`, `L-hist-ema`, `C-own-last2`, `C-own-ema`, `C-hist-last2`, `C-hist-ema`.
- **LP-OUDEYER:** per room, the list of the PREQUENTIAL losses (the loss `L` of each training scene BEFORE the update on it) in the order trained; `LP_k` = (the mean of the 20 scenes before the latest 20) − (the mean of the latest 20): window τ = 20 scenes, lag θ = 20 (adjacent windows), updated at each visit; defined once the room has ≥ 40 scenes (its 4th visit); an undefined room is picked first (least recently visited) — optimism, declared; then the same ε and ties. τ, θ declared, never tuned.
- **Controls:** **RANDOM** (S1c's band, 20 lives/seed); **ERROR** (S1c's rule: argmax of the room's loss now, ε 0.10; 10 lives/seed, replicate 0 = S1c's record); **R-ONLY** (uniform over R1, R2, R3 every policy block, never N; 20 lives/seed from `ronly:<seed>:<k>:pick:<block>`) — the arm that knows only «N is noise», the CEILING of escaping noise; **N-ONLY** (always N; 1 life/seed) — the floor, a plant; **ORACLE** (S1c's, one life per seed through `run_s1c.life`) — the reference, a greedy one-block lookahead, printed, never capped.

## 4 · The marks (per life; verdicts at block 200)
**A** (primary); **SHARE_N** (policy picks in N / 196, exploration apart; S1c's `run_marks`); **SHARE_N_late** (policy blocks 101–200 only, a reading: did it LEARN to leave); **learned per room** — the mean over the 200 boundaries of the pooled forced-naming accuracy of each learnable room's own shape words (R1 circle+square, R2 triangle+cross, R3 ring+star), their minimum (starvation), and the final accuracy; **M1** at block 200 (printed); **lock-in** (the longest run of one room in the policy blocks, printed). Every life carries `rates_curve` (13 words × 200 boundaries), the room-by-block loss tables (`L` and `C`, all four rooms), **[arch] N's history weight at every block** (`n_N(b) / Σ_j n_j(b)`: the feedback «the more N is visited, the more N weighs», printed per arm in 20-block bins and at block 200) and the per-block ear-cover check (§3). **[arch] The drop's anatomy** (RANDOM's 20 lives per seed only — they visit every room at random, an unbiased sample of N's and R1–R3's blocks): per block, for the visited room's window and for the history-wide sum, three SINGLE-factor drops from the state before the block toward the state after it — BIAS-only `L(before) − L(before with b after)`, WEIGHT-only `L(before) − L(before with V after)`, STATISTICS-only `L(before) − L(before with the standardiser after)` — plus the interaction `total − (bias + weight + statistics)`; the report prints their means over N's blocks against R1–R3's blocks and `share_bias = mean(bias-only) / mean(total)`. A word first heard in the block is taken at V = 0, b = 0 before it (the loss's own σ(0) prior).

## 5 · Bars, nulls, ceilings (decided at all 10 seeds)
**Statistic.** For arm E and seed s: `d_s(E) = mean over E's replicates of A − mean over RANDOM's 20 lives of A`; `D(E)` = the mean of `d_s` over the 10 seeds; likewise `e_s(E)`, `Dn(E)` for SHARE_N. **NULL, a distribution of 10,000 draws (#244 (2)):** at every seed RANDOM's 20 lives are split at random into a «fake arm» of 10 and a reference of the other 10; `D_null` = the mean over seeds of (mean fake − mean reference). It is conservative (its reference has 10 lives, the real comparison 20). `p(E)` = (1 + #{D_null ≥ D(E)}) / 10,001 (SHARE_N: lower tail).
- **[arch] The held-out reading (#158).** a = 0.1 was chosen over 0.3 on phase 0's reading of seeds 20261301–03 (S1c's ORACLE states), and P1 was read on them too, so those three seeds are CONTAMINATED for every ema cell (`L-own-ema`, `L-hist-ema`, `C-own-ema`, `C-hist-ema`) and for P1. **Every BEAT and LEAVE of an ema cell is therefore also computed on the seven seeds 20261304–10 ALONE:** `D'`, `p'` from a null of the same 10,000 draws restricted to those seven seeds, the sign count of 7 and the smallest passing `D'` at n = 7 printed beside them; the held-out reading PASSES iff `p' ≤ 0.0056` and `d_s > 0` (SHARE_N: `< 0`) at ≥ 6 of 7 seeds (exact one-sided sign p = 0.0625 for 6, 0.0078 for 7). **A BEAT or LEAVE that holds with seeds 01–03 and not on 04–10 is flagged CONTAMINATED: it is printed, never counted in a verdict class.** P1 is judged on 04–10 alone as well.
- **[arch] The replicate-SD flag.** The null is RANDOM's spread; a greedy arm that locks in can have a wider, even bimodal, spread across its replicates. The report prints, per arm and seed, the replicate SD of A and of SHARE_N beside RANDOM's (the SD over its 20 lives) and their ratio; **an arm whose mean ratio over seeds exceeds 2 is FLAGGED: its permutation p is anti-conservative and its BEAT / LEAVE rests on the sign count alone, at ≥ 9 of 10 seeds (exact one-sided p = 0.011)** (the 7-seed held-out reading, if it applies, at 7 of 7).
- **BEAT(E)** — E passes iff `p_A(E) ≤ 0.05 / 9 = 0.0056` (Bonferroni over the 8 cells and LP-OUDEYER; the smallest passing `D` is printed beside every `D`, #245 (5)) AND `d_s(E) > 0` at ≥ 8 of 10 seeds (one-sided exact sign p = 0.055 for 8, 0.011 for 9).
- **LEAVE(E)** — E passes iff `p_N(E) ≤ 0.0056` (lower tail) AND `e_s(E) < 0` at ≥ 8 of 10 seeds.
- **Escaped-noise share** (printed, no bar): `(A(E) − A(RANDOM)) / (A(R-ONLY) − A(RANDOM))` — the fraction of the available gain E captured.
- **Factorial main effects** (the answer to the brief): for each factor, the mean over the other factors' four cells of (high − low) on `A` and on `SHARE_N`, per seed, with the across-seed mean, its standard error, the sign count of 10 and a 95 % bootstrap interval over seeds (printed; no bar — the cells' BARS carry the verdict).
- **CEILING (world's own counts, #228 (b)):** `G = mean_s [Ā_s(R-ONLY) − Ā_s(RANDOM)]`, the most that escaping noise can buy. If `G < 3 ×` the smallest passing `D`, every BEAT is NOT GRADABLE (the instrument cannot tell an escape from nothing). Phase 0's «never N» share (0.294 of the one-step spread) is the expectation, not the bar.
- **Positive controls (the world can show it):** **PC1** `G ≥ 0.05` AND R-ONLY beats RANDOM at ≥ 8 of 10 seeds, else the toy reads NOT GRADABLE (S1c's own positive control, +0.155 to +0.220 with the ORACLE, was met at 3 of 3). **PC2** ERROR is trapped: SHARE_N(ERROR) > 0.5 at ≥ 9 of 10 seeds (the mark can fire: the TV exists). **PC3** N-ONLY: A below RANDOM's 2.5 % band edge at 10 of 10 seeds (the mark can fail).
- **Verdict classes:** (i) **FIXED** — some arm passes BEAT and LEAVE and its NULL (stage 2) does not pass BEAT; (ii) **ESCAPES BUT DOES NOT BEAT RANDOM** — an arm passes LEAVE and none passes BEAT (the 09-28 shape, repeated or repaired); (iii) **NOT FIXED** — no arm passes LEAVE. The cells' predicted directions (below) are printed against the outcome whatever the class.
- **Predictions** (written BEFORE any life; phase 0 was read): P1 [Likely, seen OFF-policy]: `L-hist-*` (the paper's quantity on the learner's own loss) sits in the trap — SHARE_N ≥ 0.5 at ≥ 8 of 10 seeds — i.e. the scope main effect on A is NEGATIVE for the `L` cells. P2 [Guessing]: the ema cells have lower SHARE_N than their last2 cells for `own`, and A stays inside RANDOM's band. P3 [Guessing]: `C-hist-*` is the only cell that leaves N AND beats RANDOM. P4 [Likely]: no arm reaches R-ONLY's A at any seed (escaped-noise share < 1). P5 [Guessing]: LP-OUDEYER ≈ `L-own-last2`.
- **Refutations:** H-quantity (loss or scope) is refuted if neither main effect on SHARE_N is negative at ≥ 8 of 10 seeds with its interval excluding 0 (and then the 1991 rule's failure is not about the quantity as varied here); H-time is refuted if the time main effect on SHARE_N AND on A are inside the null band; P1 is refuted if any `L-hist-*` cell has SHARE_N below RANDOM's band at ≥ 8 of 10 seeds (read on seeds 04–10 alone, ≥ 6 of 7). **[arch] The §0 item 4 mechanism is refuted if, over RANDOM's lives, the bias-only part is not the majority of the drop in N's blocks** (`share_bias ≤ 0.5`, own-window; the history-wide share printed beside), and the RESULT says so in those words.

## 7 · Adversarial walk (where can the mechanism fire; ablated systems; every «first / by N / every»)
1. **Where it can fire.** The world offers a choice: phase 0's one-step spread is +0.36 (best pick) against −0.65 (random), and N's true gain is negative in 78 % of the decisions (R1–R3: 32–45 %); S1c's own positive control (ORACLE − RANDOM) was +0.155 to +0.220. N's own-window drops average 0.29–0.54 and R1–R3's 0.63–1.02 per block (S1c report, interference tables), so one visit separates them by well under the per-visit spread: a recency-weighted estimate can only separate them over MANY visits (a room is visited ≈ 49 times in a life). That is the time axis's whole premise, and it is why a = 0.1 is slow.
2. **Ablated systems on the marks' own items.** R-ONLY (knows only «N is noise») and N-ONLY (only N) bracket A; the escaped-noise share is each arm's position in that bracket. The ERROR arm is the ablated progress arm for SHARE_N: a rule that follows the level of the loss, not its change.
3. **Every clause counted against the record.** «after the round-robin» = blocks 5–200 = 196 policy blocks (S1c's count); «the late share» = blocks 101–200 (100 policy blocks); «every room has a reward by block 4» holds (the round-robin visits each once; hist rewards from block 1); LP-OUDEYER is defined only from a room's 4th visit — so for the first ≈ 12 policy blocks its undefined rooms are picked first, a declared optimism, and its early SHARE_N includes them; «at ≥ 8 of 10 seeds» is a sign test (exact p 0.055), not a mean.
4. **The null's blindness.** RANDOM's band is exchangeable by construction; the stage-2 NULL keeps the real readings and removes only their address (a derangement), as S1c's did; it can lock onto one room (S1c's NULL had lock-ins of 36–95 blocks) — printed.
5. **A bar above its ceiling?** BEAT's threshold is a percentile of the null, so it has no absolute ceiling, but it can sit above what escaping noise buys: the CEILING clause (G ≥ 3 × the smallest passing D) checks that before any BEAT is read. SHARE_N's floor is 0 (policy picks only; exploration is apart), reachable by R-ONLY.
6. **Confounds named.** (a) An arm can lock into one learnable room and starve the others (A low although SHARE_N low): «learned per room» and its minimum read it. (b) `C` uses the vocabulary's type map: an assumption, not a truth about scenes. (c) The history weights count scenes experienced, not scenes held; a room visited more weighs more — the paper's «history». **[arch] The feedback is real and named: the more N is visited, the more N weighs in the history-wide reward** (N's weight per block is printed, §4), so a `hist` cell that drifts into N reinforces its own drift; the report reads it beside SHARE_N. (d) Replicates share the world seed's pools, so seed-level means are the replicate unit across worlds, replicate lives only the within-seed noise the null models. (e) The eight cells are tested against ONE null band: the Bonferroni 9 covers it; the main effects are readings.
