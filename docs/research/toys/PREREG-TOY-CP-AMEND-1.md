## 1 · G3 reworded: the unit gate tests the estimators' CODE on a world where the answer is known — it does not gate the L-hist cells' behaviour on the real learner
**The fault.** The base text's G3 says each of the eight cells and LP-OUDEYER «picks the learnable room after its first estimates on a two-room toy». Phase 0 and prediction P1 say the `L-hist-*` cells fall in the trap on the REAL learner, because a block in N lowers every window's loss (a calibration repair) — so a toy that shares the real world's interference would make G3 STOP on a RESULT, not on a bug (the gate would be #228's «a bar the mechanism cannot meet» in reverse).
**The rule (replaces G3's first sentence; the rest of G3 — the planted swaps, the zero history weight, `C` on an untrained learner = `log |T|`, `L-hist` with one visited room = `L-own`, the NULL never landing on its producer, each plant asserting it changed the bytes — stands):**
- **(a) arithmetic against hand-computed tables.** For a fixed hand-written loss table (six blocks, three rooms, the numbers in the test file, the history weights worked out on paper in the test's comments), each of the eight cells' estimate at each decision equals the hand-computed value to 1e-12; the LP-OUDEYER estimate on a hand-written prequential list equals the hand-computed difference of window means.
- **(b) an INTERFERENCE-FREE two-room toy.** A synthetic learner in which block k in room A multiplies A's excess loss by 0.8 and changes nothing else, and room B's window loss is a constant plus noise independent of every block (so the history-wide reward of a B block is ≈ 0 by construction and of an A block is positive): **all nine arms (the eight cells and LP-OUDEYER) must pick A in ≥ 95 % of the policy picks that are not ε-exploration, over 50 blocks, counting only the picks made while EVERY room's estimate is defined; the number of counted picks is printed beside the share.** This gates the code, not the world. *(edit: the architect's fix, 2026-10-03 11:26 +0400: LP-OUDEYER is undefined for a room until the room holds 40 scenes, and an undefined room is picked first, least recently visited — in the two-room toy that is blocks 3–8, three picks of B by optimism before both rooms' LP exist, which would cap LP's share near 55/58 = 0.948 < 0.95: the gate would STOP on the arm's own definition, not on a bug (#228 (a)). The eight cells are unaffected: every room has a reward after the round-robin, so no estimate is undefined at any policy pick — the arithmetic is checked, not assumed — and the rule counts all their policy picks; for LP-OUDEYER the counted picks start at block 9.)*
- **(c) the real learner's behaviour is READ, not gated.** On the real learner a 20-block smoke prints, for each `L-hist-*` cell, the room it picks at every block and its estimates (the smoke's record read whole, S1c's step 7); whether it picks N is prediction P1, decided at the unit's end on the lives, never at the gate.

## 2 · LP-OUDEYER's stage-2 NULL is defined
**The fault.** The credit derangement π credits «a reward» to π(room). LP-OUDEYER has no reward: its signal is a room's own prequential loss list. If it passed BEAT there would be no null to run.
**The rule.** LP-OUDEYER's NULL deals the ERROR STREAM across rooms: after a block in room k, the block's ten prequential losses are appended to the list of room π(k) (π the seed's derangement from `null:seed`, S1c's), and `LP_j` is computed from room j's list as for LP-OUDEYER (windows of 20 scenes, lag 20, defined from 40 scenes; an undefined room picked first, least recently visited); ε, ties and replicates are the arm's own. The pins: the credit never lands on its producer (RAISES); **with the identity credit it reproduces LP-OUDEYER's record exactly** (a planted identity that must read EQUAL, and a planted fixed point that must be CAUGHT). It runs only if LP-OUDEYER passes BEAT, 10 replicates × 10 seeds, and LP-OUDEYER is VOID if its NULL also passes BEAT — as for the cells.
