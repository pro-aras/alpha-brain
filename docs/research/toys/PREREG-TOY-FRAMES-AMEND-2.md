## 1 · The fault, and its diagnosis (RULES §2: the cause is the instrument)
AMENDMENT-1 §4 (g) fixed G4's small synthetic stream as «G6's sampler at 3,000 moments, replicate seed 1, a plant after every 10th: every rule must keep the plant (WEB-S at ≥ 9 of 10 seeds)». Run exactly so (`records/frames/ORIENT-g4_as_signed.out`; 3,000 moments, plants 180 train / 120 held): WEB-H, SENTENCE and MDL-GREEDY keep the planted frame and pass the amend1 gate, PLANT-BEATS-NULL-2 holds, the gate's parse-breaking control is CAUGHT; **WEB-S keeps the frame at 6 of its 10 seeds (it needs 9) → G4 FAILS.**
The cause, read from the web's own state per seed (`ORIENT-g4_diag.out`): the train vocabulary has **V = 15 words, so rule S's budget B = 2V is 30 edges for 105 possible pairs**; over the 3,892 train words the web REPLACES an edge 3,007 to 3,276 times (about 0.8 a word). The plant's edge enters with c = 1 and is then the least-c silent edge, so the next new edge evicts it: in seeds 1, 2, 3 and 7 it is never found in the web after any word was heard. In seeds 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10 the first plant arrived (word 22 or 44) before the budget had filled; its edge gathered evidence, consolidated (c 180, 180, 179, 180, 180, 180) and was never evicted. `refused` is 0 in every seed: the budget was NOT full of consolidated edges (that was my first hypothesis, refuted by the same diagnostic). So the defect is the STREAM's regime (a budget smaller than one plant period's churn), not the compressor, not `genome.web`, not the gate's arithmetic.
At the real regime the same mechanism has room: on an ALPHA4-regime synthetic stream (`synthetic.structure_free_alpha(20,000, 1)`, a plant after every 100th moment = ALPHA4's cadence; V 2,015, B 4,030, 16,150 train words, plants 120 train / 80 held; `ORIENT-g4_alpha_regime.out`) **all four rules keep the plant and pass the amend1 gate, WEB-S at 10 of 10 seeds (C 721.3 to 721.4 each), B = +721.86 against the architect's first-order expected value 608.45, PLANT-BEATS-NULL-2 holds** (63 CPU-s, peak RSS 134,084 kB).

## 2 · The rule (replaces AMENDMENT-1 §4 (g))
G4 runs on TWO small synthetic streams, each planted, split 60/40 after planting, each with 10 WEB-S seeds and 100 null draws:

WEB-S's gate on this stream is PRINTED, not required, with §1's diagnosis.
- **(B) the ALPHA4-regime stream:** `structure_free_alpha(20,000, 1)` (its train and held parts joined back into one stream), a plant after every 100th moment. **All four rules must pass the amend1 gate (WEB-S at ≥ 9 of 10 seeds, a seed counted only when it keeps the frame AND its C > 0), PLANT-BEATS-NULL-2 must hold, and the parse-breaking control must be CAUGHT on it.**
A miss on either stream STOPS the unit, as every G4 miss does.
