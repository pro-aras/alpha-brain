### 3.2 · The mechanism — one gene, one road
```
CHAT door chat/in chat/out; chain of frames + one pivot slot; links adjacent 5; pivot 10 distinct 20 instants; at most 6 words; a question never teaches
```
1. **The door (a sense, `creature/senses/chat.py`; the writer law holds: it never touches state/journal/memory).** It watches `$ROOT/chat/in/*.json` (bytewise order, a SEEN cursor written after the row is durable — #230) and publishes ONE tape row of a new sense, `chat`, per chat: its payload the canvas — the chat's PICTURE if it carries one (a held-out render, never text on it: the gaze fixates ink, so a question printed on the picture would be answered about the print), else the sentence rendered as a line (the shelf's renderer) — and its meta the sentence's words (cut by the door like the reader's, the ear's 8-letter law) and the id — never the family: the creature does not parse a question, and the door does not tell it what kind of question it is (the family lives only in the seat's truth file, for grading). A typed sentence thus enters HEARD and SHOWN on one moment — as ONE row of its own sense, never as `ear`/`eye` rows, so that no existing instrument (canvases, moments, births by source, the SURPRISE shares, the READ mark's shelf episodes) ever counts a chat as a world canvas and no chat word can reach the MEET road. Its truth (`chat/truth/<id>.json`: the family, the thing, the lure, the options, the bbox) is written by the SEAT beside it and is never published, never opened by the mind.
2. **The read-only law — «a question never teaches» (his explorer rule at the machinery level).** The mind routes every `chat` row to one branch, `_consume_chat`, and NEVER into the MEET road: no `pending` word, no birth, no Hebb/DELTA, no FAST episode, no ARRIVAL, no `org.t`, no `_canvas_index`, no THINK/SLEEP/EXAM/SIT boundary moved. It glances the canvas (the gaze, deterministic), reads the organism (`recall_many`, `edge_many`, the frames), composes, judges, writes `chat/out/<id>.json` and journals ONE `say` row of kind `chat` carrying the answer, its citations, and `hash_before == hash_after` (the organism's state hash around the chat — asserted). A yes-no question names a WRONG thing half the time; were a chat wired like the maker's instant, every false «is this a ring?» would teach a lie. **Nothing in a chat can change him; a grade on a chat moves nothing in this rung** (the seat writes `chat/graded/<id>.json` `{id, right, novel}` — the grade sense's own shape; the chat door publishes it as a `grade` row of kind `chat`; the mind counts it on STATUS apart from the ask door's grades, so the READINESS judge's (d) never reads a chat grade; whether a grade should move the mouth is the rung after, pre-registered then).
3. **The content (grounded, chosen FIRST; one road for every chat):** the canvas's five glances asked by the creature's own answer law (`recall_many` + the grade; ALPHA4's law, unchanged in this rung) — a free line's canvas is the line itself, so he answers what he can READ; **when the question names two or more THING-words** (a word whose own word cell has a MEET edge to a sight cell first born on a PICTURE source — maker, folder, game, hands — never on a rendered line), the content is chosen AMONG them by the larger summed edge from the canvas's lit cells (the Q4 road, `edge_many`; a tie abstains) — a fixed bias: an answer to a question that names things is one of them. No content → an abstention, rendered by the DOOR (never by the creature) as «(I do not know)» with its reason and its resemblance.
4. **The form (the composer, `genome/chain.py`, pure, deterministic; no rng, no probability over a vocabulary, no argmax over words — never next-token):** a LINK = a landed frame row (`find_frames`) whose pair was ALSO heard adjacent (w2 immediately after w1) in ≥ 5 instants; a PIVOT = a word heard in ≥ 20 multi-word instants and immediately followed by ≥ 10 distinct words (its right slot is open — Braine's pivot, Tomasello's item-based frame; §1(e) iii of MIND-THINKING-LANGUAGE). CANDIDATES = every simple path of links ending in a pivot, ≤ 5 words, containing ≥ 1 word of the question. CHOICE, in this order: the most question words · the pivot with the most distinct followers · the longest · the most adjacency evidence summed over its links · lexicographic. The answer = the chosen path + the content in the slot, or the content alone when no candidate exists. **The form is chosen before the content is placed — content-independent by construction** (a unit test: one question, two different contents → the same form).
5. **The judge (back-parse before saying — the songbird's template-then-match):** every link re-checked against the frames with its cited episodes; the pivot's follower count re-checked; the content re-checked as MEET-tied to THIS canvas's lit cells (W > 0 from a lit sight cell to the content's word cell). A failing chain is never said: the answer falls back to the content alone (counted `refused_form`); an ungrounded content is never said (counted `refused_content`).
6. **Every picture answer also POINTS:** `point` = the window of the first located glance whose own single-glance recall is the question's first thing-word (else the content); for a where-question the point is what is graded (none → an abstention on WHERE), the words are printed.
