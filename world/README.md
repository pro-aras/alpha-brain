# The folder door — your pictures for the genome (rung 1)

Put your pictures here, one folder per word:

    /data/dev/genome/world/<word>/picture1.jpg
    /data/dev/genome/world/<word>/picture2.png
    ...

Rules (each one comes from the code, not from taste):
- The folder name IS the word. Letters a–z only, at most 8 letters (the ear has 8 slots: `cat`, `cup`, `elephant` are fine; `motorbike` is refused).
- At least 5 pictures per word. At least 4 words (the questions need an "other word", and one word is withheld for «I do not know»).
- jpg, jpeg, png or webp, any size. Colour or grey. Photos or drawings.
- Different pictures, not a burst of near-copies (every third picture per word is held out for the questions; a copy would answer itself).

Nothing else is needed. When the folders are in, say so and rung 1 runs on them (docs/G1-PREREG.md).
